## Academic Uses

The portfolio is organized to support:

- peer-reviewed accounting and auditing research;
- reproducible empirical studies and replication packages;
- CAM/KAM, ICFR, IFRS, ESG and financial-reporting research;
- university teaching and student experimentation;
- open-source AI and multi-agent research;
- financial NLP, forecasting and synthetic-data methods;
- transparent benchmarking and scientific falsification;
- collaboration with universities, professional bodies, regulators and technology researchers.

## Governance Boundary

This portfolio is primarily an **education and non-commercial academic research environment**. Open and free components are preferred. Paid APIs or paid infrastructure are not the default execution path. AI output does not replace professional or scientific judgment; consequential conclusions remain subject to evidence review and human approval.
