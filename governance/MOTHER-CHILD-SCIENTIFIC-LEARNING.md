# Mother–Child Scientific Learning Constitution

This is the shared governance and learning layer for all current and future projects in this repository.

## 1. Mother–Child rule
The Mother repository is the canonical knowledge, governance, provenance, shared-evidence, and registry layer. Domain projects are Children. Originals are preserved. Children may receive relevant copies or references; validated learning may graduate back to the Mother through controlled review.

## 2. Human-development model
Every Child develops as an independent scientific learner:

**Birth → Kindergarten → School → University → PhD → Independent Scientist → Lifelong Learning**

Maturity is assessed by capability/skill, not by one global project score. A Child can be advanced in one skill and early-stage in another.

## 3. Required thinking framework
For material research and system decisions use:

**Problem → Alternatives → Evidence → CEO-100 Reference Class → Co-Scientist → AlphaEvolve → Science-Society Replication → Systems Thinking → Adversarial/Falsification Gate → Scenario/Experiment → Economic Value & Risk → Decision Card → Human Board Gate → Outcome → Learning Loop**

## 4. Scientific learning gate
No new information becomes trusted knowledge merely because an AI/model/source produced it.

**Learn → Evidence → Test → Challenge → Falsify → Human Approval → Graduate → Mother**

Failures and contradictory evidence are retained as learning evidence rather than silently removed.

## 5. Evidence and provenance
For material claims record, where applicable:
- source and organization
- date/version
- topic/domain
- license/rights status
- evidence or reproducible reference
- provenance/lineage
- validation status
- reviewer/challenge result
- human approval status

Primary standards, regulators, authoritative datasets, and peer-reviewed research outrank vendor marketing or unverified secondary summaries for scientific claims.

## 6. Graduation gates
- **Birth:** identity, mission, scope, safety/governance.
- **Kindergarten:** terminology and foundational concepts.
- **School:** standards, regulations, worked examples, basic datasets.
- **University:** scholarly literature, research design, statistics, ML/NLP where relevant.
- **PhD:** hypothesis generation, causal reasoning, replication, adversarial testing and falsification.
- **Independent Scientist:** agents/models, benchmarks, Digital Twins, reproducible experiments, independent review.
- **Lifelong Learning:** new evidence is continuously evaluated through the same controlled gates.

## 7. Skill Passport
Each Child should maintain a skill/evidence record with:
`skill | maturity_stage | evidence | test | falsification/challenge | status | last_reviewed | human_approval`.

## 8. Safety and development
- additive development by default; do not delete originals
- least-privilege access and separated secrets
- versioned, reproducible changes
- no proprietary redistribution without rights
- distinguish official/primary evidence from third-party material
- no causal/performance claim without suitable validation
- final consequential judgment remains human

## 10. Inheritance
All future projects inherit this constitution unless an explicit project-specific rule is stricter. Domain-specific rules may extend this framework but should not weaken evidence, falsification, provenance, security, or human-approval requirements.
