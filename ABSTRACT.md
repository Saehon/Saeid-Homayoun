# ECONOVA-S™: An FT50- and AJG/ABS 4*/4-Grounded Dual-Core Architecture for Governed AI Scientific Discovery in Economics and Finance — Architecture and Proof of Concept

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence can increasingly generate hypotheses, coordinate specialized agents, execute empirical workflows, and search large scientific design spaces, yet economic and financial research lacks a durable architecture that separates stable scientific meaning from rapidly changing AI technologies. **ECONOVA-S™** addresses this problem through a dual-core scientific economic intelligence architecture developed from a **systematic, relevance-gated synthesis of current FT50 and AJG/ABS 4*/4 research** across economics, finance, accounting, information systems, strategy, organization science, sustainability, behavioral economics, and AI-enabled decision making. The architecture contains exactly two permanent cores. The **Stable Economic Knowledge Core™** preserves economic theory, causal directed acyclic graphs, construct definitions and Variable DNA™, econometric identification rules, replication benchmarks, behavioral and experimental protocols, finance and asset-pricing logic, sustainability mechanisms, falsification standards, and private-versus-social welfare functions. The **Replaceable Technology Core™** contains interchangeable language models, multi-agent runtimes, Stata/EViews/R/Python adapters, retrieval systems, model routers, simulators, databases, and emerging AI protocols. A supporting **AI-to-AI Scientific Intelligence Fabric™** connects the two cores through structured hypothesis generation, empirical conversion, model search, independent review, replication, adversarial testing, Evidence Passport™ construction, and human approval; it is not a third core.

To establish computational feasibility, **POC-001** instantiates the architecture around the illustrative mechanism **Data Capability → Green Innovation → Firm Value → Social Data Value** using a deterministic synthetic panel of 20 firms observed from 2020–2025. The executable pipeline implements three explicit hypotheses, Co-Scientist-style hypothesis ranking, ERA-style empirical specifications, firm and year fixed-effect regressions with firm-clustered standard errors, an AlphaEvolve-inspired specification tournament based on a frozen out-of-sample fitness rule, a 2025 temporal holdout, placebo and expected-sign red-team checks, FT50-style empirical tables, an Evidence Passport™, and a Human Gate. The pipeline runs end-to-end and the automated validation suite passes. Crucially, POC-001 is **demonstration evidence only**: its estimates are associational, synthetic-data results are not interpreted as economic findings, and `discovery_claim_allowed` remains `false`. The contribution of ECONOVA-S is therefore not a claimed discovery from the POC, but a reproducible and governable architecture for moving from top-journal theory and causal design to AI-assisted empirical search, independent verification, welfare interpretation, and human scientific judgment. The next validation stage replaces the synthetic panel with real economic and financial data and requires replication, construct validation, credible identification, external validation, and out-of-sample evidence before any scientific-discovery claim.

## Research Foundation Statement

The FT50 and AJG/ABS 4*/4 foundation refers to a systematic synthesis of the **relevant current literature across these leading journal sets**. It does not claim that every article ever published in every FT50 or AJG/ABS 4*/4 journal has been individually reviewed.
