# NAAIL OpenLab™: Evidence-Governed Multi-Agent AI for Accounting, Auditing, Finance, Sustainability, and Scientific Discovery

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is rapidly changing how accounting, auditing, finance, sustainability, and business research are performed, but the use of increasingly autonomous AI agents creates a fundamental governance problem: systems may generate analyses, hypotheses, models, and professional recommendations faster than their evidence, reproducibility, provenance, and human accountability can be verified. **NAAIL OpenLab™** addresses this problem as an evidence-governed, multi-agent research and education platform designed to connect domain knowledge, computational methods, scientific discovery, and human professional judgment within a reproducible architecture.

The platform is organized around a **Two-Core Constitution**. The **Stable Knowledge Core™** preserves domain meaning, standards, theory, empirical constructs, causal assumptions, accounting and auditing logic, economic and financial models, sustainability frameworks, research protocols, and professional constraints. The **Technology Core™** contains replaceable AI models, agent frameworks, retrieval systems, knowledge graphs, Digital Twins, statistical software, model-routing infrastructure, and emerging scientific-computing tools. Specialized modules—including **POMELO™, KIWI™, VERA™, ECONOVA-S™, IFRS, PCAOB, ICFR/TimesFM, ESG, forensic, research, audit, and student agents**—operate within this architecture rather than as independent competing platforms.

NAAIL uses an evidence-governed scientific workflow in which AI-generated outputs must move through **Evidence Passports, hypothesis generation, empirical testing, falsification, adversarial review, independent replication, reproducibility checks, and a Human Gate** before they can be promoted as research or professional conclusions. The architecture supports Co-Scientist-style hypothesis generation and critique, AlphaEvolve-style search across candidate models and specifications, multi-agent scientific challenge and replication, causal and econometric analysis, temporal and out-of-sample validation, accounting and audit evidence tracing, and controlled integration of external open-source frameworks and public datasets. The repository also includes executable prototypes, continuous-integration workflows, synthetic and public-data benchmarks, audit and finance agents, containerized demonstrations, and reproducibility documentation.

The central contribution of NAAIL OpenLab is therefore not autonomous replacement of researchers, accountants, auditors, or decision makers. It is a **governed infrastructure for augmenting human scientific and professional judgment while preserving evidence quality, provenance, uncertainty, reproducibility, falsifiability, and accountability**. Prototype outputs are treated as research or educational evidence unless independently validated for a defined professional use. Scientific-discovery, assurance, audit, regulatory, or real-company conclusions require appropriate empirical evidence and explicit human review.

## Alignment with Big Four and Enterprise AI Platforms

NAAIL OpenLab is directionally aligned with the transformation now visible across the Big Four, while remaining an **independent academic and research platform rather than a replica, affiliate, or endorsed implementation of any firm's proprietary system**. PwC's next-generation assurance architecture centers on **Maestro**, an AI-native assurance platform, together with agentic audit workflows, continuous risk visibility, and human-led judgment; PwC is also working with OpenAI on an AI-native finance function covering planning, forecasting, reporting, procurement, payments, treasury, tax, and accounting close. Deloitte has embedded a connected network of AI agents into **Deloitte Omnia** for Audit & Assurance and has introduced **ControlCatalyst.AI** for internal audit, SOX, risk, controls, and compliance. EY has embedded enterprise-scale multi-agent capabilities into **EY Canvas**, integrating agentic AI throughout the audit while preserving human judgment, professional skepticism, and accountability. KPMG uses **KPMG Workbench** as a foundational platform for coordinating AI agents across service delivery and continues to integrate AI capabilities into **KPMG Clara**, its global smart audit platform; KPMG also has a strategic alliance with OpenAI and is identified by OpenAI as an Elite Partner.

These industry developments closely correspond to several NAAIL design principles: **multi-agent orchestration rather than isolated chatbots; end-to-end workflow integration; continuous and earlier risk identification; evidence-linked controls; human-led approval; governed deployment; and the separation of professional knowledge from replaceable AI technology**. NAAIL extends these ideas into an academic and open research setting by adding explicit reproducibility, falsification, adversarial testing, Evidence Passports, independent replication, model/provider comparison, scientific-discovery workflows, student laboratories, and Human Gates. The objective is not to compete with proprietary Big Four platforms, but to provide a transparent research environment in which comparable agentic accounting, auditing, finance, ESG, internal-control, and assurance architectures can be studied, tested, challenged, and taught.

NAAIL is also aligned with the broader **OpenAI enterprise ecosystem**. OpenAI's current Partner Network includes **PwC, KPMG, and Ernst & Young**, and OpenAI's collaboration with PwC illustrates a move from general-purpose generative AI toward governed agents embedded directly in finance operations. NAAIL therefore treats OpenAI models, ChatGPT/enterprise capabilities, Codex, and future agent technologies as **replaceable components inside the Technology Core**, not as the permanent scientific or professional authority of the system. The same architecture can support Microsoft, Google, Anthropic, open-source, and local models under common evidence, evaluation, security, and Human Gate requirements. This provider-neutral structure is intended to make NAAIL useful for comparative research on how different frontier AI ecosystems can support accounting and assurance without allowing any model provider to replace professional standards or human accountability.

## Research and Professional Scope

NAAIL OpenLab supports research and education across accounting, auditing and assurance, corporate reporting, internal control, finance, economics, management accounting, sustainability and ESG, forensic accounting, corporate governance, business analytics, and AI-enabled scientific discovery. The platform is provider-neutral and may use commercial, open-source, or local AI models through governed adapters.

## Evidence Boundary

Repository implementation, successful software execution, or passing automated tests demonstrates technical functionality only. It does **not** by itself establish empirical validity, audit assurance, regulatory approval, scientific discovery, commercial readiness, endorsement by a Big Four firm or AI provider, or superiority over alternative systems. Material research and professional claims require preserved evidence, reproducible evaluation, independent challenge where appropriate, and human approval.
