# NAAIL OpenLab™: Evidence-Governed Multi-Agent AI for Accounting, Auditing, Finance, Sustainability, and Scientific Discovery

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is rapidly changing how accounting, auditing, finance, sustainability, and business research are performed, but the use of increasingly autonomous AI agents creates a fundamental governance problem: systems may generate analyses, hypotheses, models, and professional recommendations faster than their evidence, reproducibility, provenance, and human accountability can be verified. **NAAIL OpenLab™** addresses this problem as an evidence-governed, multi-agent research and education platform designed to connect domain knowledge, computational methods, scientific discovery, and human professional judgment within a reproducible architecture.

The platform is organized around a **Two-Core Constitution**. The **Stable Knowledge Core™** preserves domain meaning, standards, theory, empirical constructs, causal assumptions, accounting and auditing logic, economic and financial models, sustainability frameworks, research protocols, and professional constraints. The **Technology Core™** contains replaceable AI models, agent frameworks, retrieval systems, knowledge graphs, Digital Twins, statistical software, model-routing infrastructure, and emerging scientific-computing tools. Specialized modules—including **POMELO™, KIWI™, VERA™, ECONOVA-S™, IFRS, PCAOB, ICFR/TimesFM, ESG, forensic, research, audit, and student agents**—operate within this architecture rather than as independent competing platforms.

NAAIL uses an evidence-governed scientific workflow in which AI-generated outputs must move through **Evidence Passports, hypothesis generation, empirical testing, falsification, adversarial review, independent replication, reproducibility checks, and a Human Gate** before they can be promoted as research or professional conclusions. The architecture supports Co-Scientist-style hypothesis generation and critique, AlphaEvolve-style search across candidate models and specifications, multi-agent scientific challenge and replication, causal and econometric analysis, temporal and out-of-sample validation, accounting and audit evidence tracing, and controlled integration of external open-source frameworks and public datasets. The repository also includes executable prototypes, continuous-integration workflows, synthetic and public-data benchmarks, audit and finance agents, containerized demonstrations, and reproducibility documentation.

The central contribution of NAAIL OpenLab is therefore not autonomous replacement of researchers, accountants, auditors, or decision makers. It is a **governed infrastructure for augmenting human scientific and professional judgment while preserving evidence quality, provenance, uncertainty, reproducibility, falsifiability, and accountability**. Prototype outputs are treated as research or educational evidence unless independently validated for a defined professional use. Scientific-discovery, assurance, audit, regulatory, or real-company conclusions require appropriate empirical evidence and explicit human review.

## Research and Professional Scope

NAAIL OpenLab supports research and education across accounting, auditing and assurance, corporate reporting, internal control, finance, economics, management accounting, sustainability and ESG, forensic accounting, corporate governance, business analytics, and AI-enabled scientific discovery. The platform is provider-neutral and may use commercial, open-source, or local AI models through governed adapters.

## Evidence Boundary

Repository implementation, successful software execution, or passing automated tests demonstrates technical functionality only. It does **not** by itself establish empirical validity, audit assurance, regulatory approval, scientific discovery, commercial readiness, or superiority over alternative systems. Material research and professional claims require preserved evidence, reproducible evaluation, independent challenge where appropriate, and human approval.
