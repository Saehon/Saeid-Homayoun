# ECONOVA-S™: An FT50- and AJG/ABS 4*/4-Grounded Dual-Core Architecture for Autonomous Scientific Discovery in AI, Data Economy, Finance, and Sustainable Welfare

## Abstract

Artificial intelligence is increasingly capable of generating hypotheses, coordinating specialized agents, executing analytical workflows, and searching large scientific design spaces, yet economic and financial research still lacks a durable architecture that separates stable scientific knowledge from rapidly changing AI technologies. **ECONOVA-S™** addresses this gap through a dual-core scientific economic intelligence architecture developed from a **systematic, relevance-gated synthesis of current FT50 and AJG/ABS 4*/4 research** across economics, finance, accounting, information systems, strategy, organization science, operations, sustainability, behavioral economics, and AI-enabled decision making. The architecture contains exactly two permanent cores. The **Stable Economic Knowledge Core™** preserves economic theory, finance and asset-pricing logic, data-economy mechanisms, behavioral and experimental protocols, sustainability and climate-finance mechanisms, causal directed acyclic graphs, Variable DNA™ specifications, econometric identification rules, replication benchmarks, falsification standards, and private-versus-social welfare functions. Its evidence layer is living rather than static: newly relevant FT50 and AJG/ABS 4*/4 research is continuously incorporated through literature validation and benchmark updates without changing the core scientific schema. The **Replaceable Technology Core™** contains interchangeable large language models, multi-agent runtimes, Stata/EViews/R/Python adapters, Qualtrics, GraphRAG and vector retrieval systems, model routers, simulators, databases, and emerging agent protocols, allowing technological replacement without silently altering scientific meaning.

Between the two cores, an **AI-to-AI Scientific Intelligence Fabric™** coordinates a Mirendil-inspired autonomous R&D loop, Google Co-Scientist-style hypothesis generation and critique, ERA-style conversion of hypotheses into reproducible empirical tests, AlphaEvolve-inspired evolutionary search over models, measures, algorithms, and specifications, Computational Discovery, and AlphaFold-inspired latent-structure reasoning. ECONOVA-S requires systems mapping and causal-DAG governance before estimation, replication of established findings before extension, real-data provenance, construct validation, chronology-safe and out-of-sample evaluation where appropriate, independent AI-to-AI review, adversarial red-teaming, robustness and falsification, reproducibility, economic-significance assessment, and welfare interpretation. No result is treated as a scientific discovery solely because it is novel, statistically significant, or predictively accurate; discovery claims require a complete Chain-of-Evidence™, Evidence Passport™, and explicit human approval. ECONOVA-S therefore positions frontier AI as a governed scientific search, experimentation, and verification system rather than an autonomous scientific authority. Its central research question is: **When does data become information, and when does information become economic and social value?**

## Research Foundation Statement

The FT50 and AJG/ABS 4*/4 foundation refers to a systematic synthesis of the **relevant current literature across these leading journal sets**. It does not claim that every article ever published in every FT50 or AJG/ABS 4*/4 journal has been individually reviewed.
