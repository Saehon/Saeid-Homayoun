# ECONOVA-S™ Roadmap

This roadmap separates scientific architecture maturity from software-release maturity.

## Architecture specification

**Canonical architecture:** V2.5

The architecture remains governed by exactly two permanent cores:
1. Stable Economic Knowledge Core™
2. Replaceable Technology Core™

## Software and research roadmap

### v1.0 — Governed Scientific Economic Intelligence Platform

## Non-goals

ECONOVA-S does not treat novelty, statistical significance, predictive accuracy or LLM confidence as sufficient evidence of scientific discovery.

## Governing rule

**AI explores. Economics constrains. Evidence verifies. Humans approve.**
