02 · Verify

A feed for professionals—not an attention trap

The interface adopts fast discovery, relevance and interaction, but never ranks research by exaggerated claims or invented results.

1. Explore in one click

Role-based ordering and guided walkthroughs make research accessible without installing an AI engine.

2. Inspect the evidence

Every experience links to its source repository or an intentionally public product overview. Synthetic examples are labelled.

3. Keep human judgment

A demonstration is not empirical validation, a regulatory conclusion, or professional advice. Human review remains required.

Research maturity notice. This page is an interactive discovery prototype. It does not invoke proprietary POMELO, audit financial statements, query private Drive files or produce live AI-generated professional conclusions. Real-world decisions require verified data, authority checks, and human approval.
