# CAM/KAM Benchmark

Goal: evaluate whether a model can identify, classify and longitudinally track Critical/Key Audit Matters from authorized audit-report evidence.

Tasks: extraction; topic classification; NEW_TOPIC detection; ENTRY/EXIT/PERSISTENCE; evidence-grounding; optional next-period discovery research.

Minimum reporting: sample definition, temporal split, precision/recall/F1, PR-AUC for rare-event tasks, evidence coverage, false-positive/negative analysis, model/prompt/version, and human adjudication protocol.

Do not treat model output as an audit opinion.
