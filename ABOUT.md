# About ECONOVA-S™

ECONOVA-S™ is a dual-core scientific economic intelligence platform for AI, data economy, finance, and sustainable welfare research.

Integrates Co-Scientist-style reasoning, Computational Discovery, AlphaEvolve-inspired model evolution, evidence-grounded RAG, econometrics, replication, and scientific red-team validation.

Designed for rigorous, reproducible research with human scientific governance; commercial use requires prior written permission.
