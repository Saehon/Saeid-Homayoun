# ArXiv and benchmark references

The following references were checked on 24 September 2026. Links point to the official arXiv record or the authors' official code/data release. They are cited as research inputs, not as evidence that the present package has reproduced their results.

| *ESG-Bench: Benchmarking Long-Context ESG Reports for Hallucination Mitigation* | Human-annotated ESG report QA and hallucination/verifiability testing. | [arXiv:2603.13154](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13154) |
