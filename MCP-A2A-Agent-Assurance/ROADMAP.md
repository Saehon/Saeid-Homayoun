## Product path

**Research reputation:** benchmark, theory, transparent failures, reproducibility, peer-reviewed evidence.

**Commercial path:** enterprise subscriptions, assurance services, controlled integrations, certification/benchmarking, agentic transaction assurance, and professional training.

Commercial claims should follow demonstrated reliability; the research benchmark is part of the product moat, not marketing decoration.
