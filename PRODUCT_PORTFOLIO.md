## Portfolio presentation rule

Public profile and job/research applications should lead with the original-product repositories. Forks and supporting repositories should be described as **upstream references, integrations, benchmarks, replications, or experiments**, not as products developed by NAAIL.

## Professional repository standard

All original products should follow [`NAAIL_PRODUCT_STANDARD.md`](./NAAIL_PRODUCT_STANDARD.md), including:

- clear maturity status;
- architecture and quick-start documentation;
- evaluation and reproducibility requirements;
- citation metadata;
- security and contribution policies;
- roadmap and changelog where appropriate;
- data/source provenance;
- third-party rights and independence statements;
- explicit human-review gates for high-stakes professional conclusions.
