AuditBERT — US CAM

Industry-aware U.S. CAM topic classification, with source-verification and uncertainty.

← All four projectsVerified GitHub source ↗Google Scholar ↗Kaggle ↗

Research method

TF–IDF/logistic-regression reproducible baseline; FinBERT is a separate prospective benchmark.

Verification gate

Validate CAM source, audit-report year, licensing, firm-level split, topic taxonomy and calibration.

Scientific boundary

Do not interpret CAM presence or absence as an audit-quality judgment.

Publishing checklist

  1. Freeze construct definitions and documented licenses.
  2. Prepare non-leaking and time-aware train/validation/test sets.
  3. Benchmark an interpretable baseline against eligible ML models.
  4. Publish reproducible scripts and legally shareable derived outputs.
  5. Document human audit, uncertainty and null/adverse findings.
