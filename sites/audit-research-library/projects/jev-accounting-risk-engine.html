Research method

Point-in-time accounting features with logistic baseline and selective-prediction/reliability evaluation.

Verification gate

Provenance, information availability dates, target independence, no look-ahead leakage, group splits.

Scientific boundary

The linked Hugging Face dataset is synthetic and not proof of real-world performance.

Functional synthetic pilot · 17 October 2026

Risk feature pipeline executed locally

A 100-row simulated 10-firm, 5-year, two-account-domain prototype successfully exercised the eight-feature risk engine, chronological train/validation/test partitioning and a regularized logistic baseline. Eleven local software tests passed, including future information leakage and unknown ICFR handling.

Scientific boundary: these are artificial observations. No real SEC CompanyFacts retrieval, Jev model, TimesFM model, CAM effectiveness estimate, or FT50 empirical claim is established. The Hugging Face-compatible CSV/card bundle is prepared for owner review, not uploaded.
