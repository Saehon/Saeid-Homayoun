## Academic research focus
I research how accounting information systems, financial reporting, auditing, and AI interact. My research portfolio covers critical/key audit matters (US CAM, UK/EU KAM), internal control over financial reporting (ICFR), SEC/XBRL analytics, audit risk, AI accountability, sustainability and data-driven management accounting.

**Research principle:** AI is an auditable measurement and decision-support infrastructure; valid empirical claims require appropriate accounting theory, reliable data, chronology, independent replication, and human professional judgment.
