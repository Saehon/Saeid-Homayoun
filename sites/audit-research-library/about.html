Dr Saeid Homayoun

Accounting, Auditing & Information Systems Scholar
Senior Lecturer in Accounting & Sustainability, University of Gävle, Sweden · PhD in Accounting & Control

I study how accounting information systems, financial reporting, audit assurance and artificial intelligence interact. My research connects accounting theory and audit judgment with financial NLP, SEC/XBRL data, machine learning, evidence traceability and scientific replication.
