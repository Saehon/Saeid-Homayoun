# FRANKENSTEIN Governance

FRANKENSTEIN inherits NAAIL OpenLab governance and adds programme-specific boundaries.

## Required rules

- preserve the two-core constitution;
- distinguish evidence, inference, simulation and human judgment;
- preserve finding IDs and source references for material claims;
- expose uncertainty, contradiction and missing evidence;
- require human approval for consequential professional conclusions;
- never treat agent agreement as authority;
- never commit secrets, client data, restricted standards text or proprietary audit methodology;
- use synthetic/public data for public demonstrations unless rights are explicitly documented;
- calibrate thresholds to the entity and engagement before real-world use;
- record model/provider/version when generative systems are used.

## Human-only decisions in this prototype

- audit opinion;
- fraud conclusion or allegation;
- material-weakness conclusion;
- regulatory/IFRS compliance conclusion;
- accounting-entry authorization;
- control modification;
- disciplinary or sanctions decisions.
