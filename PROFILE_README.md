# Saeid Homayoun

### Accounting · Auditing · Sustainability · Evidence-Governed AI

I am an accounting and auditing researcher at the University of Gävle, Sweden. My work connects accounting, assurance, sustainability reporting, forensic accounting, empirical research, and evidence-governed artificial intelligence.

I design research systems that combine authoritative professional knowledge, reproducible data analysis, knowledge graphs, retrieval-augmented generation, multi-agent reasoning, digital twins, adversarial evaluation, and explicit human approval.

> **Evidence first. Reproducibility by design. Human judgment remains accountable.**

## Research Areas

## Research Principles

My research emphasizes:

- evidence traceability and provenance;
- reproducibility and falsification;
- professional and scientific accountability;
- explicit separation of evidence from inference;
- adversarial review and independent validation;
- standards-aware reasoning;
- responsible AI governance;
- human approval before high-stakes professional or scientific claims.

## Collaboration

I welcome research collaboration in accounting, auditing, forensic accounting, sustainability assurance, financial-text analysis, empirical AI, and responsible agentic systems.

I am particularly interested in collaborations involving **AI for scientific discovery, audit intelligence, LLM-based empirical measurement, multi-agent research systems, digital twins, causal and computational discovery, and verifiable AI**.
