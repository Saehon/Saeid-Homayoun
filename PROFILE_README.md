# Saeid Homayoun

### Accounting, Auditing, Sustainability and Evidence-Governed AI Research

I am a **Senior Lecturer and researcher at the University of Gävle, Sweden**, specializing in accounting, auditing, sustainability reporting and assurance, forensic accounting, empirical research, and evidence-governed artificial intelligence.

My research develops accountable AI systems that connect professional accounting knowledge, standards and regulation, empirical evidence, knowledge graphs, retrieval-augmented generation, multi-agent reasoning, digital twins, reproducible analytics, and explicit human oversight.

> **Evidence first. Reproducibility by design. Human judgment remains accountable.**

## Research Principles

My research emphasizes:

- evidence traceability and provenance;
- reproducibility and falsification;
- professional and scientific accountability;
- explicit separation of evidence from inference;
- adversarial review and independent validation;
- standards-aware reasoning;
- responsible AI governance; and
- human approval before high-stakes professional or scientific claims.

## Collaboration

I welcome academic and research collaboration in accounting, auditing, forensic accounting, sustainability assurance, financial-text analysis, empirical AI, and responsible agentic systems.

I am particularly interested in **AI for scientific discovery, audit intelligence, LLM-based empirical measurement, multi-agent research systems, digital twins, causal and computational discovery, and verifiable AI**.
