# Contributing to ECONOVA-S™

ECONOVA-S™ is a controlled research and intellectual-property project maintained by Saeid Homayoun.

## Contribution policy

Contributions are not accepted under an open-source inbound=outbound model.

Before any external contribution is merged, the contributor must obtain written approval from the repository owner and, when requested, sign an appropriate Contributor License Agreement (CLA), intellectual-property assignment, confidentiality agreement, or other written instrument.

Submitting a pull request, issue, comment, idea, suggestion, code fragment, model, dataset, prompt, architecture, or other material does **not** by itself transfer ownership to the repository owner and does **not** grant the submitter rights in ECONOVA-S™. For that reason, substantive external contributions will not be merged until ownership and licensing are documented.

## Confidential material

Do not submit confidential, proprietary, unpublished, patent-sensitive, personally identifiable, restricted, licensed, or third-party material unless you have explicit authority to do so and the repository owner has approved the disclosure in writing.

## Patent-sensitive contributions

Potential inventions, novel technical mechanisms, unpublished algorithms, system-control methods, and implementation details that may warrant patent review should be discussed privately before public submission.

## Research contributions

Academic suggestions, replication results, bug reports, and literature references are welcome for review. Acceptance does not imply authorship, co-ownership, endorsement, or a license beyond the repository LICENSE.

## Commercial proposals

Commercial integration, consultancy, licensing, partnerships, or deployment proposals should not be submitted as code contributions. They require a separate written commercial agreement.
