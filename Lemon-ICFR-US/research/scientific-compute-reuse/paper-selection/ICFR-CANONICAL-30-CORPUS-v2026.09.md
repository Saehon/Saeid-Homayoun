## Governance rules

1. This 30-paper corpus supersedes the earlier four-paper selection as the broad ICFR candidate corpus; prior four-paper manifests remain archived for provenance.
2. The 30 papers are not mechanically averaged.
3. Meta-analysis and review papers build the knowledge map; they are not coefficient sources.
4. Management Science papers are prioritized for replication/executability, but code/data must be independently verified before executable admission.
5. FT50 empirical papers provide the direct scientific-model bank.
6. Theory papers provide mechanisms and falsifiable structural predictions.
7. Final executable M1 models require primary-source verification, exact estimand alignment, provenance, and human approval.
8. GitHub links are recorded only when a public repository was verified.
