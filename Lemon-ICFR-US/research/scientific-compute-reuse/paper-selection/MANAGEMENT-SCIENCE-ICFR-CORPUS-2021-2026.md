## External evidence/data layer — Audit Analytics

Audit Analytics is treated as a non-peer-reviewed external evidence and data-source layer, not as a substitute for journal evidence.

Use cases include:
- material-weakness prediction red flags;
- SOX 404 adverse-assessment trends;
- repeat adverse ICFR assessments;
- remediation patterns;
- IPO-related control weaknesses;
- internal-control issue taxonomies;
- links between ICFR, late filings, restatements, auditor changes, CFO changes, disclosure controls, and cybersecurity events.

Relevant category/source:
https://blog.auditanalytics.com/category/audit-compliance/internal-controls-icfr-sox404/

Notable evidence examples:
- “Predicting Material Weakness” (2017): AQRM red flags and one-year-ahead material-weakness prediction.
- “SOX 404 Disclosures: An Eighteen-Year Review” (2022): long-run ICFR disclosure trends.
- “Repeat Adverse ICFR Assessments” (2023): persistence of adverse assessments.
- IPO/internal-control weakness analyses and remediation studies.
