Journal: Journal of Accounting Research 50(3) (2012), 811–843
DOI: 10.1111/j.1475-679X.2011.00434.x
Regime: SOX 404.
Population: restating firms whose original misstatements are linked to underlying control weaknesses.
Scientific role: reporting/detection conditional on underlying weakness; designed to separate reporting from existence.

Reported descriptive result: only a minority acknowledge existing weaknesses; separate author summary reports 32.4% during misstatement period.
Generalizability limitation: restating-firm design may not generalize to weaknesses that do not lead to restatements.
