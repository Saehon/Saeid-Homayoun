# ICFR Scientific Compute Reuse — Execution to Submission Roadmap

## Phase 7A — 30-paper ingestion
Create one evidence passport for each paper. Extract role, theory, estimand, outcome, unit, sample, timing, variables, estimator, coefficients/specification, data source, code/replication status, and provenance.

## Phase 7B — compatibility graph
Classify each paper by:
- weakness existence/risk
- discovery
- disclosure/reporting
- remediation
- governance/monitoring
- audit opinion
- IT/cyber controls
- reporting-process resilience
- consequences
- theory/mechanism

Never pool incompatible estimands.

## Phase 7C — executable admission
Only empirical models with sufficient primary evidence become M1 candidates. Meta-analysis/SLR inform the knowledge graph; theory papers inform mechanisms and falsification; Management Science packages benchmark replication engineering.

## Phase 7D — frozen empirical design
Freeze outcome, prediction horizon, unit, train/validation/test periods, feature timing, inclusion/exclusion, missing-data rules, and evaluation metrics before ML comparison.

## Phase 7E — model tournament
M1 = published science
M2 = traditional ML
M3 = deep/foundation models
M4 = scientific prior + residual learning

Report accuracy, calibration, recall/precision, transportability, interpretability, compute cost, data burden, reproducibility, and provenance.

## Phase 7F — falsification
Run temporal, industry, size, auditor, data-quality, and alternative-outcome stress tests. Record failures rather than hiding them.

## Phase 8 — manuscript
Write results only after execution. Maintain reproducible tables/figures and a replication package.

## Phase 9 — submission
Choose journal after results and contribution are known. Journal fit is evaluated from realized evidence, not fixed ex ante. No submission before human approval.
