# Article 01 — ICFR Scientific Compute Reuse

## Working title
**Scientific Compute Reuse in Accounting: Turning Published ICFR Models into Executable, Falsifiable, and Reusable Knowledge**

Status: PRE-RESULTS WORKING MANUSCRIPT
Target family: Management Science / JAR / TAR / JAE
Topic: Internal Control over Financial Reporting (ICFR)
Program: LEMON-SCI

## Core research question
Can published ICFR knowledge be transformed from paper-based descriptions into provenance-preserving executable scientific objects, and how does that scientific baseline compare with modern machine-learning and residual-learning alternatives?

## Scientific architecture
30-paper canonical corpus -> evidence passports -> estimand graph -> variable ontology -> executable model contracts -> M1 Published Science -> M2 Traditional ML -> M3 Deep Learning -> M4 Scientific Prior + Residual Learning -> falsification -> transportability -> manuscript.

## Current corpus
- 5 meta-analysis / quantitative synthesis papers
- 5 systematic literature reviews
- 10 Management Science papers from 2021–2026, prioritized for replication/data/code
- 6 FT50 empirical ICFR papers
- 4 theory/mechanism papers

Canonical source:
`paper-selection/ICFR-CANONICAL-30-CORPUS-v2026.09.md`

## Current empirical status
Pre-results. No unexecuted empirical result is presented as a finding.

ACK2007 remains the first engineering/scientific-model pilot. Scientific admission is governed separately from engineering reproducibility and requires primary-source provenance.

## Planned contribution
1. Scientific-compute reuse as a new representation of accumulated accounting knowledge.
2. A provenance-preserving compiler from published models to executable contracts.
3. A reproducible comparison of published science versus ML/DL alternatives.
4. A scientific-residual learning design that learns only after the published-science baseline is frozen.
5. A topic-level architecture that can later generalize beyond ICFR.
