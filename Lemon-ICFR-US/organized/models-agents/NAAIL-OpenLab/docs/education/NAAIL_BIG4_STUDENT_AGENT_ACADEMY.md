# NAAIL Big Four Student Agent Academy™

## Purpose

The NAAIL Big Four Student Agent Academy™ is a research-safe university–industry learning model that prepares accounting and audit students to work with AI-enabled professional workflows before entering practice.

The model does **not** require an audit firm to disclose proprietary production systems, confidential client data, audit methodology, prompts, models, or internal controls. Instead, a participating firm may contribute a limited educational use case, learning objective, challenge brief, guest session, or approved educational proxy-agent specification. NAAIL converts that contribution into a synthetic Digital Twin learning environment.

The Academy is intended to create value for three parties:

- **Students:** practical experience with AI-enabled audit reasoning, evidence evaluation, professional skepticism, documentation, and human oversight.
- **Universities:** industry-relevant teaching, measurable skills development, research opportunities, and employability evidence.
- **Participating firms:** a pre-employment learning pipeline, employer-branding opportunity, aggregate skills insight, and a safe mechanism to shape AI-ready graduate capabilities without exposing proprietary systems.

## Educational proxy-agent principle

A proxy agent is a **teaching approximation**, not a replica of a firm's proprietary AI system.

Every proxy agent must:

1. use synthetic or appropriately licensed data;
2. avoid confidential firm methodology and client information;
3. identify itself as an educational simulation;
4. expose evidence used for material claims;
5. allow students to challenge and override its recommendations;
6. log model/agent/tool versions where applicable;
7. terminate material conclusions at a Human Gate;
8. never imply firm endorsement unless a written partnership agreement explicitly permits it.

## Recommended pilot structure

### NAAIL Student Agent Pilot 001

- **1 participating professional-services firm**;
- **1 university course**;
- **20–40 master students**;
- **1 educational proxy audit agent**;
- **3 synthetic Digital Twin cases**;
- **4–6 week teaching period**;
- **pre/post assessment** of audit judgment, AI verification, and professional skepticism;
- **aggregate anonymized report** to the partner.

### Initial case family

1. Revenue Recognition & Cut-off
2. Goodwill Impairment
3. ICFR / Control Deficiency

These cases align with the existing NAAIL Prototype 003 frozen synthetic benchmark family.

## Student task model

For each case, students should complete a structured sequence:

```text
Understand the entity
→ identify audit risk
→ identify assertion(s)
→ inspect evidence
→ review agent recommendation
→ search for contradictory evidence
→ propose audit procedure
→ document judgment
→ accept / modify / reject agent conclusion
→ Human Gate
```

The educational objective is not to teach students to trust AI. It is to teach them to **work with, test, challenge, document, and govern AI-supported professional judgment**.

## Student readiness dimensions

NAAIL may assess:

- Risk identification
- Assertion alignment
- Procedure design
- Evidence quality assessment
- Professional skepticism
- Contradictory-evidence detection
- AI verification
- Documentation quality
- Human–AI judgment
- Reproducibility / traceability
- Escalation judgment
- Time-to-resolution

The canonical NAAIL audit metrics remain:

- **RPA** — Risk–Procedure Alignment
- **AA** — Assertion Alignment
- **EG** — Evidence Grounding
- **PS** — Professional Skepticism
- **DS** — Documentation Sufficiency
- **DIST** — Decision Integrity / Stability

Education-specific extensions may include:

- **AIV** — AI Verification
- **CER** — Contradictory Evidence Recognition
- **HOR** — Human Override Reasoning
- **ESC** — Escalation Judgment
- **RET** — Learning Retention

Scores must be treated as educational measurements with documented validity limits, not as definitive measures of employability or professional competence.

## Recruitment pathway

Recruitment is optional and separate from academic grading.

A participating firm may receive only aggregate/anonymized course-level results by default. Individual student information may be shared for recruitment only with explicit student consent and appropriate university/privacy approval.

NAAIL should never sell individual student performance data.

## Research design opportunity

The Academy can support controlled research comparing learning conditions such as:

```text
Traditional case
vs
General AI assistant
vs
Single audit agent
vs
Sequential specialist agents
vs
Governed multi-agent system + Human Gate
```

Potential outcomes include:

- audit judgment accuracy;
- professional skepticism;
- evidence-grounding quality;
- automation bias / AI overreliance;
- conclusion reversal;
- documentation quality;
- learning retention;
- task time;
- confidence calibration;
- ability to detect fabricated or weak evidence.

Any research study must follow informed-consent, ethics, privacy, preregistration/analysis-plan, reproducibility, and Human Gate requirements where applicable.

## Partner value proposition

A partner can participate without providing production technology. The minimum viable contribution can be one of:

- a challenge brief;
- learning objectives;
- a guest lecture;
- a case review;
- an approved educational agent specification;
- a synthetic-data scenario;
- mentorship;
- a student challenge or hackathon;
- feedback on the NAAIL readiness rubric.

The partner receives an aggregate report showing areas such as student readiness, common reasoning failures, professional-skepticism performance, evidence-grounding quality, AI-overreliance indicators, and recommended pre-employment training priorities.

## Branding and non-endorsement

Until a written agreement exists:

- do not use a firm's logo;
- do not name a track after a firm;
- do not state that a firm sponsors, certifies, validates, or endorses NAAIL;
- use neutral labels such as **Firm Alpha**, **Firm Beta**, or **Industry Partner**.

## Public/private boundary

**Public:** educational architecture, synthetic cases, score definitions, research protocol, student instructions, evaluation principles.

**Private/partner-controlled:** unpublished partner briefs, proprietary learning objectives, internal feedback, recruitment data, restricted benchmarks, confidential methodology, credentials, production endpoints, and any patent-sensitive implementation.

## One-sentence partnership proposition

> NAAIL OpenLab provides a safe synthetic Digital Twin and educational proxy-agent environment in which accounting students can learn to test, challenge, document, and govern AI-supported audit judgments before entering professional practice—without requiring firms to disclose proprietary audit technology or client data.
