## Origin correction and verification status (12 October 2026)

The research owner clarifies that the project datasets were **collected from SEC EDGAR**, and that their resemblance to Audit Analytics is **a format/schema choice only**. Our previous documentation attributed the files categorically to Audit Analytics; that attribution is **withdrawn as an established fact**. The statement of SEC origin is owner-reported and **has not yet been independently checked against original accession numbers and filing URLs**.

Fields such as `AUDIT_OPINION_FKEY`, company keys, topic taxonomies and enrichment columns resemble vendor exports, but a field label alone does not prove where a record came from. The UK/European KAM corpus needs particular attention: trace each claimed SEC-based observation to an actual EDGAR filing, or record another authenticated public or licensed primary source when no SEC filing exists.

See [the stepwise source reconciliation checklist](PROVENANCE_RECONCILIATION_2026-10-12.md).

## Redistribution boundary
A genuinely SEC-derived, correctly attributed, independently traceable public subset may be suitable for public reproducibility. Any non-SEC vendor enrichments must be identified and rights checked separately. Keep unverified extracts, manuscripts and credentials out of public GitHub, Kaggle and Hugging Face until source mapping, permission review and secrets scanning have passed.
