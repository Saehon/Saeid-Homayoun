# Saeid Homayoun

**Accounting & Auditing Researcher | AI, Large Language Models & Reproducible Financial Intelligence**

I am an accounting researcher at the University of Gävle, Sweden. My work connects **accounting theory, audit quality, ESG and financial disclosures** with **LLM evaluation, NLP, data analytics, agentic AI and human-centered assurance**.

### Research interests

- **LLMs and financial reasoning** — benchmarks, evidence grounding, error taxonomies and reproducible evaluations
- **Auditing and professional judgment** — CAM/KAM, internal controls, ICFR and audit evidence
- **Agentic AI for accounting** — multiple-agent workflows, uncertainty, falsification, provenance and human review
- **Financial and sustainability reporting** — IFRS, ESG/ISSB, earnings quality and governance
- **Open and reproducible science** — Python, transparent datasets, versioned experiments and research validation
