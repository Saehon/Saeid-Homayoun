## Advancing evidence-grounded AI in accounting and assurance

My research brings together **accounting theory, audit and assurance, financial data analytics, large language models (LLMs), and agentic AI**. A central question runs through this work: *How can AI systems support high-stakes professional judgments while preserving the traceability of evidence, reproducibility of results, scientific skepticism, and human responsibility?*

The research spans financial-reporting decisions, Critical and Key Audit Matters (CAM/KAM), internal control over financial reporting (ICFR), AI model evaluation, and sustainability-related information. The emphasis is on **methods that can be inspected, challenged, and reproduced**, rather than unsupported claims about autonomous AI.

> **Research in progress — private working materials.** These projects are **actively evolving**. The **primary working materials and substantive project documentation are maintained in my personal, access-controlled Google Drive**, including detailed research drafts, internal analysis materials, supporting research records, and evolving project plans. **They are not publicly accessible through this GitHub repository.** GitHub provides selected public-safe research descriptions, versioned software, evaluation protocols, and reproducibility materials. Public repository contents should not be interpreted as the complete project, a final research outcome, an independent validation, or a production-ready system.

### Scientific standards shared across the projects

**Evidence provenance** → **construct and model validation** → **reproducible code and data controls** → **adversarial/falsification tests** → **independent expert review where applicable** → **human professional judgment**.

These stages are scientific and engineering objectives; the public implementation of each programme may cover only a subset. The [research evidence policy](RESEARCH_EVIDENCE_POLICY.md) distinguishes *concept*, *implemented*, *tested*, *measured*, *independently reviewed*, and *published* outputs.
