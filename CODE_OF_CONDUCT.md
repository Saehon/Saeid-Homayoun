# Code of Conduct

ECONOVA-S™ is a research and scientific-software project. Participation should reflect the standards expected in serious academic and technical collaboration.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should:
- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- distinguish evidence from opinion;
- disclose material limitations, conflicts and uncertainty;
- avoid fabricated citations, data, results or benchmarks;
- preserve reproducibility and provenance;
- challenge ideas and methods rather than individuals;
- respect intellectual property, licenses, privacy and research ethics;
- report security or data-governance concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:
- harassment, intimidation or discriminatory conduct;
- deliberate misrepresentation of evidence or authorship;
- undisclosed manipulation of analyses to obtain preferred results;
- posting secrets, confidential data or restricted material;
- knowingly bypassing scientific, security or licensing controls;
- claiming ECONOVA-S endorsement, certification or scientific discovery without authorization.

## Scientific disagreement

Substantive disagreement is welcome. Critiques should identify the claim, evidence, method or assumption being challenged and, where possible, propose a falsifiable test or alternative explanation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, close or remove contributions that violate this policy or threaten the scientific integrity, security, licensing or reputation of the project.

By participating, contributors agree to follow this Code of Conduct together with `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md`, the repository license and applicable research-ethics requirements.
