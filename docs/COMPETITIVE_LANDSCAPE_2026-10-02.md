# Competitive Landscape — ECONOVA-S™ / NAAIL Research Hub

**Date:** 2026-10-02  
**Scope:** flagship research hub and portfolio governance.

## Representative competitors

1. **MindBridge AI** — financial-risk intelligence and augmented assurance.
2. **Optro (formerly AuditBoard)** — audit, risk, controls, compliance and GRC system of action.

## Capability matrix

| Dimension | ECONOVA-S / NAAIL target | MindBridge | Optro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full-population financial analytics | Integration target through specialist agents | Core public capability | Available through analytics/control workflows |
| Audit / controls workflow | Evidence-governed research workflow | Audit/assurance workflow | Core audit/GRC workflow |
| Scientific reproducibility | Core research objective | Not central in reviewed public positioning | Not central in reviewed public positioning |
| Falsification / replication | Core research-assurance objective | Not evident as a research-science layer | Not evident as a research-science layer |
| Cross-model / provider-neutral orchestration | Architecture target | Governed agentic capabilities now public | Agentic GRC direction now public |
| Human approval boundary | Explicit governance requirement | Professional judgment emphasized | Practitioner orchestration emphasized |
| Evidence lineage / research provenance | Core differentiator hypothesis | Explainability and transaction rationale | Evidence/control documentation |
| Continuous assurance | Portfolio target | Continuous financial oversight | Continuous monitoring/GRC |

## Positioning hypothesis

Do **not** position ECONOVA-S as a replacement for either platform. Position it as a **research-assurance and evidence-governance layer** that can test, reproduce, falsify and document claims produced by domain agents and external systems.

## Next competitive build

- Preserve a provider-neutral evidence contract.
- Add benchmark cases that compare reproducibility, provenance and human-review routing.
- Keep commercial-platform comparisons evidence-based and version-dated.
- Separate research prototype claims from production readiness.

## Evidence basis

This comparison uses publicly documented capabilities reviewed on 2026-10-02:

- MindBridge: full-population financial analysis, explainable risk scoring, anomaly detection, continuous/periodic monitoring, and audit/assurance workflows: https://www.mindbridge.ai/ and https://www.mindbridge.ai/technology/
- Optro (formerly AuditBoard): GRC, internal audit, controls/compliance, analytics, continuous monitoring, automated evidence and testing workflows: https://optro.ai/auditboard-to-optro and legacy product materials at https://auditboard.com/

**Boundary:** this is a capability-overlap map, not a claim of market superiority. "Not evident" means the capability was not evident in the reviewed public materials; it does not prove the competitor lacks it.
