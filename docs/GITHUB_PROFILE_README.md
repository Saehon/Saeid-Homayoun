## Flagship research platform

### [NAAIL OpenLab™](https://github.com/Saehon/Saeid-Homayoun)
**Next-Generation Accounting, Audit & Assurance Intelligence Lab**

NAAIL OpenLab is my public, non-commercial research and education platform for evidence-governed multi-agent and Digital Twin experimentation in accounting, auditing, assurance, finance, internal control, sustainability, governance, and scientific discovery.

Core principle:

**Evidence → Analysis → Challenge → Falsification → Replication → Human Review**

The platform separates a **Stable Knowledge Core™** from a **Replaceable Technology Core™**, allowing models and tools to change without silently changing the professional or scientific meaning of the task.

## Research standard

I am especially interested in work that is:

**Reproducible · Evidence-grounded · Falsifiable · Provider-neutral · Human-accountable**

A successful model run, benchmark, simulation, or GitHub Action demonstrates technical execution. It does not by itself establish scientific validity, causal evidence, regulatory approval, professional assurance, or superiority over alternatives.
