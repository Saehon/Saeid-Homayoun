Accounting × Auditing × Artificial Intelligence

Evidence-grounded AI for accounting and assurance.

I am Saeid Homayoun, Senior Lecturer in Accounting at the University of Gävle. My work connects accounting, auditing and sustainability research with AI, NLP, multi-agent systems and reproducible empirical methods.

Research agenda

Three connected areas of work

The research program combines domain knowledge in accounting and auditing with computational methods that can be tested, replicated and governed.

01

AI-enabled auditing

Audit analytics, ICFR, CAM/KAM, inspection logic, evidence tracing and human-in-the-loop assurance.

02

Financial reporting & sustainability

IFRS, ESG disclosure, corporate governance, forensic accounting and financial-reporting judgment.

03

Computational research systems

NLP, AuditBERT-style models, multi-agent reasoning, causal inference and reproducible data pipelines.
