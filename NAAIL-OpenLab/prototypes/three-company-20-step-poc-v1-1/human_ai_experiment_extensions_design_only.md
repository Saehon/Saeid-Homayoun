# Three-Company Human–AI Experiment Extensions — Design Only

**Status:** `DESIGN_ONLY` — no participant observations have been collected.

## Walmart task
Professional judgment case on a retail contingency disclosure. Conditions:
T0 Human only; T1 Human + AI; T2 Human + AI + explanation; T3 Human + AI + contradictory evidence.
Outcomes: classification accuracy, disclosure judgment, skepticism, contradiction recognition, confidence, time.

## JPMorgan task
Professional judgment case on allowance for loan losses / Level 3 valuation evidence. Same T0–T3 conditions.
Outcomes: risk-assessment accuracy, evidence weighting, model-reliance calibration, skepticism, contradiction recognition, confidence, time.

No behavioral result is claimed until preregistration, participant recruitment, consent/ethics requirements where applicable, execution, and analysis are completed.
