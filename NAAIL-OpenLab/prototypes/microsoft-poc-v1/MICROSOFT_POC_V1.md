**Golden Anchor Company:** Microsoft Corporation  
**Fiscal anchor:** FY ended June 30, 2026  
**Maturity:** `RESEARCH_PROTOTYPE`

Prove that NAAIL can connect:

**Real public company evidence → Data & Evidence Mesh™ → Evidence Passport™ → Accounting/Audit/Finance/Text/Innovation modules → Microsoft Digital Twin → AI-cost and behavioral experiment layers → Human Gate™.**

## Management Accounting / AI Cost

A **synthetic Microsoft-like AI business process** tests BSC/ABC/TDABC/AI-ABC/TTD-AIC concepts. Internal Microsoft management-accounting data are not claimed or inferred.

Process:

**RESOURCE → ACTIVITY → TIME → TOKENS → COST → OUTPUT → QUALITY → VALUE**

Cost optimization is subordinate to evidence/quality thresholds.

## AI model cost benchmark

`ai_cost_benchmark.csv` demonstrates the arithmetic of **Cost per Verified Output** using public LiteLLM price snapshots and explicitly **synthetic verification rates**. This validates the costing pipeline, not comparative model intelligence.
