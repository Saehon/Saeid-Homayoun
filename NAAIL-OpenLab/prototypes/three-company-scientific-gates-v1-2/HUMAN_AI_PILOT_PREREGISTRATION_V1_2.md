# NAAIL Prototype V1.2 — Human–AI Pilot Preregistration Draft

**Status:** `DESIGN_ONLY`  
**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Companies:** Microsoft, Walmart, JPMorgan Chase

## Research purpose
Test whether evidence-governed AI assistance changes professional accounting/audit judgment quality, evidence use, calibration, decision time, and resistance to contradictory evidence.

## Conditions
- **T0 — Human only:** evidence packet, no AI.
- **T1 — Human + AI:** evidence packet plus AI recommendation.
- **T2 — Human + AI + explanation:** AI recommendation plus traceable explanation/evidence links.
- **T3 — Human + AI + contradictory evidence:** same as T2 plus a deliberately relevant contradictory evidence item.

## Common task families
1. CAM classification and risk mapping.
2. Financial-statement/assertion mapping.
3. ICFR judgment.
4. Evidence retrieval and source discrimination.
5. Contradiction detection.
6. Sector-specific professional judgment.

## Primary outcomes
Professional correctness against a frozen rubric; evidence-grounding score; contradiction detection; confidence/calibration; completion time; revision behavior after contrary evidence; and human override/acceptance behavior.

## Governance
Participant recruitment, consent/ethics requirements, power analysis, randomization implementation, preregistration platform, and analysis plan must be completed before participant execution. No participant data exist in V1.2.
