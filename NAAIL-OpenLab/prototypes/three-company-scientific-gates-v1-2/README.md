## What changed from V1.1

Prototype V1.2 closes the previously open bounded **Fama–French execution gate** for all three companies and advances the patent/entity-resolution and professional-AI-benchmark infrastructure without overstating incomplete evidence.

### Newly executed
- 31-month split-consistent market-return package, 2024-01 through 2026-07;
- CAPM, FF3 and FF5 for MSFT/WMT/JPM with HC3 inference;
- dividend-inclusive approximation sensitivity;
- shorter 19-month window sensitivity;
- Walmart split-contamination falsification;
- professional-benchmark gold set plus a leakage-prone same-session harness self-pilot retained only as a schema check;
- integrated V1.2 Digital Twin state;
- V1.2 artifact validation and reproducibility runner.

### Advanced but not scientifically complete
- patent/innovation: source viability and major assignee exemplars validated; aggregate counts/citations/diversity remain unexecuted;
- professional AI benchmark: harness/gold set are `RESEARCH_PROTOTYPE`; blind independently invoked multi-model benchmarking remains open;
- Cost per Verified Professional Output™: definition frozen, numeric estimate not executed because cost and human-verification telemetry are missing.

### Human-dependent gates remain open
- T0–T3 participant execution: `DESIGN_ONLY`;
- WMT/JPM Human Gate: `REGISTERED_NOT_EXECUTED`;
- independent replication: `REGISTERED_NOT_EXECUTED`.

## Scientific boundary

This release is a research prototype. It does not convert a short-window factor model into a causal claim, a self-pilot into a blind model benchmark, a patent assignee exemplar into aggregate patent evidence, or an AI-generated packet into a Human Gate decision.
