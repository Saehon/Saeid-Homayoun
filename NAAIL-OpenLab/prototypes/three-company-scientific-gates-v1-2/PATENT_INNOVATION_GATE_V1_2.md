## Why aggregate counts are not promoted

A publishable count/citation/diversity measure requires:
1. complete historical assignee alias resolution;
2. parent/subsidiary treatment rules fixed before counting;
3. grant-year and citation-window definitions frozen;
4. technology-class mapping frozen;
5. duplicate/family handling documented;
6. direct reproducible access to the relevant PatentsView/ODP files.

Those conditions are not all satisfied in this execution environment. No patent count, citation count, technology-diversity score, or R&D-scaled patent-intensity value is fabricated.
