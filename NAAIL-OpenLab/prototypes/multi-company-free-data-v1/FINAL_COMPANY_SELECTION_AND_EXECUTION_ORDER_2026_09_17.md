## Final selection

NAAIL will retain all nine companies, but execute them in controlled waves to preserve comparability and scientific validity.

### Wave 0 — Golden Anchor

1. **Microsoft Corporation (`MSFT`)** — technology / cloud / AI; canonical Golden Anchor.

Microsoft remains the reference implementation. Its 15/15 bounded artifact-validation result does not by itself close the broader scientific validation gate. Fama–French execution, PatentsView innovation validation, professional-task AI benchmark, T0–T3 human experiment, falsification and independent replication remain separate gates.

### Wave 1 — U.S. SEC replication cohort

2. **Walmart Inc. (`WMT`)** — retail / supply chain / operations.  
3. **JPMorgan Chase & Co. (`JPM`)** — financial services / banking.  
4. **Intuit Inc. (`INTU`)** — accounting/financial software and AI-enabled services.  
5. **Exxon Mobil Corporation (`XOM`)** — energy / capital-intensive operations.  
6. **Fluor Corporation (`FLR`)** — engineering / construction / long-term contracts.  
7. **The Boeing Company (`BA`)** — aerospace / defense / program and contract complexity.

These companies are selected because the current NAAIL environment has a common minimum evidence stack: public SEC annual filings and XBRL, public auditor-report evidence, verified connected IEX market access, public Fama–French factor data, public macro data, issuer disclosures and open USPTO patent-data channels subject to source terms.

### Wave 2 — cross-border / IFRS extension

8. **Shopify Inc. (`SHOP`)** — cross-border technology/commerce issuer.  
9. **SAP SE (`SAP`)** — European IFRS software issuer / U.S. ADR and 20-F filer.

Wave 2 is retained as part of the official cohort, but it is not mechanically pooled with Wave 1. Explicit jurisdiction, GAAP/IFRS, currency, listing/ADR and factor-model adapters must be documented first.

## Why this nine-company design is preferred

The cohort gives NAAIL meaningful industry heterogeneity without abandoning reproducibility:

`Technology/AI → Retail → Banking → Accounting Software → Energy → Engineering/Construction → Aerospace → Cross-Border Commerce → IFRS Enterprise Software`

This supports replication of the common NAAIL evidence-governance architecture while allowing sector-specific accounting and audit adapters to be tested rather than hidden.

## Mandatory common pipeline

For every company, the standard sequence is:

`Public Filing → XBRL/Financial Evidence → Variable Dictionary → Audit/CAM/ICFR Evidence → Bounded Text → Market Data → Factor Package → Innovation Evidence → Evidence Passport™ → Robustness/Falsification → Human Gate™`

Common minimum outputs:

- source/provenance registry;
- filing/XBRL extraction record;
- standardized and sector-specific variable dictionary;
- audit/CAM/ICFR mapping where applicable;
- bounded filing-text features;
- market-return package;
- factor-model input/output record;
- innovation measures with assignee/entity-resolution notes;
- company-specific Evidence Passport™;
- falsification/robustness record;
- Human Gate decision.
