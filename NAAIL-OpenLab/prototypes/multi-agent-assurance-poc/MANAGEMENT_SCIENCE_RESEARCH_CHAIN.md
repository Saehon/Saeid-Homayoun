## Interpretation boundary

This chain is a theory-building and research-design scaffold. The cited papers do not by themselves validate the NAAIL/LEMON commercial product or prove that a multi-agent system dominates alternative designs. Exact paper findings, effect sizes and causal interpretations must be verified from primary sources before use in a manuscript or product claim.

## Proposed Management Science question

Can an evidence-governed multi-agent architecture that separates evidence verification, specialist financial-risk analysis, independent challenge, assurance review and final human authority improve decision quality, calibration, evidence use and accountability relative to human-only, single-model AI and ordinary multi-agent alternatives?

## Testable architecture

Evidence acquisition → provenance and temporal cutoff → Evidence Passport → specialist risk analysis → independent Challenger/Falsifier → model-disagreement test → Assurance Reviewer → Human Approval Gate → outcome tracking → failure memory.

## First empirical wedge

LEMON–ICFR should be the first specialist research/product module because it can be evaluated on point-in-time financial-reporting and internal-control evidence using explicit outcome definitions, temporal holdouts, calibration metrics and human-review outcomes.

## Commercial interpretation

The parent opportunity is not another accounting chatbot. It is a provider-neutral trust and assurance layer for AI-enabled finance, accounting, audit and controls. LEMON is the first specialist product; CAM/KAM, PCAOB, AAER/forensic, IFRS, ESG and cyber/control modules can be added to the same assurance infrastructure after validation.
