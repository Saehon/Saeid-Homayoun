## Identification decision

The architecture effect is identified by holding substantive evidence constant while varying governance intensity:

- `A0` — direct model answer from the frozen evidence;
- `A1` — A0 evidence plus Evidence Passport™ governance;
- `A2` — A1 plus public-safe NAAIL verification, contradiction/falsification and decision-path governance.

A1/A2 may organize, transform and challenge the same evidence, but they may not receive additional substantive evidence, standards excerpts, hidden answer keys, human hints or external retrieval unavailable to A0.
