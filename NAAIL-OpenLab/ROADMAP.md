# NAAIL OpenLab™ — Product & Research Roadmap

## V0.2 — Public research release
- non-commercial research/education license;
- business-school scope;
- Audit Digital Twin concept;
- FT50/AJG evidence architecture;
- citation/ORCID metadata;
- public/private IP boundary.

## V0.3 — Enterprise research foundation
- governed 37-agent registry;
- NAAIL Control Plane™ specification;
- provider-neutral runtime contract;
- evaluation standard and frozen benchmark policy;
- observability/tracing standard;
- security and threat model;
- product/research release gates.

## V0.4 — Interactive Audit Workspace
A student/researcher can:
1. open synthetic Client XYZ;
2. select an audit scenario;
3. inspect planning/materiality/risk;
4. launch specialist agents;
5. review evidence and citations;
6. inspect disagreements and quality scores;
7. approve/reject/escalate conclusions;
8. export a reproducible run manifest.

## V0.5 — Executable Agent Mesh
- specialist delegation and explicit handoffs;
- graph workflows;
- checkpoint/resume;
- structured outputs;
- scoped tool permissions;
- model routing;
- human approvals;
- complete trace IDs.

## V0.6 — Evidence & Provenance Plane
- Evidence Passport™;
- source/version/license metadata;
- standards mapping;
- citation verification;
- Knowledge Graph/GraphRAG;
- literature manifests;
- Chain-of-Evidence and run manifests.

## V0.7 — Comparative Digital Twin Benchmark
Run frozen cases across Firm Alpha–Delta and publish research-safe comparative results for accuracy, audit quality, evidence use, stability, cost, latency, and human-agent interaction.

## V0.8 — Education & Research Platform
- instructor dashboard;
- student learning pathways;
- rubrics and assessments;
- experiment manager;
- replication packages;
- benchmark leaderboards;
- research-paper workflow.

## V0.9 — Controlled pilots
University/research collaborations using synthetic or properly licensed data under explicit governance.

## V1.0 — Stable NAAIL research platform
Release only when the platform has documented governance, reproducible benchmarks, traceable agent behavior, validated Digital Twin experiments, regression gates, and a clear IP/security boundary.

## Non-goal
NAAIL is not intended to clone proprietary Big Four products. It is an independent research and education platform that studies and advances evidence-governed AI for professional work.
