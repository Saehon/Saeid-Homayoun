## Canonical naming rule

The three lines above are the canonical public identity for NAAIL OpenLab™ from 2026-09-16 onward.

Use **Next-Generation Accounting, Audit & Assurance Intelligence Lab** as the expansion of **NAAIL** in current public documentation, architecture pages, research-platform descriptions, Google Drive mirrors, presentations, website copy, and future releases.

The retired geographic expansion **Nordic Accounting, Audit & Assurance Intelligence Lab** is a historical name only. Historical release records, archived evidence, citations to previously issued material, and third-party references may retain the wording when preserving provenance requires it, but current canonical pages must use the new global identity.
