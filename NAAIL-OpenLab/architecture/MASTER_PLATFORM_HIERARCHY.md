# NAAIL OpenLab™ — Canonical Master Platform Hierarchy

> **PATENT RIGHTS RESERVED — PATENT APPLICATION PREPARATION IN PROGRESS**

**Canonical version:** V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin

## Fixed public architecture

**NAAIL OpenLab™** is the umbrella platform. It preserves exactly two permanent cores:

1. **Knowledge Core™** — governed professional/scientific knowledge and evidence semantics.
2. **Technology Core™** — replaceable models, tools, simulation technologies, data connectors, and execution infrastructure.

All other NAAIL elements remain governed supporting agents, layers, services, Digital Twins, evaluations, or interfaces. No third permanent core is created.
