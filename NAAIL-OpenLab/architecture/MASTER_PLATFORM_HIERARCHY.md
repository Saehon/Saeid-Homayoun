## Frozen constitutional architecture

The governing document is **[NAAIL Two-Core Constitution](../TWO_CORE_CONSTITUTION.md)**.

NAAIL OpenLab™ permanently preserves exactly two cores:

1. **Stable Knowledge Core™** — governed scientific/professional meaning: accounting, auditing, finance, economics, management, behavioral science, innovation/entrepreneurship theory, Nobel-inspired theory, IFRS/assurance knowledge, sustainability, causal inference, research design, construct definitions, professional judgment, replication, falsification and scientific governance. **Balanced Scorecard, Strategy Maps, ABC, TDABC, strategic management accounting and performance-measurement theory belong here as stable management-accounting knowledge.**
2. **Replaceable Technology Core™** — replaceable implementations: LLMs, AI agents, model routers, RAG/GraphRAG/KAG technology, vector/graph databases, Python/R/Stata, simulation engines, APIs, connectors, GitHub packages, MCP/A2A, observability/evaluation, benchmark tooling, AI FinOps, telemetry and visualization technologies.

**No third permanent core is permitted.** New domains, datasets, vendors, models or technologies extend NAAIL only through modular layers, specialist programmes/agents or replaceable implementations.

### Management Accounting & AI Cost placement

**Stable Knowledge Core™** governs BSC/Strategy Maps, ABC, TDABC, budgeting, variance analysis, responsibility accounting, cost pools/drivers, profitability, capacity/unused capacity, performance measurement and management-accounting constructs.

The **Management Accounting & AI Cost Intelligence Layer™** applies that stable knowledge across traditional operations and AI workflows. Public NAAIL extensions include **AI Activity-Based Costing™**, **Token- and Time-Driven AI Costing™ (TTD-AIC)** and governed Cost-to-Value research. These extensions are not represented as original Kaplan/Kaplan–Norton/Kaplan–Anderson frameworks.

The **Open Model Benchmark & Cost Intelligence Layer™** and **Visualization & Decision Intelligence Layer™** use replaceable benchmark, FinOps, observability, routing, dashboard and visualization technologies. A technology upgrade must not silently change the scientific meaning of the management-accounting constructs.

Public scientific objective:

**Resources → Activities → Time → Tokens → Agents → Decisions → Quality → Evidence → Cost → Value**

Operational objective:

**Find the minimum defensible AI cost that satisfies required quality, evidence, reliability, reproducibility and professional-judgment standards—not minimum token cost alone.**

### Stable meaning vs replaceable implementation

Governed theory, standards, evidence semantics, source authority, construct definitions, ontologies and approved knowledge objects remain under Stable Knowledge Core governance. Retrieval/simulation/benchmark/FinOps/visualization implementations—embedding models, vector databases, graph engines, RAG/GraphRAG/KAG frameworks, LLMs, agent runtimes, model benchmarks, price feeds, telemetry and dashboards—remain replaceable Technology Core infrastructure or supporting layers.

## Governance inherited by every layer/programme

Where applicable, every layer and programme must use provenance, license controls, Evidence Passport™, causal/decision DAGs, versioning, reproducibility, replication, falsification, red-team/adversarial review and Human Approval Gate™.

For AI-cost/model comparison specifically:

- price and benchmark observations require timestamped provenance;
- benchmark ranking is not treated as scientific truth;
- public data are not assumed to permit unrestricted redistribution;
- cost minimization may not override evidence, quality or governance requirements;
- external implementations must be validated before promotion.
