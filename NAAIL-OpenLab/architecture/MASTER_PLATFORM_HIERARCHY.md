**Canonical version:** V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin

## Fixed public architecture

**NAAIL OpenLab™** is the umbrella platform. It preserves exactly two permanent cores:

1. **Knowledge Core™** — governed professional/scientific knowledge, behavioral theory, standards, evidence semantics, ontologies, and research knowledge.
2. **Technology Core™** — replaceable models, tools, agent runtimes, experiment/simulation technology, analytics, data connectors, and execution infrastructure.

All other NAAIL elements remain governed supporting agents, layers, services, Digital Twins, evaluations, experiments, or interfaces. **No third permanent core is created.**

## Behavioral Decision Science placement

The Behavioral Decision Science & Human–AI Experimentation Layer is a **supporting research layer only**.

At a high level:

- the **Knowledge Core™** governs behavioral theories, constructs, literature, professional-domain interpretation, measurement semantics, and research evidence;
- the **Technology Core™** provides replaceable experiment execution, randomization, logging, statistical/causal analysis, text analytics, AI-treatment tooling, and Digital Twin infrastructure;
- the layer may support accounting, auditing, finance, economics, management, ESG/sustainability, and professional-judgment studies;
- behavioral measures are treated as **empirical proxies**, not permanent personality classifications or psychological diagnoses;
- study designs should emphasize credible identification, reproducibility, falsification, replication, and Human Approval;
- FT50/AJG status must be verified when used and must not be treated as a frozen hard-coded ranking.

Detailed treatment orchestration, outcome schemas, experiment-control mechanics, unpublished measurement logic, and patent-sensitive implementation remain private pending filing review.
