# NAAIL OpenLab™ — Canonical Master Platform Hierarchy

> **PATENT RIGHTS RESERVED — PATENT APPLICATION PREPARATION IN PROGRESS**

**Canonical version:** V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin

## Fixed public architecture

**NAAIL OpenLab™** is the umbrella platform. It preserves exactly two permanent cores:

1. **Knowledge Core™** — governed professional/scientific knowledge and evidence semantics.
2. **Technology Core™** — replaceable models, tools, simulation technologies, data connectors, and execution infrastructure.

All other NAAIL elements remain governed supporting agents, programmes, layers, services, Digital Twins, evaluations, or interfaces. No third permanent core is created.

### CCCMP placement

CCCMP™ is a modular specialist programme for construction, infrastructure, energy, oil & gas, mining, EPC/EPCM, PPP, major capital projects and megaprojects. It combines high-level project-cost, contract, procurement, claims/delay, project-finance/accounting, behavioral-decision-science, research and education capabilities while reusing the existing NAAIL cores and governance.

**CCCMP is not a third core.** Its professional knowledge is governed through the Knowledge Core; its replaceable tools, connectors, simulation technologies and execution infrastructure reside in the Technology Core.

Detailed CCCMP orchestration, contract/claims graphs, entitlement logic, event/state transitions, source-to-agent routing, behavioral treatment logic, Evidence Passport internals and unpublished implementations remain private pending patent filing review.
