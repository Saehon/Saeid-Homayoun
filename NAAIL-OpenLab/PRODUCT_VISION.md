# NAAIL OpenLab™ — Product Vision

**A research-first, human-led AI platform for business-school education, audit simulation and scientific discovery.**

NAAIL OpenLab combines a business-school learning environment with an Audit Digital Twin, evidence intelligence, governed AI agents and reproducible research workflows.

## Public product pillars

### 1. Audit Workspace
A guided environment for planning, risk assessment, controls, evidence, analytics, documentation, review and reporting using synthetic cases.

### 2. Multi-Agent Audit Lab
A governed network of specialist audit agents working under human supervision. Public simulations use fictional Firm Alpha–Delta architectures and Client XYZ.

### 3. Digital Twin
Students and researchers can run the same synthetic audit problem across different agent architectures and compare risk identification, evidence quality, professional judgment, CAM/KAM decisions, documentation quality, cost and time.

### 4. Evidence Intelligence
Professional tasks prioritize applicable standards and regulation. Academic tasks use relevance-gated FT50, AJG/ABS 4*/4 and selected high-quality 3 literature for theory, methods, measurement and research design.

### 5. Quality & Governance
NAAIL is designed around evidence traceability, human approval, adversarial review, reproducibility and explicit limitations. AI output is not treated as an audit opinion or scientific discovery by default.

### 6. Scientific Discovery
Research workflows integrate competing hypotheses, empirical design, computational discovery, adversarial review, falsification, replication and Chain-of-Evidence.

## Industry inspiration
The product is informed by publicly documented directions in modern audit technology, including connected agent networks, workflow-native AI, enterprise multi-agent orchestration, real-time engagement visibility, cross-platform orchestration and AI observability.

NAAIL does **not** reproduce Deloitte Omnia, KPMG Clara, EY Canvas, PwC Aura/Next Gen Audit/agent OS, or any other proprietary product. Firm Alpha–Delta are independent research archetypes.

## Current focus
**Auditing first.**

The broader platform architecture supports Accounting, Auditing, Finance and Economics at bachelor’s and master’s levels, but the first deep product vertical is Audit Intelligence and Digital Twin simulation.

## Public release boundary
The public repository contains research-safe documentation, synthetic simulations and educational materials. Deeper product architecture, unpublished methods, patent-candidate mechanisms and restricted development artifacts are maintained separately.
