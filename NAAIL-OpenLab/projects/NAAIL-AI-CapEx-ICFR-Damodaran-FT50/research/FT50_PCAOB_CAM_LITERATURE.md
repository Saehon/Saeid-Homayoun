## Publication positioning and limits
A journal-worthy novelty requires the incremental explanatory value of **ex ante, independently measured account risk and CAM alignment**, the **auditor-firm's as-of-date public inspection exposure**, and **real AI-specific investment** for future cash flow, impairment, or investor pricing. Do NOT present a new AI model or the CAM–ICFR association alone as an FT50 breakthrough.
Complete verified systematic searches, publication cutoffs, paper replication-availability assessment, and a theory-driven identification strategy before submitting.
