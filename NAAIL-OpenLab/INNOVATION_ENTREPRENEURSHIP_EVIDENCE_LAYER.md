# NAAIL Innovation & Entrepreneurship Evidence Layer™

**Platform:** NAAIL OpenLab™ — V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin  
**Status:** `PATENT_HOLD_NON_ENABLING`  
**Architecture role:** cross-cutting governed supporting capability — **not a core**

> **PATENT RIGHTS RESERVED — PATENT APPLICATION PREPARATION IN PROGRESS**

## Purpose

The **NAAIL Innovation & Entrepreneurship Evidence Layer™** connects innovation and entrepreneurship theory with rights-cleared real data, patents, startup and firm-formation evidence, economic complexity, behavioral experiments, AI agents, causal inference, Digital Twins, reproducible research, and current FT50/AJG research benchmarks.

It serves the full NAAIL platform across:

**Accounting · Auditing · Finance · Economics · Management · Entrepreneurship · Innovation · Strategy · Sustainability · AI/Data Economy · Business Education · Scientific Research**

It does **not** create a third permanent core and does not change the canonical professional-agent set or validated Prototype 003 checkpoint.

## Fixed two-core placement

### Stable Knowledge Core™

Governs theory, construct definitions, innovation/entrepreneurship evidence semantics, research literature, professional knowledge, measurement definitions, causal structures, provenance meaning, and validated knowledge objects.

### Replaceable Technology Core™

Provides replaceable data connectors, network-analysis tools, causal/econometric tooling, experiment platforms, simulation technology, graph infrastructure, AI agents, language models, analytics, databases, and execution infrastructure.

The Innovation & Entrepreneurship Evidence Layer™ spans these two cores as a governed supporting layer only.

## Public capability families

### Innovation & patent evidence

Candidate governed source families may include public patent and IP statistics, patent applications/grants, inventors, assignees, citations, technology classes, technological novelty/impact indicators, regional innovation measures, green/AI/biotechnology patent indicators, and other legally reusable innovation evidence.

Public source names are reference targets only. Every source must pass current license, access, provenance, version, citation, and redistribution review before ingestion or reuse.

### Entrepreneurship & firm-dynamics evidence

The layer may evaluate rights-cleared public evidence on startup formation, employer-firm entry, business entry/exit, firm survival, job creation, founder/business characteristics, entrepreneurial density, regional entrepreneurship, and related government or research indicators.

### Science-to-innovation evidence

Supports research on lawful links among scientific knowledge, papers, patents, inventors, universities, startups, financing, commercialization, products, and economic value.

### Digital innovation evidence

Supports public-indicator research on software ecosystems, open-source activity, developers, repositories, organizations, programming languages, digital capability, regional technology activity, innovation diffusion, and AI ecosystems.

**GitHub repository ≠ authoritative dataset.** Public repository/activity indicators require provenance and measurement validation before use.

### Economic complexity & entrepreneurial opportunity research

Supports research combining economic/product complexity, relatedness, diversification, patent capability, startup formation, digital capability, market growth, and other governed measures.

Any **Entrepreneurial Opportunity** measure is a research variable for empirical testing—not a deterministic recommendation to founders, firms, investors, or policymakers.

### Startup/founder evidence

Only legally reusable, provenance-verified startup, founder, financing, registry, or public announcement sources may be used. Scraped or mirrored datasets are not authoritative merely because they are available on GitHub or another public website.

### Innovation networks

Supports high-level network research involving inventor networks, founder networks, university–startup relationships, startup–VC relationships, patent-citation networks, scientific collaboration, technology diffusion, and entrepreneurial ecosystems using rights-cleared data and replaceable graph/network technology.

### Behavioral entrepreneurship

Supports research on observable decision constructs such as entrepreneurial risk taking, loss aversion, overconfidence proxies, optimism, ambiguity aversion, framing, escalation of commitment, pivot behavior, founder persistence, investor/VC decisions, AI reliance, AI override, and founder–AI collaboration.

These are empirical research constructs—not psychological diagnoses or permanent personality classifications.

### Startup Digital Twin™

Public research scope covers high-level multi-agent startup simulation across idea formation, research, prototyping, IP, team formation, financing, product development, customers, growth, scaling, pivoting, exit, and failure, including governed shocks such as financing, competition, regulation, technology, demand, talent, cyber, supply-chain, and AI-adoption shocks.

Detailed agent state models, transition rules, reward structures, orchestration, scenario generation, and unpublished simulation mechanics remain private under patent hold.

### Entrepreneurial finance

Supports research linking innovation/startup variables with public/rights-cleared financial, macroeconomic, valuation, funding, patent, market, and firm evidence to study venture financing, cost of capital, ownership/dilution, capital structure, valuation, exits, acquisitions, IPOs, patent value, and innovation risk.

## Open-source technology registry — public boundary

Candidate technology/reference resources may include economic-agent simulation, agent-based modeling, experimental platforms, network analysis, causal inference, financial simulation/RL, NLP/Transformers, econometrics, and data-access libraries.

Examples may include **HARK, Mesa, oTree, SNAP, DoWhy, EconML, FinRL, ABIDES, Hugging Face Transformers, NetworkX, Statsmodels, and pandas-datareader**, subject to current license, maintenance, security, quality, and execution verification.

**Registry entry ≠ dependency installed.**  
**Open-source software ≠ authoritative data.**

## Innovation theory and research gate

The layer may organize foundational innovation/entrepreneurship theories such as Schumpeterian innovation and creative destruction, disruptive innovation, resource-based view, dynamic capabilities, absorptive capacity, open innovation, knowledge spillovers, entrepreneurial ecosystems, real options, agency, information asymmetry, behavioral entrepreneurship, institutional theory, platform/network effects, and transaction-cost economics.

It may also connect relevant Nobel/foundational theories through the separate **NAAIL Nobel Theory-to-Evidence & AI Experiment Engine™**.

Public scientific logic:

**Theory → Construct → Hypothesis → Variable → Rights-cleared Data → Experiment / Causal Test → Replication / Falsification → Evidence Passport™ → Human Gate™**

Theory names are not treated as validated measurements by themselves.

## FT50 / AJG research governance

When journal status is used, the current **Financial Times FT50** list and current **Chartered ABS Academic Journal Guide** edition/rating must be verified dynamically rather than hard-coded.

Relevant innovation/entrepreneurship research may be benchmarked against current high-quality general-management, strategy, finance, economics, information-systems, innovation, and entrepreneurship journals after current list/rating verification.

Lawful open versions may be discovered through sources such as OpenAlex, SSRN metadata/abstracts, institutional repositories, author pages, CORE, DOAJ, RePEc, and other rights-cleared sources. Public availability does not itself authorize redistribution.

## Practitioner Knowledge RAG boundary

Publicly accessible consulting, professional, university, and practitioner insights may be indexed at metadata/citation/link level where appropriate. Copyrighted public-access material is treated as `PUBLIC_ACCESS_COPYRIGHTED`, `KNOWLEDGE_RAG_ONLY`, `METADATA_ONLY`, or another rights-controlled class unless explicit reuse rights permit more.

## Measurement families

High-level research variables may include patent quantity/impact/originality/generality, inventor counts, scientific reliance, R&D intensity, startup entry/survival, employment growth, innovation productivity, technology diversity, founder experience, ecosystem density, digital/open-source activity, economic/product complexity, entrepreneurial opportunity measures, VC intensity, scaling, innovation text measures, AI adoption, founder behavior, confidence/calibration, and pivot behavior.

Every operationalization requires construct validity, provenance, versioning, transformation documentation, and reproducibility.

## Scientific research pipeline

Publication-grade projects should, where applicable, document:

**Research Question → FT50/AJG Literature → Theory → Hypothesis Tournament → Causal DAG → Variable DNA™ → Rights-cleared Data → AI/Behavioral Experiment → Digital Twin → Econometrics/Causal ML → Robustness → Replication → Falsification → Evidence Passport™ → Human Gate™**

## Rights, provenance and Evidence Passport™

Every source/data object must pass a license/access gate and preserve sufficient provenance for reproducibility and lawful reuse. Public classification families may include open reuse, Creative Commons classes, academic/non-commercial, public-access copyrighted, metadata/link-only, permission-required, restricted, and do-not-ingest states.

Detailed machine-readable license logic, connector contracts, data-object schemas, transformation pipelines, hashes, graph relations, Evidence Passport internals, opportunity-scoring mechanics, experimental orchestration, and Startup Digital Twin state-transition implementation remain private pending patent filing review.

## Final public objective

The layer studies and simulates, at a governed high level:

**Scientific Discovery → Invention → Patent → Entrepreneurship → Financing → Commercialization → Innovation → Scaling → Productivity → Firm / Market / Social Value**

using rights-cleared real data, reproducible open-source technology, current research evidence, behavioral experiments, causal inference, Digital Twins, Evidence Passport™ governance, and accountable human scientific approval.
