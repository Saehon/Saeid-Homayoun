# NAAIL™ — Evidence-Governed AI for Business

**Powered by NAAIL OpenLab™**  
**Marketplace target:** v0.2.4  
**Status:** Public marketplace-preparation package — **not yet submitted, approved, certified, or listed** by OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Anthropic, or any other provider.

> **Turn professional questions into traceable evidence, reproducible analysis, and human-reviewed decisions.**

NAAIL™ is an evidence-governed AI platform for **Accounting, Audit, Finance, Economics, Sustainability, Research, and Business-School Education**. It combines professional digital twins, multi-agent reasoning, reproducible empirical analysis, adversarial review, Chain-of-Evidence, and mandatory Human Gate approval.

## Who it is for

| User | What NAAIL helps them do |
|---|---|
| **Auditors & assurance teams** | Investigate risks, CAM/KAM issues, ICFR, evidence gaps, and professional judgments |
| **Researchers & universities** | Move from research question to hypotheses, empirical design, code, robustness, replication, and evidence |
| **Finance & economics teams** | Analyze filings, valuation, asset pricing, economic data, and scenario evidence |
| **Students** | Practice professional judgment through synthetic digital-twin cases and AI-versus-human review |
| **CFO / controller teams** | Explore reporting risk, controls, disclosure, ESG, and evidence-supported decisions |

## Three marketplace product families

### 1. NAAIL Research Co-Scientist™
**From research question to reproducible empirical study.**

- literature and evidence validation;
- competing-hypothesis generation and critique;
- causal DAG governance;
- ERA-style empirical design;
- Python / R / Stata-ready research specifications;
- robustness, falsification, replication, and AI-to-AI review;
- Evidence Passport™ and Human Gate™.

**Best for:** researchers, doctoral students, universities, research centers, and evidence-intensive business analytics.

### 2. KIWI™ Audit Intelligence
**From audit risk to evidence-governed professional judgment.**

- CAM/KAM analysis;
- audit assertions and risk–procedure alignment;
- IFRS / SEC / PCAOB evidence workflows;
- ICFR and controls analysis;
- digital-twin audit simulations;
- adversarial reviewer / critic workflows;
- RPA, AA, EG, PS, DS, and DIST evaluation;
- Human Gate before material conclusions.

**Best for:** audit education, assurance research, professional training, and controlled audit-intelligence experiments.

### 3. ECONOVA-S™ Finance & Data-Economy Intelligence
**From financial/economic question to reproducible analytical evidence.**

- SEC / XBRL evidence;
- Fama–French and asset-pricing research;
- valuation and Damodaran-style public-data workflows;
- economic digital twins;
- model comparison and scenario testing;
- reproducible empirical outputs.

**Best for:** finance, economics, valuation, capital-markets research, and data-economy studies.

## One simple customer journey

```text

          ↓
NAAIL finds and validates evidence
          ↓
Specialist agents analyze the problem
          ↓
Independent AI critic challenges the result
          ↓
Reproducibility / robustness / falsification checks
          ↓
Evidence Passport™ + Chain-of-Evidence
          ↓
Human Gate™
          ↓
Decision / Report / Research Artifact / Learning Feedback
```

The user should not need to choose among dozens of agents. NAAIL routes specialist capabilities behind a simple professional interface.

## Why NAAIL is different

NAAIL is designed around **evidence governance**, not autonomous claims.

```text
✓ Source-grounded evidence
✓ Citation and provenance traceability
✓ Human approval for material conclusions
✓ Reproducible analysis
✓ Adversarial AI-to-AI review
✓ Causal DAG governance
✓ Falsification and replication
✓ Failure Memory™
✓ Digital-twin evaluation
✓ Provider-neutral architecture
```

**Scientific invariant:** model confidence, statistical significance, agent consensus, or predictive accuracy alone is never sufficient to establish a scientific or professional conclusion.
