# NAAIL OpenLab™ Privacy Policy — Draft for Marketplace Review

**Status:** Draft. Requires legal review and production-specific completion before marketplace submission.

## Scope

This draft describes the intended privacy practices for the NAAIL OpenLab™ marketplace-facing service. It must be updated to reflect the actual production infrastructure, legal entity, subprocessors, data locations, retention periods, contact details, and applicable law before launch.

## Data NAAIL may process

Depending on the feature used, the service may process account identifiers, research questions, prompts, uploaded research materials, datasets authorized by the user, generated analyses, evidence metadata, tool-execution metadata, technical logs, and support communications.

## Purposes

Data should be processed only to provide and secure the requested research service, maintain reproducibility and provenance, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, satisfy legal obligations, and improve the service where a lawful and disclosed basis exists.

## Sensitive and confidential research material

Users should submit only material they are authorized to process. Production systems must apply access controls, data minimization, tenant isolation, and logging/redaction controls appropriate to confidential research materials.

## Third-party AI and cloud providers

NAAIL may rely on third-party infrastructure or AI providers selected for the deployed product. The production privacy policy must name or link to the current subprocessor/provider list and explain when user content is transmitted to those providers.

## Model training

The production policy must state explicitly whether user content is used for training NAAIL models or any third-party models. No such use should be implied or hidden.

## Retention

Production retention periods must be documented by data class. Secrets and authentication credentials must not be retained in model-visible logs.

## User rights and requests

A production release must provide a working method for users to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, export, or other rights available under applicable law.

## Security

NAAIL intends to apply least privilege, encryption in transit, appropriate encryption at rest, secrets management, tenant isolation, logging minimization, incident-response controls, and provider-specific security requirements.

## Automated decisions

NAAIL is designed as a human-led research platform. Material scientific, audit, accounting, assurance, financial, or professional conclusions remain subject to Human Gate review and should not be treated as solely automated binding decisions.

## Children

The marketplace product should not be directed to children unless a provider-specific and legally compliant education configuration is separately designed and approved.

## Contact

Before submission, replace this section with the legal entity name, postal address, privacy contact email, and any required EU/EEA representative or data-protection contact information.

## Changes

Material changes to production privacy practices should be reflected in an updated policy with an effective date and version history.
