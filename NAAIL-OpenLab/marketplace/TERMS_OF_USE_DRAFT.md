# NAAIL OpenLab™ Terms of Use — Draft for Marketplace Review

**Status:** Draft. Requires legal review and completion before production launch.

## 1. Research-purpose service

NAAIL OpenLab™ provides AI-assisted research, analysis, evidence-governance, and educational functionality. It is not a substitute for independent professional judgment, legal advice, investment advice, audit sign-off, accounting certification, or regulatory approval.

## 2. Human responsibility

Users remain responsible for reviewing outputs, checking source evidence, validating empirical designs and code, confirming rights to data, and approving material conclusions. NAAIL's Human Gate is a workflow control and does not transfer professional responsibility from the user or their organization.

## 3. Acceptable use

Users must not use the service to violate law, third-party rights, marketplace policies, confidentiality obligations, data licenses, academic-integrity requirements, or security controls.

## 4. User content and data rights

Users represent that they have sufficient rights to submit and process the content and datasets they provide. Restricted or licensed materials may be subject to additional limitations and must not be redistributed through the service without authorization.

## 5. AI limitations

AI systems can produce incomplete, incorrect, biased, outdated, or non-reproducible outputs. Agent consensus, confidence scores, statistical significance, and model performance do not by themselves establish scientific truth or professional adequacy.

## 6. Third-party services

Some deployments may depend on third-party model, cloud, data, identity, or marketplace providers. Their separate terms, availability, quotas, and policies may apply.

## 7. Intellectual property

NAAIL names, platform architecture, documentation, software, and research assets are governed by the applicable repository license, commercial-use policy, trademark notice, and any separate production agreement. Third-party materials retain their original rights and licenses.

## 8. Availability and changes

The service may change as providers, models, standards, security requirements, and research methods evolve. Features may be modified, suspended, or withdrawn where required for security, compliance, rights, or product reasons.

## 9. No warranty / limitation language

Production legal counsel must insert jurisdiction-appropriate warranty disclaimers, limitation-of-liability language, governing law, dispute terms, and consumer/business provisions before launch. This public draft intentionally does not invent those legal terms.

## 10. Contact

Before marketplace submission, insert the legal entity name, registered or business address, support contact, and legal/privacy contact information.
