# NAAIL OpenLab™ Marketplace Listing Metadata

## Canonical product name

**NAAIL OpenLab™ — Evidence-Governed Research Co-Scientist**

## Short description

Evidence-governed AI for reproducible research in accounting, auditing, finance, economics, and sustainability.

## Long description

NAAIL OpenLab™ is a human-led AI research platform designed to move from a research question to a traceable, reproducible, and reviewable scientific workflow. It helps users frame questions, generate and critique competing hypotheses, build empirical designs, work with rights-cleared evidence and data, compare models and specifications, perform robustness and falsification checks, build a Chain-of-Evidence, and route material conclusions through a Human Gate.

NAAIL is designed for research and education workflows where provenance, reproducibility, limitations, and human judgment matter. Provider-specific models and agents are treated as replaceable components behind a governed interface rather than as the source of scientific truth.

## Safety / governance statement

NAAIL does not treat AI output, model confidence, agent consensus, statistical significance, or benchmark performance as an automatically approved scientific or professional conclusion. Material conclusions remain subject to evidence validation, stated limitations, reproducibility controls, and human review.
