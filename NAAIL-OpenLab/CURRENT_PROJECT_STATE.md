## Management Accounting & AI Cost expansion

Public assets:

- [`MANAGEMENT_ACCOUNTING_AI_COST_INTELLIGENCE_LAYER.md`](./MANAGEMENT_ACCOUNTING_AI_COST_INTELLIGENCE_LAYER.md)
- [`OPEN_MODEL_BENCHMARK_COST_INTELLIGENCE_LAYER.md`](./OPEN_MODEL_BENCHMARK_COST_INTELLIGENCE_LAYER.md)
- [`VISUALIZATION_DECISION_INTELLIGENCE_LAYER.md`](./VISUALIZATION_DECISION_INTELLIGENCE_LAYER.md)

The research direction extends established management-accounting logic into AI-enabled operations while keeping theory stable and implementation replaceable.

NAAIL-developed extensions include **AI Activity-Based Costing™**, **Token- and Time-Driven AI Costing™ (TTD-AIC)** and Cost-to-Value Decision research. These are NAAIL extensions inspired by established management-accounting ideas; they are not represented as original Kaplan/Kaplan–Norton/Kaplan–Anderson frameworks.

Scientific objective:

**Resources → Activities → Time → Tokens → Agents → Decisions → Quality → Evidence → Cost → Value**

Operational objective:

**Minimum defensible AI cost subject to required quality, evidence, reliability, reproducibility and professional-judgment standards.**

Candidate benchmark/FinOps/visualization technologies remain reference targets until current license, maintenance, security, provenance, quality and execution verification. Price/benchmark values require timestamped provenance.

## Architecture principle

**Stable Theory / Knowledge → Evidence / Data → Replaceable Technology → Agents / Simulations → Decision → Verification → Evidence Passport™ → Human Approval Gate™**

## Governance requirement

Every layer/programme must use, where applicable: provenance, license controls, Evidence Passport™, causal/decision DAGs, versioning, reproducibility, replication, falsification, red-team/adversarial review and Human Approval Gate™.

For AI-cost/model comparisons, cost minimization may not silently override quality, evidence, reliability, reproducibility or professional judgment.

## Permanent priorities

1. preserve exactly two permanent cores;
2. keep BSC/ABC/TDABC and other stable management-accounting meaning in/governed through Stable Knowledge Core™;
3. keep benchmark, FinOps, observability, routing and visualization implementations replaceable;
4. test AI-ABC/TTD-AIC/Cost-to-Value constructs scientifically rather than presenting them as established doctrine;
5. timestamp price and benchmark observations with provenance;
6. never optimize AI cost at the expense of required evidence/quality/reliability;
7. enforce evidence provenance, rights, reproducibility, replication/falsification, red-team review and Human Approval;
8. keep public patent-sensitive descriptions high-level until filing review.
