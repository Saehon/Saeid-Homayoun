# NAAIL OpenLab™ — Two-Core Constitution

> **PATENT RIGHTS RESERVED — PATENT APPLICATION PREPARATION IN PROGRESS**

**Canonical architecture:** NAAIL OpenLab™ — V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin  
**Constitutional status:** FROZEN  
**Permanent core count:** 2

## Constitutional rule

NAAIL OpenLab™ permanently preserves exactly two cores:

1. **Stable Knowledge Core™**
2. **Replaceable Technology Core™**

**No third permanent core may be created.** New domains, datasets, AI models, open-source packages, vendors, agents, simulations, educational modules, research programmes or professional applications must be added as modular supporting layers, specialist agents/programmes, or replaceable technology.

## 1. Stable Knowledge Core™

The Stable Knowledge Core™ contains governed scientific and professional meaning that should remain valid even when technology changes. It includes, at a high level:

- accounting;
- auditing;
- finance;
- economics;
- management;
- management-accounting theory, including Balanced Scorecard / Strategy Maps, Activity-Based Costing, Time-Driven Activity-Based Costing, budgeting, responsibility accounting, variance analysis, cost-pool/cost-driver theory, profitability, capacity management, performance measurement and strategic management accounting;
- behavioral science;
- innovation and entrepreneurship theory;
- Nobel-inspired economic theory;
- IFRS / assurance knowledge;
- sustainability;
- causal inference;
- research design;
- construct definitions;
- professional judgment;
- replication;
- falsification;
- scientific governance.

The Stable Knowledge Core governs meaning, evidence semantics, theory, standards, constructs, causal structures and professional/scientific judgment. Technology may use these governed objects but may not silently redefine them.

**BSC, ABC and TDABC are stable management-accounting knowledge. NAAIL-developed AI costing extensions may build on them, but must not be presented as original Kaplan/Kaplan–Norton/Kaplan–Anderson frameworks.**

## 2. Replaceable Technology Core™

The Replaceable Technology Core™ contains implementations that may be upgraded, replaced, retired or substituted without redesigning the NAAIL architecture. It includes, at a high level:

- LLMs;
- AI agents;
- model routers;
- RAG / GraphRAG / KAG implementations;
- vector databases;
- graph databases;
- Python / R / Stata;
- simulation engines;
- APIs;
- data connectors;
- GitHub packages;
- MCP / A2A interoperability;
- observability and evaluation tools;
- model benchmark/evaluation implementations;
- AI FinOps / cloud-cost tooling;
- telemetry and trace tooling;
- dashboard and visualization software.

A technology change does not automatically change canonical scientific meaning, theory, standards, constructs, evidence semantics or approved knowledge objects.

### Management Accounting & AI Cost placement

The **Management Accounting & AI Cost Intelligence Layer™** operationalizes stable management-accounting theory across traditional and AI-enabled operations. Its public research scope includes Balanced Scorecard, Strategy Maps, ABC, TDABC, AI Activity-Based Costing™, Token- and Time-Driven AI Costing™ (TTD-AIC), AI-cost transparency, capacity/unused-capacity analysis and cost-to-value research.

The **Open Model Benchmark & Cost Intelligence Layer™** and **Visualization & Decision Intelligence Layer™** are supporting layers using replaceable benchmark, pricing, FinOps, observability, routing and dashboard technology. A benchmark/provider/dashboard change must not redefine management-accounting theory or scientific constructs.

Public scientific objective:

**Resources → Activities → Time → Tokens → Agents → Decisions → Quality → Evidence → Cost → Value**

Operational objective:

**Find the minimum defensible AI cost that satisfies required quality, evidence, reliability, reproducibility and professional-judgment standards—not minimum token cost alone.**

### Placement clarification

Governed retrieval meaning, ontology, evidence semantics, source authority and approved knowledge objects belong under Stable Knowledge Core governance. The underlying retrieval, simulation, benchmark, pricing, FinOps and visualization implementation—LLM, embedding model, vector database, graph engine, RAG framework, benchmark suite, cost feed, telemetry framework or dashboard technology—is replaceable Technology Core infrastructure or a supporting layer.

## Placement rule

If a capability should remain valid when models, software, datasets or vendors change, place it in or govern it through the **Stable Knowledge Core™**.

If a capability can be replaced by a better implementation, place it in the **Replaceable Technology Core™** or in a modular supporting layer.

Examples:

- innovation theory → Stable Knowledge Core™;
- Kahneman / contract theory / causal inference → Stable Knowledge Core™;
- BSC / ABC / TDABC / strategic management accounting → Stable Knowledge Core™;
- SEC / FRED / patent / startup / management-accounting data → NAAIL Data & Evidence Mesh™;
- HELM / LiveBench / benchmark connectors / LiteLLM / OpenCost / FinOps / observability / dashboard software → Replaceable Technology Core™ or governed supporting layers;
- Mesa / oTree / HARK / FinRL / DoWhy → Replaceable Technology Core™;
- Management Accounting & AI Cost Intelligence → cross-cutting layer;
- Open Model Benchmark & Cost Intelligence → cross-cutting layer;
- Visualization & Decision Intelligence → cross-cutting layer;
- Innovation & Entrepreneurship → cross-cutting layer;
- Behavioral Science → cross-cutting layer;
- CCCMP™ → specialist programme;
- Business School Digital Twin → cross-cutting simulation layer.

## Governance inherited by every layer and programme

Every layer, programme and agent must use, where applicable:

- provenance;
- license controls;
- Evidence Passport™;
- causal / decision DAGs;
- versioning;
- reproducibility;
- replication;
- falsification;
- red-team / adversarial review;
- Human Approval Gate™.

For model-cost/benchmark evidence, NAAIL additionally requires timestamped price/benchmark provenance and forbids optimizing cost in a way that silently reduces required scientific/professional quality or evidence standards.
