## Upgrade summary

The 16 September 2026 upgrade expanded NAAIL's global business-education, Digital Twin, data/evidence, professional-agent, and scientific-governance architecture while preserving the fixed **V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin** umbrella and exactly two permanent cores:

- **Knowledge Core™**
- **Technology Core™**

The upgrade introduced or consolidated named supporting capabilities including the NAAIL Data & Evidence Mesh™, Business School Simulation & Digital Twin Layer, Decision–Consequence Engine™, Professional Judgment Passport™, VERA™, and the Audit & Accounting Digital Twin research family.

## Patent-first hardening later on 2026-09-16

After identifying that some implementation-level details had already been publicly committed, the project adopted:

**Patent first → public disclosure second.**

The current public branch was then hardened so patent-sensitive mechanisms are described only at a non-enabling high level. Detailed algorithms, state-transition logic, dependency/revocation behavior, containment, evaluation isolation, promotion/demotion mechanisms, technical flowcharts, claim concepts, and unpublished embodiments were moved to a private patent-readiness workspace.

This hardening **does not erase earlier Git history**. Known relevant historical commits are recorded in [`PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE_LOG.md`](./PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE_LOG.md).
