## Upgrade summary

The 16 September 2026 upgrade expanded NAAIL's global business-education, Digital Twin, data/evidence, professional-agent, behavioral-research, project/capital-programme, and scientific-governance architecture while preserving the fixed **V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin** umbrella and exactly two permanent cores:

- **Knowledge Core™**
- **Technology Core™**

The upgrade introduced or consolidated named supporting capabilities including the **NAAIL Value Expansion Fabric™**, **NAAIL Behavioral Decision Science & Human–AI Experimentation Layer™**, **CCCMP™ — Project Cost, Contract & Claims Management Programme**, NAAIL Data & Evidence Mesh™, Business School Simulation & Digital Twin Layer, Decision–Consequence Engine™, Professional Judgment Passport™, VERA™, and the Audit & Accounting Digital Twin research family.

No new permanent core was created.

## CCCMP specialist-programme upgrade

CCCMP™ was added as a governed specialist programme for:

- construction and infrastructure;
- energy, oil & gas and mining;
- EPC/EPCM and PPP;
- major capital projects and megaprojects.

Its public high-level scope combines:

- cost engineering, estimating, EVM and project controls;
- contract, procurement and commercial management;
- claims, delay, quantum and dispute analysis;
- project finance, accounting and IFRS-related project issues;
- behavioral project decision science and human–AI judgment;
- public/rights-cleared project, procurement, macro, energy, commodity and legal evidence families;
- research, education and Digital Twin simulation.

CCCMP reuses the two NAAIL cores. Professional/project knowledge is governed through the Knowledge Core; replaceable analytics, connectors, experiment/simulation tools and execution infrastructure reside in the Technology Core.

Detailed contract/claims graphs, entitlement logic, source-to-agent routing, Evidence Passport internals, automated rights controls, behavioral treatment structures, project event/state transitions and Digital Twin execution mechanics remain private under patent hold.

A public [`docs/research/CCCMP_RESEARCH_GOVERNANCE.md`](./docs/research/CCCMP_RESEARCH_GOVERNANCE.md) now establishes research/education/industry separation, rights-aware evidence use, current FT50/AJG verification when rankings are used, behavioral-research safeguards, causal/simulation distinction and human approval.

## Behavioral Decision Science upgrade

A governed supporting research capability was added to study human judgment and AI-assisted decision making across accounting, auditing, finance, economics, management, ESG/sustainability, professional education and project/capital-programme contexts.

The public research framing covers:

- behavioral economics and finance;
- behavioral accounting and auditing;
- professional skepticism and evidence search;
- human–AI reliance, override, advice taking and trust calibration;
- behavioral and financial text analytics;
- controlled experiments, Digital Twin replications, archival validation, and human–AI studies;
- causal identification, preregistration, power analysis, replication, falsification, external validation, and reproducibility;
- dynamic verification of current FT50/AJG status when journal rankings are used.

Behavioral variables are defined as **empirical research proxies**, not permanent personality classifications or psychological diagnoses.

Detailed treatment arms, outcome-vector schemas, experiment-control logic, unpublished measurement mechanics, and potentially patent-sensitive orchestration remain private pending filing review.

## Patent-first hardening

After identifying that some implementation-level details had already been publicly committed, the project adopted:

**Patent first → public disclosure second.**

The current public branch is hardened so patent-sensitive mechanisms are described only at a non-enabling high level. Detailed algorithms, state-transition logic, dependency/revocation behavior, containment, evaluation isolation, promotion/demotion mechanisms, experiment-control implementation, contract/claims graph mechanics, technical flowcharts, claim concepts, and unpublished embodiments are maintained in a private patent-readiness workspace.

This hardening **does not erase earlier Git history**. Known relevant historical commits are recorded in [`PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE_LOG.md`](./PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE_LOG.md).
