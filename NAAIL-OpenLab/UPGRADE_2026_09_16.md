Authoritative/professional evidence domains may include **IFRS Foundation/IASB, ISSB, PCAOB, IAASB, IFAC/IESBA, EFRAG/ESRS, GRI/GSSB, IOSCO, SEC** and relevant jurisdictional authorities.
