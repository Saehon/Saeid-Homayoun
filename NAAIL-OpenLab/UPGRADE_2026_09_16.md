## Upgrade summary

The 16 September 2026 upgrade expanded NAAIL's global business-education, Digital Twin, data/evidence, professional-agent, behavioral-research, and scientific-governance architecture while preserving the fixed **V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin** umbrella and exactly two permanent cores:

- **Knowledge Core™**
- **Technology Core™**

The upgrade introduced or consolidated named supporting capabilities including the **NAAIL Value Expansion Fabric™**, **NAAIL Behavioral Decision Science & Human–AI Experimentation Layer™**, NAAIL Data & Evidence Mesh™, Business School Simulation & Digital Twin Layer, Decision–Consequence Engine™, Professional Judgment Passport™, VERA™, and the Audit & Accounting Digital Twin research family.

No new permanent core was created.

## Behavioral Decision Science upgrade

A new governed supporting research capability was added to study human judgment and AI-assisted decision making across accounting, auditing, finance, economics, management, ESG/sustainability, and professional education.

The public research framing covers:

- behavioral economics and finance;
- behavioral accounting and auditing;
- professional skepticism and evidence search;
- human–AI reliance, override, advice taking and trust calibration;
- behavioral and financial text analytics;
- controlled experiments, Digital Twin replications, archival validation, and human–AI studies;
- causal identification, preregistration, power analysis, replication, falsification, external validation, and reproducibility;
- dynamic verification of current FT50/AJG status when journal rankings are used.

Behavioral variables are defined as **empirical research proxies**, not permanent personality classifications or psychological diagnoses.

Detailed treatment arms, outcome-vector schemas, experiment-control logic, unpublished measurement mechanics, and potentially patent-sensitive orchestration remain private pending filing review.

## Patent-first hardening later on 2026-09-16

After identifying that some implementation-level details had already been publicly committed, the project adopted:

**Patent first → public disclosure second.**

The current public branch was then hardened so patent-sensitive mechanisms are described only at a non-enabling high level. Detailed algorithms, state-transition logic, dependency/revocation behavior, containment, evaluation isolation, promotion/demotion mechanisms, experiment-control implementation, technical flowcharts, claim concepts, and unpublished embodiments are maintained in a private patent-readiness workspace.

This hardening **does not erase earlier Git history**. Known relevant historical commits are recorded in [`PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE_LOG.md`](./PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE_LOG.md).
