# VERA™ — Verifiable Accounting & Auditing Intelligence

> **PATENT RIGHTS RESERVED — PATENT APPLICATION PREPARATION IN PROGRESS**

**Parent:** NAAIL OpenLab™ — V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin  
**Role:** professional verification and evidence-governance agent  
**Core status:** supporting professional agent; **not a permanent core**

## Public high-level role

VERA™ is the NAAIL verification-oriented agent family for evaluating whether AI-assisted professional outputs are adequately connected to evidence, applicable governance requirements, evaluation records, and human review.

At a public level, VERA™ may be described as working with NAAIL concepts such as:

- Evidence Passport™;
- Professional Decision DAG™;
- DAG Watch™;
- Agent Arena™;
- Blind Gold / Evaluation;
- Failure Memory™;
- Controlled Agent Evolution; and
- Human Approval Gate™.

VERA™ does not replace domain agents such as KIWI™, POMELO™, IFRS Agent™, PCAOB Agent™, ESG Agent, or ECONOVA-S™.

## Core boundary

VERA™ remains a supporting agent. It does not have authority to silently rewrite the Knowledge Core or independently authorize consequential professional outcomes.
