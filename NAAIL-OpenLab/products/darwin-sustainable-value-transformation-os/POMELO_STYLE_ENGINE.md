# POMELO-Style Engine Contract

Every product engine follows the same governed backbone:

1. **Event ingestion** — ERP/contract/ESG/audit event enters with timestamp and provenance.
2. **Semantic linkage** — event is linked to product, project, supplier, customer, capacity, capital and relevant standards.
3. **Construct Firewall** — facts, accounting constructs, estimates, predictions, causal claims, recommendations and approved actions remain typed separately.
4. **Evidence Passport** — source, version, retrieval/effective date, transformations and unresolved gaps are preserved.
5. **Competing hypotheses** — Co-Scientist generates alternative explanations; no single-story shortcut.
6. **Systems graph** — dependencies, feedback loops, delays, constraints and trade-offs are evaluated.
7. **Counterfactual worlds** — current path is compared with feasible alternatives, including do-nothing.
8. **Falsification / challenger** — independent challenge attempts to break the leading recommendation.
9. **Value gate** — relevant economic / sustainability / assurance objective is calculated with anti-double-counting controls.
10. **Human Approval Gate** — material actions require named human ownership and explicit approval.
11. **Outcome attribution** — expected vs actual outcome is decomposed into decision effect and external effects.
12. **Learning / failure memory** — validated outcomes update governed reference classes, not raw self-modifying production logic.

This public specification is non-enabling. Proprietary algorithms, prompts, private benchmarks, credentials and licensed IFRS content remain private.
