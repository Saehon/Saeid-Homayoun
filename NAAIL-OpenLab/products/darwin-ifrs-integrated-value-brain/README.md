# DARWIN IFRS Integrated Value Brain™

**Status:** Public, non-enabling product architecture / research prototype specification  
**Umbrella:** NAAIL OpenLab™  
**North Star:** **Maximum Integrated Sustainable Economic Value**  
**Decision timing:** **Before commitment**, during execution, and after outcome realization.

## Product thesis

DARWIN IFRS Integrated Value Brain™ is an ERP-connected decision layer that converts IFRS-triggered business events into auditable economic-value and sustainability-value decisions before a company commits resources.

It is not an ERP replacement, an IFRS chatbot, an ESG reporting tool, or an EVA calculator.

The design combines:

- IFRS Accounting Brain™;
- Internal Audit Assurance Brain™;
- Cost Truth Engine™;
- Management Accounting Decision Engine™;
- Economic Value Ledger™;
- IFRS Value Genome™;
- Economic Value Impact (EVI) / EVA;
- Sustainability Value Added (SVA);
- Double Materiality Filter;
- Six-Capital Transformation Graph™;
- Systems Thinking Engine™;
- Opportunity Cost Engine™;
- Counterfactual & Scenario Engine™;
- DARWIN MetaBrain™;
- CEO-100 Reference Class;
- Co-Scientist Hypothesis Engine;
- Science Discovery;
- AlphaEvolve-style optimization;
- Falsification / Challenge Agent;
- Human Approval Gate™;
- Outcome & Learning Loop.

## Core question

> **Given an IFRS-relevant business event, which compliant and controllable alternative creates the greatest integrated sustainable economic value relative to the best available alternative?**

## Operating modes

1. **PREVENT** — evaluate PO, SO, contract, lease, CapEx and M&A before commitment.
2. **OPTIMIZE** — find the best alternative using relevant cost, opportunity cost, capital charge, sustainability effects and system constraints.
3. **LEARN** — compare expected vs realized value, attribute outcomes and update governed decision memory.
