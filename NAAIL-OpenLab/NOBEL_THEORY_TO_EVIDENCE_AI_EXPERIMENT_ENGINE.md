## Role

The **NAAIL Nobel Theory-to-Evidence & AI Experiment Engine™** is a cross-cutting governed research capability serving all NAAIL domains. It is **not a third core**, does not replace the canonical professional agents, and is **not a CCCMP-specific module**.

Its public scientific objective is:

**Theory → Construct → Variable → Rights-cleared Data → AI Experiment → Digital Twin → Causal Test → Replication → FT50/AJG Benchmark → Evidence Passport™ → Human Gate™**

## Theory families

The Engine may organize foundational theory families associated with major contributions in:

- judgment under uncertainty, prospect theory, framing, anchoring and loss aversion;
- experimental economics and market experiments;
- behavioral economics and mental accounting;
- asset pricing, market efficiency and predictability;
- portfolio theory, corporate finance and CAPM;
- risk and option valuation;
- information asymmetry, signaling and screening;
- contracts, incentives and agency;
- transaction costs, institutions and governance;
- mechanism design, auctions and bidding;
- causal inference and natural experiments;
- institutions and economic development;
- innovation, growth, AI adoption and productivity.

The registry is intended to translate theory into research constructs, hypotheses, measurable variables, experiments and falsification tests—not biographies.

## Governed data/evidence scope

Candidate public or legally reusable source families include:

- **Accounting / auditing:** SEC EDGAR, CompanyFacts/XBRL, public filings, SEC/PCAOB public enforcement/inspection material, public textual-analysis resources and academic/non-commercial resources where terms permit;
- **Finance:** Fama–French, Shiller public series, investor-sentiment series, Damodaran, SCF, FRED/ALFRED;
- **Economics / management:** World Bank, governance indicators, BEA/BLS/Census, OECD public-access data where permitted, Our World in Data;
- **Behavioral / consumer:** UCI public datasets, legally reusable OSF datasets, public investor-confidence data and GDELT-derived measures;
- **Research:** OpenAlex, SSRN metadata/abstracts, CORE, DOAJ, RePEc and institutional repositories.

Every source remains subject to license/access, provenance, versioning and Evidence Passport™ governance. Public access does not imply unrestricted copying or redistribution.

## Technology / replication registry

Candidate open-source technology and replication resources include HARK, Mesa, ABIDES, FinRL, DoWhy, EconML, verified open financial-language models, Hugging Face Transformers, pandas-datareader, NetworkX, Statsmodels and suitable behavioral-experiment repositories after license and quality review.

A registry entry does **not** mean a dependency is installed, executed, endorsed, authoritative, or license-cleared for every use.

## Behavioral text research

The Engine may evaluate observable textual/decision constructs such as tone, uncertainty, litigiousness, constraining language, modal language, readability, confidence, risk language, causal language, forward-looking language, numerical specificity, self/collective language, semantic similarity, novelty, managerial-overconfidence proxies, auditor-skepticism proxies and AI reliance/override language.

These are empirical constructs, not psychological diagnoses.

## Human–AI experimental research

The public research scope includes controlled human-only, human+AI, explanation, confidence, critic, critic–defender, multi-agent debate, Evidence Passport™, contradictory-evidence and Human Approval conditions.

Potential manipulated dimensions include framing, anchoring, information order, AI confidence/accuracy, uncertainty, contradictory evidence, task complexity, time pressure, incentives and management pressure.

Potential outcomes include decision accuracy, confidence/calibration, decision time, evidence acquisition, behavioral-bias proxies, algorithm aversion/appreciation, AI reliance/override, skepticism, revision, advice taking and final economic outcomes.

Detailed treatment orchestration, outcome schemas and unpublished experimental-control mechanisms are withheld under patent hold.

## Business-school Digital Twins

## FT50 / AJG research gate

When journal status is used, NAAIL must dynamically verify the current **Financial Times FT50** list and **Chartered ABS Academic Journal Guide** edition/rating rather than hard-code rankings. Lawful working-paper/open-access versions should be preferred. Copyrighted papers default to metadata + abstract + citation + URL unless their license permits fuller reuse.

**Theory → Literature Evidence → Causal DAG → Hypotheses → Power Analysis → Pre-registration → Data → Variable DNA™ → Randomization/Identification → AI Treatment → Behavioral Measures → Main Test → Robustness → Falsification → Replication/OOS → FT50/AJG Benchmark → Evidence Passport™ → Human Gate™.**
