# NAAIL OpenLab™ — Public Preview

**Published public preview — 14 September 2026**  
**GitHub publication state — PUBLIC**  
**Google Drive working mirror — SYNCHRONIZED**

NAAIL OpenLab™ is an independent, evidence-governed AI research and education platform for Accounting, Auditing, Assurance, Finance, Economics, Sustainability, and professional AI evaluation.

> **Models generate. Agents debate. Code tests. Evidence decides. Students challenge. Humans approve.**

## Why this public preview exists

This page gives universities, audit firms, researchers, students, technology partners, and prospective collaborators one concise entry point into the current NAAIL public architecture.

The public preview intentionally exposes research-safe interfaces, synthetic Digital Twin cases, evaluation logic, reproducibility rules, education workflows, and provider-neutral architecture while keeping proprietary orchestration, credentials, private benchmarks, restricted data, partner-confidential material, and patent-sensitive implementation outside the public repository.

## Current public pillars

### 1. Audit Digital Twin Laboratory
Synthetic **Client XYZ** cases provide a frozen environment for testing professional judgment, AI agents, evidence use, and human oversight.

Initial domains:
- Revenue Recognition & Cut-off
- Goodwill Impairment
- ICFR / Control Deficiency

Every material conclusion remains subject to a **Human Gate**.

### 2. Student Agent Academy™
A university–industry learning model prepares students to work with AI-enabled audit and assurance workflows before entering professional practice.

Core flow:

`Industry Partner → Educational Proxy Agent → Synthetic Digital Twin → Student Challenge → Evidence-Governed Evaluation → Human Gate → Aggregate Partner Insight`

The model is designed so an industry partner can contribute an approved learning objective, challenge brief, guest session, synthetic scenario, or educational proxy-agent specification without disclosing proprietary production systems or confidential client data.

## Big Four / industry proposition

NAAIL is not positioned as a replacement for proprietary firm platforms. The stronger proposition is a **neutral research, education, Digital Twin, benchmarking, evidence-governance, and AI-validation layer** that can coexist with existing firm technology.

A first partnership pilot is designed around:
- one university course;
- one participating industry partner;
- approximately 20–40 master-level students;
- 4–6 weeks;
- three synthetic Digital Twin cases;
- one Educational Proxy Agent;
- student and instructor workspaces;
- aggregate anonymized partner reporting;
- pre/post learning evaluation;
- mandatory Human Gate.

Individual student information is not shared with partners by default. Recruitment use requires explicit student consent plus appropriate institutional/privacy approval. NAAIL student scores are not validated for automated hiring decisions.

## Evaluation framework

Professional metrics:
- **RPA** — Risk–Procedure Alignment
- **AA** — Assertion Alignment
- **EG** — Evidence Grounding
- **PS** — Professional Skepticism
- **DS** — Documentation Sufficiency
- **DIST** — Decision Integrity / Stability

Student AI-readiness metrics:
- **AIV** — AI Verification
- **CER** — Contradictory Evidence Recognition
- **HOR** — Human Override Reasoning
- **ESC** — Escalation Judgment

These are research and educational constructs and should not be treated as validated professional certification or employment-selection instruments.

## Public / private boundary

**Public:** research-safe architecture, synthetic benchmark descriptions, selected code, educational specifications, marketplace scaffolding, replication protocols, citation metadata, and reproducibility artifacts.

**Private:** proprietary orchestration/control logic, provider credentials, private prompts, unpublished methods, private benchmark extensions, licensed/restricted data, partner-confidential content, individual recruitment data, patent-candidate mechanisms, and commercial strategy.
