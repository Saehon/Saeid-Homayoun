## Release decision

The **NAAIL Innovation & Entrepreneurship Evidence Layer™** is publicly registered as a cross-cutting governed supporting capability under the existing two-core NAAIL architecture.

It does **not** create a third core and is **not CCCMP-specific**.

## Public scope

The public layer identifies high-level research capability across:

- innovation and entrepreneurship theory;
- patents/IP and technological innovation;
- entrepreneurship and firm dynamics;
- science-to-innovation/commercialization;
- digital/open-source innovation;
- economic complexity and opportunity research;
- startup/founder evidence;
- innovation networks;
- behavioral entrepreneurship and human–AI decisions;
- Startup Digital Twins;
- entrepreneurial finance;
- causal inference, replication and falsification;
- FT50/AJG research benchmarking;
- Evidence Passport™ and Human Gate™ governance.

The public research objective is:

**Scientific Discovery → Invention → Patent → Entrepreneurship → Financing → Commercialization → Innovation → Scaling → Productivity → Firm / Market / Social Value**
