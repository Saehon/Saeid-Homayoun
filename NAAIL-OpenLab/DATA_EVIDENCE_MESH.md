# NAAIL Data & Evidence Mesh™

> **PATENT RIGHTS RESERVED — PATENT APPLICATION PREPARATION IN PROGRESS**

**Umbrella:** NAAIL OpenLab™ — V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin  
**Status:** governed supporting layer; **not a third core**

## Public high-level description

NAAIL Data & Evidence Mesh™ is the public name for a governed evidence-connection layer that may reference or retrieve rights-cleared/public information for NAAIL research and Digital Twin simulations while preserving source provenance, applicable terms, versioning, and human review.

Priority public/reference sources include:

- SEC EDGAR / XBRL;
- FRED / ALFRED;
- Fama–French Data Library;
- World Bank;
- Our World in Data CO₂ / Energy;
- OpenAlex;
- OpenSanctions where applicable to non-commercial research; and
- optional OpenBB connectors subject to underlying-provider terms.

The public objective remains:

**Real Data → Evidence Passport™ → Multi-Agent Analysis → Digital Twin Simulation → Decision → Consequence → Verification → Human Approval.**

## Storage and rights principle

Large third-party datasets, credentials, and restricted/licensed material should not be stored in this public GitHub repository by default. NAAIL should prefer source links, APIs/connectors, small rights-cleared fixtures, source identifiers, and provenance metadata.

Third-party sources retain their own licenses, data rights, authoritative status, and source terms.

## Patent hold

Detailed connector contracts, provenance schemas, dependency behavior, validation/reconciliation logic, evidence-routing rules, Evidence Passport internals, technical interfaces, implementation code, and unpublished embodiments are maintained privately pending patent filing review.

Some implementation-level information was already published in Git history before adoption of the patent-first policy. See [`PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE_LOG.md`](./PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE_LOG.md).

## Two-core boundary

NAAIL continues to preserve exactly two permanent cores: **Knowledge Core™** and **Technology Core™**. The Data & Evidence Mesh™ remains a supporting layer and does not change that architecture.

## Independence

NAAIL OpenLab™ is independent. Inclusion of a source or connector does not imply affiliation, endorsement, certification, partnership, unrestricted redistribution rights, or that a named source was actually used in any particular experiment.
