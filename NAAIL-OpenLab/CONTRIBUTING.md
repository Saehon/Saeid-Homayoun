# Contributing to NAAIL OpenLab™

NAAIL OpenLab is a non-commercial research and education project with active intellectual-property, patent, licensing, and research-integrity considerations.

## What is welcome now
- bug reports;
- documentation corrections;
- reproducibility reports;
- benchmark suggestions;
- synthetic-case proposals;
- literature/evidence references;
- security disclosures through the designated private channel when available.

## Code contributions
Do **not** submit substantial code, agent logic, orchestration methods, proprietary datasets, or patent-sensitive technical methods until a contributor-IP/CLA process has been formally adopted for NAAIL OpenLab.

This protects both contributors and the project by avoiding ambiguity over authorship, copyright, patent rights, and future research/commercial licensing.

## Contribution rules
1. Do not submit confidential, personal, client, student, employer, or restricted information.
2. Do not submit copyrighted standards/articles/course packs or licensed datasets without redistribution rights.
3. Do not copy proprietary Big Four or vendor code, prompts, screenshots, internal methods, or protected product materials.
4. Synthetic examples must be clearly marked synthetic.
5. Scientific claims must include reproducible evidence and limitations.
6. AI-generated contributions remain the submitter's responsibility and must be reviewed for accuracy, originality, rights, and security.
7. Do not add secrets, tokens, passwords, credentials, or private URLs.

## Research contribution template
A research proposal should specify:
- research question;
- theory/literature basis;
- synthetic/public/licensed data source;
- expected variables/measures;
- evaluation/falsification plan;
- reproducibility artifacts;
- IP/rights status.

## Attribution
Material accepted contributions may be acknowledged or added to release-specific authorship/citation metadata according to documented contribution and authorship criteria. Contribution does not automatically grant commercial-use, trademark, patent, or relicensing rights.
