# Microsoft FY2026 POC — Evidence Snapshot

**Company:** Microsoft Corporation (NASDAQ: MSFT)  
**Fiscal year end:** June 30, 2026  
**10-K filing date:** July 29, 2026  
**SEC accession:** 0001193125-26-323660  
**Auditor:** Deloitte & Touche LLP

## Public filing evidence
- Deloitte expressed an unqualified opinion on Microsoft's internal control over financial reporting as of June 30, 2026.
- The audit report identified two critical audit matters:
  1. Revenue Recognition.
  2. Income Taxes — Uncertain Tax Positions.
- Microsoft describes significant judgment in revenue recognition for arrangements containing multiple products/services and cloud components.
- Microsoft disclosed unresolved IRS transfer-pricing matters and $28.9 billion of proposed additional tax, plus penalties and interest, for tax years 2004–2013.
- The POC uses these public disclosures only to demonstrate a transparent risk-screening architecture.
