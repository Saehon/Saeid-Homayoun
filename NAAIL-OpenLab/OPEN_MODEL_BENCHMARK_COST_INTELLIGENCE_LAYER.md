**Status:** `PATENT_HOLD_NON_ENABLING`  
**Constitutional placement:** cross-cutting supporting layer under the frozen two-core NAAIL architecture.

This layer supports evidence-governed comparison of AI models and workflows using replaceable benchmark, pricing, latency, throughput, context, reliability and task-fit evidence. It is not a permanent core.

Candidate reference/technology families may include HELM, LiveBench, Hugging Face benchmark/leaderboard resources, public Arena-style preference data, LiteLLM pricing metadata, MLPerf, OpenCost, OpenTelemetry GenAI conventions, Microsoft FinOps Toolkit and open observability/gateway tooling, subject to current license, maintenance, provenance, quality and execution verification.

Public rules:

- benchmark score ≠ absolute intelligence;
- preference ranking ≠ scientific truth;
- public benchmark data ≠ unrestricted redistribution;
- pricing must be timestamped and source-controlled;
- registry entry ≠ installed/executed dependency;
- cheaper model ≠ automatically preferred;
- higher benchmark score ≠ automatically preferred;
- model/workflow choice must satisfy required quality, evidence, reliability, reproducibility and governance thresholds.

The layer supports the public concept of a **NAAIL Cost-to-Value Frontier™** and governed model-routing research. Detailed aggregation, weighting, routing and optimization logic remains private pending filing review.

This layer serves the NAAIL Management Accounting & AI Cost Intelligence Layer™, Digital Twins, scientific experiments, education and specialist agents without modifying the Stable Knowledge Core™.
