## Purpose

The Value Expansion Fabric increases the practical, educational, research, and professional value of NAAIL through modular supporting capabilities that remain governed by provenance, licensing, reproducibility, and human approval.

At a high level, the expansion covers:

- broader accounting, SEC/XBRL, textual-analysis, benchmarking, enforcement, and forensic evidence sources;
- finance, economics, macroeconomic, capital-market, climate, ESG, and scholarly-data sources;
- open-source ERP and synthetic-company simulation resources;
- professional accounting, audit, assurance, governance, sustainability, finance, fraud, and risk knowledge;
- research-discovery resources including lawful open-access metadata and working-paper repositories;
- legally accessible Big Four and professional-body guidance;
- original NAAIL educational question banks and competency-aligned simulations;
- Knowledge RAG / GraphRAG / KAG research and education support;
- license/access classification and evidence-provenance governance;
- Business-School Digital Twins spanning accounting, audit, IFRS, internal audit, ICFR, forensic/fraud, ERP, management accounting, finance, capital markets, ESG/sustainability, governance, economics, and professional education.

## Rights and source-governance rule

The expansion follows these permanent rules:

- **free access does not mean free redistribution**;
- third-party data, standards, papers, software, guidance, and educational materials retain their original rights and terms;
- large third-party datasets and credentials are not committed to GitHub by default;
- source use is limited to permitted retrieval, metadata, citation, transformation, linking, or ingestion modes;
- proprietary examination banks and confidential live examination questions are excluded;
- confidential real-company ERP data is excluded;
- every production/research use remains subject to evidence provenance, license/access review, reproducibility controls, and Human Approval Gate™ where applicable.
