## Canonical scientific protocol

Across NAAIL research:

1. Generate competing hypotheses using multi-agent scientific reasoning.
2. Critique, rank, and improve hypotheses before empirical testing.
3. Convert surviving hypotheses into reproducible empirical designs with real data, explicit variables, identification strategies, code, metrics, and robustness tests.
4. Compare and evolve competing models, measures, prompts, algorithms, and specifications using frozen scientific fitness criteria rather than p-value optimization.
5. Search for latent structures and mechanisms in high-dimensional accounting, audit, financial, and sustainability data.
6. Preserve a Chain-of-Evidence from research question through literature, data, measurement, model, identification, results, robustness, replication, interpretation, and human decision.
7. Require literature validation, adversarial review, falsification, reproducibility, and human approval before a discovery claim.

## Common reproducibility standard

Flagship empirical projects should progressively converge on:

```text
project/
├── README.md
├── CITATION.cff
├── LICENSE / rights notice
├── data_manifest/
├── schemas/
├── src/
├── tests/
├── notebooks/
├── configs/
├── results/
└── run_manifest.json
```

Each study should document data provenance, transformations, exclusions, variables, model specifications, identification assumptions, evaluation metrics, robustness and falsification tests, software environment, random seeds where relevant, known limitations, and release rights.

## Portfolio governance

NAAIL OpenLab separates:

- **Public research assets** — intentionally released documentation, notebooks, synthetic examples, code, and replication artifacts.
- **Proprietary research systems** — private code, patent-sensitive architecture, evaluation assets, restricted datasets, and pre-commercial systems.
- **Third-party assets** — software, data, standards, papers, models, and repositories governed by their original rights and licenses.

No private repository should be copied into the public hub merely for convenience.
