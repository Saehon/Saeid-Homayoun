## Research methodology

Across NAAIL OpenLab research, the preferred scientific workflow is:

1. Generate competing hypotheses using multi-agent scientific reasoning.
2. Critique, rank, and improve hypotheses before empirical testing.
3. Convert surviving hypotheses into reproducible empirical designs with real data, explicit variables, identification strategies, code, metrics, and robustness tests.
4. Compare and evolve competing models, measures, prompts, algorithms, and specifications using frozen scientific fitness criteria rather than p-value optimization.
5. Search for latent structures and hidden mechanisms in high-dimensional accounting, audit, financial, and sustainability data.
6. Preserve a Chain-of-Evidence from research question through literature, data, measurement, model, identification, results, robustness, replication, interpretation, and human decision.
7. Require literature validation, adversarial review, falsification, reproducibility, and human approval before any discovery claim.

## Portfolio governance

NAAIL OpenLab follows a clear separation between:

- **Public research assets** — openly visible repositories, documentation, notebooks, demonstrations, and reproducibility material.
- **Proprietary research systems** — source code, architecture, confidential evaluation assets, patent-sensitive components, and pre-commercial systems that remain private.
- **Third-party repositories or forks** — used as research dependencies, benchmarks, inspiration, or reproducibility infrastructure; inclusion in this registry does not imply authorship of upstream code.
