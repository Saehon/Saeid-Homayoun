# Management Science paper — NAAIL RiskOS

**Title:** *Who Assures the Agents? Evidence-Governed Multi-Agent Architectures for Audit Risk*

This folder contains the version-controlled manuscript source for the NAAIL RiskOS research paper.

## Core model

`AR = IR × CR × DR`

The research contribution is not a new multiplicative audit-risk factor. NAAIL RiskOS treats agent assurance as governance of the inputs and decisions surrounding IR, CR, and DR through Evidence Passports, construct-validity gates, independent challenge/falsification, and Human Gate approval.

## Current research-prototype status

- Microsoft FY2026 is the bounded proof-of-concept / Golden Anchor.
- Risk indices are illustrative and uncalibrated.
- Production approval: **NO**.
- Independent scientific replication: **PENDING**.
- Missing evidence is recorded as a gap, not as low risk.
- The paper distinguishes demonstrated artifacts from hypotheses and future empirical tests.
