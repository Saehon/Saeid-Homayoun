## Fixed architecture: exactly two permanent cores

NAAIL preserves exactly two permanent cores:

1. **Stable Knowledge Core™** — governed theory, standards, professional knowledge, evidence semantics, ontologies, research evidence, causal structures, behavioral constructs, and validated knowledge objects.
2. **Replaceable Technology Core™** — replaceable models, tools, agent runtimes, RAG/GraphRAG/KAG, simulation and experimental technology, analytics, connectors, databases, evaluators, and execution infrastructure.

All other elements remain supporting capabilities, including:

**KIWI™ · POMELO™ · VERA™ · IFRS Agent™ · PCAOB Agent™ · ESG Agent · ECONOVA-S™ · Evidence Passport™ · Professional Decision DAG™ · DAG Watch™ · Agent Arena™ · Failure Memory™ · Controlled Agent Evolution · Decision–Consequence Engine™ · Professional Judgment Passport™ · Human Approval Gate™ · NAAIL Data & Evidence Mesh™**.

The **NAAIL Nobel Theory-to-Evidence & AI Experiment Engine™**, **NAAIL Value Expansion Fabric™**, **NAAIL Behavioral Decision Science & Human–AI Experimentation Layer™**, and **CCCMP™ — Project Cost, Contract & Claims Management Programme** are governed supporting capabilities. They do **not** create a third core, alter the canonical agent set, or change the validated public checkpoint.

## NAAIL Nobel Theory-to-Evidence & AI Experiment Engine™

The **[Nobel Theory-to-Evidence & AI Experiment Engine™](./NOBEL_THEORY_TO_EVIDENCE_AI_EXPERIMENT_ENGINE.md)** is a cross-cutting research layer serving **Accounting + Auditing + Finance + Economics + Management + Governance + Sustainability + Behavioral Science + Education + Research**.

It is **not a third core** and is **not CCCMP-specific**.

Its public scientific path is:

**Theory → Construct → Variable → Rights-cleared Data → AI Experiment → Digital Twin → Causal Test → Replication → FT50/AJG Benchmark → Evidence Passport™ → Human Gate™**

Public theory families include foundational work in behavioral judgment, experimental economics, behavioral economics, asset pricing, portfolio/corporate finance, option/risk theory, information asymmetry, contracts/incentives, transaction costs/governance, mechanism design, auctions, causal inference, institutions/development, and innovation/growth/AI productivity.

NAAIL does not treat a theory name as measurement. Research objects should define constructs, observable variables, hypotheses, identification/randomization, falsification, replication, evidence provenance and human approval.

Detailed theory schemas, experiment orchestration, connector/provenance contracts, unpublished measurement logic and technical implementation remain private under patent hold.

## NAAIL Value Expansion Fabric™

The **[Value Expansion Fabric](./VALUE_EXPANSION_FABRIC.md)** increases NAAIL's research, education, professional-simulation, and evidence value without changing the V2026.3 model.

At a high level it expands six connected capability families:

1. **Data & Evidence** — accounting/SEC, macro/finance, ESG/climate, misconduct/litigation/forensic, scholarly and benchmarking source families.
2. **Professional Knowledge** — IFRS/accounting, audit/assurance, sustainability, governance, controls, fraud, finance, risk and professional-body knowledge.
3. **ERP & Synthetic Companies** — open-source ERP evaluation plus rights-cleared synthetic business-school company environments.
4. **Research Co-Scientist Evidence** — lawful open-research discovery, metadata, citations and reproducible evidence workflows.
5. **Professional Education** — original NAAIL question banks, competency-aligned scenarios and evidence-grounded professional-judgment exercises.
6. **Business-School Digital Twins** — accounting, audit, IFRS, internal audit, ICFR, forensic/fraud, ERP, management accounting, finance, capital markets, ESG/sustainability, governance, economics and professional education.

All source use is subject to **license/access classification, provenance, citation, Evidence Passport™ governance, reproducibility, and human approval**. Free access is not treated as permission to copy or redistribute.

### Research and education

NAAIL connects business-school education, dynamically verified FT50/AJG research context, authoritative professional standards, professional competencies, public/rights-cleared evidence, Digital Twins, behavioral experimentation, theory-to-evidence research, project/capital-programme research, sustainability, and accountable human judgment.

At a high level, NAAIL may use governed public/reference sources such as **SEC EDGAR/XBRL, FRED/ALFRED, Fama–French, Shiller public datasets, World Bank, Our World in Data, OpenAlex, public enforcement/regulatory evidence, lawful behavioral/consumer datasets, public procurement/project source families, and optional verified connectors**.
