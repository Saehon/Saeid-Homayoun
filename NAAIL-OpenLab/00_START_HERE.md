**NAAIL OpenLab™ — V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin** is the fixed umbrella architecture.

## Three questions a visitor should answer in under one minute

### What is NAAIL?

A global evidence-governed platform connecting **business-school learning, FT50/AJG research, authoritative standards, professional competencies, public/licensed evidence, Digital Twins, AI agents, sustainability and human judgment**.

It is positioned as an **Evidence-Governed Multi-Agent Business School Digital Twin for Education, Research, Professional Simulation, and Verifiable Human–AI Judgment**—not merely an AI tutor or chatbot.

### What can I run or learn?

Accounting, audit, CAM/KAM, IFRS, PCAOB/ISA-oriented assurance, internal audit, ICFR, forensic accounting, finance/investment, management accounting, ESG/sustainability, ERP/business-process, company, regulator, capital-market, economics, governance and scientific-discovery simulations. The public validated execution checkpoint remains Prototype 003; other modules carry explicit maturity/execution labels.

The new Decision–Consequence Engine™ provides a deterministic research/education prototype in which student decisions can change later synthetic Digital Twin states. Its transition magnitudes are pedagogical assumptions, not real-world causal estimates.

### What supports every serious simulation?

**Learning objective + directly relevant FT50/AJG evidence or declared gap + authoritative standard/regulation + professional competency mapping + data provenance + Digital Twin + AI execution status + Evidence Passport™ + Professional Decision DAG™ + Decision–Consequence ledger + assessment + Professional Judgment Passport™ + People/Planet/Society/Sustainable-Profit reflection + Human Approval Gate™.**

## Research, standards and professional ecosystem

NAAIL separates four roles before AI enters a simulation:

1. **Business-school education:** AACSB/institutional learning goals and experiential-learning design; EFMD/EQUIS/AMBA may be alignment references where applicable, without implying accreditation.
2. **Research:** FT50 and Chartered ABS AJG 4*/4 evidence where directly relevant; lower-tier/other peer-reviewed evidence or an explicit evidence-gap label where needed.
3. **Authoritative professional sources:** IFRS Foundation/IASB/ISSB, EFRAG/ESRS/EU, PCAOB, IAASB, IESBA, SEC, GRI/GSSB, IIA and other jurisdictional authorities as applicable.
4. **Professional competency mapping:** AICPA/CPA, CIMA/CGMA, ACCA, IMA/CMA/SMA, IIA/CIA/CRMA, ACFE/CFE, CFA Institute/CFA, ISACA/CISA, GARP/FRM and related bodies.

Research never overrides an applicable authoritative standard; professional-body alignment never equals certification; naming an organization never implies endorsement or partnership.
