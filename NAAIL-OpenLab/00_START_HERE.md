## What is NAAIL?

NAAIL is an independent evidence-governed research and education platform connecting **business-school learning, research, professional standards, public/rights-cleared evidence, Digital Twins, AI agents, behavioral experimentation, sustainability, verification, and accountable human judgment**.

It is not positioned merely as an AI tutor or chatbot.

## Behavioral decision science — without architecture drift

The Behavioral Decision Science & Human–AI Experimentation Layer adds a governed research capability spanning behavioral economics, finance, accounting, auditing, management, organizational/consumer behavior, professional skepticism, evidence search, human–AI reliance/override, trust calibration and behavioral text analytics.

The **Knowledge Core™** governs behavioral theory, literature, construct definitions, validated measures and research evidence. The **Technology Core™** supplies replaceable experimental execution, randomization, analytics, AI-agent treatment tooling, simulation, reproducibility and logging.

Behavioral measures are treated as **empirical proxies**, not permanent personality classifications or psychological diagnosis. Studies should use current research-quality gates, including verification of current FT50/AJG status where journal rankings are referenced, credible identification, preregistration/power analysis where appropriate, robustness, replication, falsification, Evidence Passport™ governance and human approval.

Detailed treatment arms, outcome-vector implementation, behavioral-passport internals, experimental orchestration and unpublished research-control mechanics remain private under patent hold.
