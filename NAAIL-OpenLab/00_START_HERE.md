## Fastest technical review

Start with the **AAR Corp CAM unit test**. It is intentionally narrow and reproducible: one company, 10 CAM observations, 2020–2024, two CAMs per year.

The frozen 2024 transition is:

```text
Revenue -> EXIT
Acquired Intangibles / Business Combination -> ENTRY
Inventory -> PERSIST
CAM_COUNT -> unchanged at 2
CARS -> 0.667
```

The case is used for measurement development and falsification—not population inference. It includes frozen RPA/EDS/SIS/SQI/CIIS/CARS results, regression tests, an exact year-level sign test, and robustness checks.

**Research identity:** Accounting & Audit AI · Agentic AI · Scientific Discovery · Financial/Economic Data Science.

The public repository contains research-safe material. Proprietary POMELO/VERA implementation details, restricted data, private benchmarks, and patent-sensitive mechanisms remain outside the public release.
