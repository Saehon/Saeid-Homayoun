# NAAIL Behavioral Decision Science & Human–AI Experimentation Layer™

> **PATENT RIGHTS RESERVED — PATENT APPLICATION PREPARATION IN PROGRESS**

**Public status:** `PATENT_HOLD_NON_ENABLING`

This is a governed supporting research layer under **NAAIL OpenLab™ — V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin**. It does **not** create a third permanent core and does not alter the canonical architecture.

## Fixed architecture

NAAIL continues to preserve exactly two permanent cores:

1. **Knowledge Core™**
2. **Technology Core™**

The Behavioral Decision Science & Human–AI Experimentation Layer™ spans the two cores through governed interfaces:

- the **Knowledge Core™** governs behavioral theory, constructs, research evidence, literature mappings, validated measures, standards, research protocols, and approved semantic definitions;
- the **Technology Core™** provides replaceable experiment execution, randomization, behavioral/text analytics, AI-agent treatments, simulation tooling, logging, reproducibility tooling, and data connectors.

No new permanent core is introduced.

## Public research scope

The layer expands NAAIL research and education into:

- behavioral economics;
- behavioral finance;
- behavioral accounting;
- behavioral auditing;
- management decision-making;
- consumer and organizational behavior;
- professional skepticism and evidence search;
- human–AI trust, reliance, override and judgment revision;
- behavioral text analytics;
- causal and experimental research using Digital Twins.

Behavioral measures are treated as **empirical research proxies**, not permanent personality classifications or psychological diagnoses.

## Experimental domains

High-level experimental domains include accounting, auditing, finance, economics, management, governance, and ESG/sustainability.

Studies may compare human-only and AI-assisted decision conditions, evaluate how evidence presentation affects professional judgment, and examine when AI mitigates or amplifies decision biases. Detailed treatment structures, measurement logic, experimental control designs, and unpublished implementation specifications are maintained privately pending patent review.

## Evidence and data governance

Authorized research data should route through existing NAAIL governance, including:

**NAAIL Data & Evidence Mesh™ → Evidence Passport™ → governed behavioral measurement → experiment/simulation → verification → Human Approval Gate™**.

Potential public/reference data families include SEC EDGAR/XBRL, macroeconomic and market data, textual-analysis resources, open research metadata, and legally reusable behavioral datasets. Source listing does not imply unrestricted ingestion, redistribution, or execution. Each source remains subject to its own license, terms, provenance and access conditions.

## Research-quality gate

The layer adopts a **Top-Journal Research Design Gate** at a high level:

- verify current FT50 and Chartered ABS/AJG status before journal-ranking claims;
- ground behavioral mechanisms in current research;
- prefer credible causal identification where appropriate;
- use preregistration, power analysis, manipulation/attention checks, treatment-integrity checks, robustness, replication and external validation as appropriate;
- preserve Evidence Passport™ provenance and reproducibility records;
- require human approval for consequential research/publishing decisions.

Journal rankings are not hard-coded as permanent truth and must be re-verified when used.

## Human–AI judgment research

The layer supports research questions such as:

> When, why and for whom does AI reduce, preserve or amplify behavioral bias and professional-judgment error?

Potential outputs include research protocols, reproducible analysis packages, Digital Twin experiments, behavioral/text analytics, replication/falsification evidence, and publication-positioning materials.

## Relationship to existing NAAIL capabilities

This layer may work with existing NAAIL agents and services including KIWI™, POMELO™, VERA™, IFRS Agent™, PCAOB Agent™, ESG Agent, ECONOVA-S™, Evidence Passport™, Professional Decision DAG™, Agent Arena™, Decision–Consequence Engine™, Professional Judgment Passport™, and Human Approval Gate™.

Its purpose is to **increase research and educational value without changing the main NAAIL model**.
