**Date:** 2026-09-16

> **PATENT RIGHTS RESERVED — PATENT APPLICATION PREPARATION IN PROGRESS**

This release freezes the NAAIL architecture at exactly two permanent cores:

1. **Stable Knowledge Core™**
2. **Replaceable Technology Core™**

No third permanent core is permitted.

## Constitutional effect

Future domains, datasets, models, vendors, GitHub technologies, agents, programmes, Digital Twins and education/research capabilities must be placed as:

- governed Stable Knowledge;
- replaceable Technology Core implementation;
- modular cross-cutting layer; or
- specialist agent/programme.
