## Core principle

Every serious NAAIL simulation should connect:

```text
Business-School Learning Objective
        ↓
FT50 / AJG Research or Explicit Evidence Gap
        ↓
Applicable Authoritative Standard / Regulation
        ↓
Professional-Body Competency Mapping
        ↓
Public / Licensed / Synthetic Evidence
        ↓
NAAIL Digital Twin
        ↓
AI / Agent Condition
        ↓
Student / Professional Challenge
        ↓
Assessment + Evidence Passport™
        ↓
People × Planet × Society × Sustainable Profit
        ↓
Human Gate™
```

Research informs the simulation but does not override applicable authoritative standards. Professional-body alignment is a learning map, not external certification. Referencing an accreditation body, standard setter, regulator, professional body, university or employer never implies endorsement or partnership.

## Academic / professional alignment domains

### Business-school education

AACSB and institutional learning outcomes may be used to frame curriculum renewal, experiential learning, responsible technology use, professional judgment and societal impact. EFMD/EQUIS and AMBA may be considered as additional international quality/alignment contexts where relevant. NAAIL does not claim external accreditation.

### Research

Directly relevant FT50 and Chartered ABS AJG 4*/4 research is preferred for the **Research-Backed™** label. When no suitable top-journal evidence exists, the Evidence Card must disclose the gap rather than force a citation.

### Standard setters / regulators

Depending on the simulation and jurisdiction, official sources may include IFRS Foundation/IASB/ISSB, EFRAG/ESRS/EU, PCAOB, IAASB, IESBA, SEC, GRI/GSSB, The IIA and other jurisdictional authorities.

### Professional bodies / credentials

Public competency mappings may include AICPA/CPA, CIMA/CGMA, ACCA, IMA/CMA/SMA, IIA/CIA/CRMA, ACFE/CFE, CFA Institute/CFA, ISACA/CISA, GARP/FRM and related bodies. Mapping does not imply approval, waiver, equivalence or certification.

## Educational principles

1. Students must distinguish source evidence, research inference, standards requirements and model-generated reasoning.
2. AI output is never authoritative regulatory or professional evidence by itself.
3. Real public data retain source provenance and applicable usage constraints.
4. Synthetic client/Digital Twin evidence must be clearly labeled as synthetic.
5. Reproducibility, professional skepticism, evidence citation and human judgment are part of the learning objective—not optional extras.
6. Major business simulations should consider People, Planet, Society and Sustainable Profit when those dimensions are material.
7. Proposed education scores require validity evidence before high-stakes use and are not employment truth.
8. NAAIL education modules do not imply endorsement, sponsorship, accreditation, certification or partnership by referenced organizations unless explicitly documented.
