## Education-platform contract

Every material NAAIL simulation promoted as Research-Backed™, Standards-Grounded™ or Profession-Aligned™ must record:

1. learning objective;
2. FT50/AJG research support or an explicit evidence gap;
3. applicable authoritative standards/regulation;
4. professional-body competency mapping;
5. evidence/data provenance and rights status;
6. Digital Twin identity and frozen version;
7. actual AI execution status;
8. assessment criteria;
9. People × Planet × Society × Sustainable Profit™ reflection when material;
10. Human Gate™ responsibility.

## Institutional and professional ecosystem

The education architecture may align to public material from AACSB and, where appropriate, EFMD/EQUIS or AMBA as education-quality contexts. Professional and standards mappings may include IFRS Foundation/IASB/ISSB, PCAOB, IAASB, IFAC/IESBA, EFRAG/ESRS, GRI/GSSB, SEC, AICPA/CPA, CIMA/CGMA, ACCA, IMA/CMA/SMA, IIA/CIA/CRMA, ACFE/CFE, CFA Institute/CFA, ISACA/CISA, GARP/FRM, COSO and relevant jurisdictional bodies.

These references identify public standards, competencies, evidence domains or education contexts. They do **not** imply accreditation, certification, endorsement, affiliation or partnership.
