# NAAIL Professional Swarm Academy™

**Status:** v0.2.4 target educational capability  
**Platform:** NAAIL OpenLab™ — V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin  
**Audience:** Business-school students, doctoral researchers, instructors, and professional-training pilots.

## Objective

The NAAIL Professional Swarm Academy™ is a research and teaching environment in which students work with evidence-governed specialist agents inside synthetic Digital Twins. The educational objective is not to teach students to accept AI output; it is to teach them to **form, test, challenge, document, and escalate professional judgments** when AI systems participate in the workflow.

## Supported simulation families

### Audit & assurance

- audit planning and risk assessment;
- revenue recognition and cut-off;
- estimates and impairment;
- ICFR / internal-control deficiency;
- CAM/KAM reasoning through KIWI™;
- PCAOB-style inspection evidence review;
- IFRS accounting judgment;
- fraud/forensic investigation;
- ESG/sustainability assurance.

### Finance / investment banking

- financial-statement and SEC/XBRL analysis;
- DCF and comparable-company valuation;
- M&A case analysis;
- capital-structure and financing analysis;
- equity-research recommendation;
- credit/risk analysis;
- ECONOVA-S™ macro/data-economy analysis;
- investment-committee challenge and review.

### Consulting / risk / governance

- business-problem framing;
- data evidence and analytics;
- alternative recommendation generation;
- implementation risk;
- controls/compliance review;
- board/executive decision simulation.

## Standard learning sequence

```text
1. Case + frozen evidence released
2. Student records independent initial judgment
3. Student chooses / is assigned specialist-agent team
4. Agents retrieve only approved evidence/tools
5. Independent analyses are produced
6. Critic challenges the proposed conclusion
7. Defender responds with evidence
8. Falsifier searches for counter-evidence
9. Student may ACCEPT / MODIFY / REJECT / REQUEST MORE EVIDENCE / ESCALATE
10. Professional Decision DAG™ + Evidence Passport™ are finalized
11. Instructor/Human Gate reviews the case
12. Student receives evidence-linked feedback
```

## Assessment

The Academy inherits the existing NAAIL professional/student metrics:

- **RPA** — Risk–Procedure Alignment;
- **AA** — Assertion Alignment;
- **EG** — Evidence Grounding;
- **PS** — Professional Skepticism;
- **DS** — Documentation Sufficiency;
- **DIST** — Decision Integrity / traceability;
- **AIV** — AI Verification;
- **CER** — Contradictory Evidence Recognition;
- **HOR** — Human Override Reasoning;
- **ESC** — Escalation Judgment.

Suggested additional swarm-specific research metrics:

- **AGD** — Agent Disagreement Detection;
- **ECR** — Evidence Conflict Resolution;
- **SAR** — Specialist-Agent Routing quality;
- **HGR** — Human Gate Resolution quality;
- **COE** — Chain-of-Evidence completeness.

These additional measures are proposed constructs and require formal operationalization, validity testing, and empirical evaluation before publication claims.

## Industry-partner model

A professional firm, bank, consultancy, regulator, or technology company may contribute a learning objective, public challenge brief, guest session, synthetic scenario, or approved educational proxy-agent specification. NAAIL then converts that contribution into a synthetic, evidence-governed exercise.

No partner needs to disclose proprietary production systems or confidential client information.

The default reporting model is aggregate/anonymized. Individual student performance is not disclosed for recruitment or employment use without explicit consent plus applicable institutional and privacy approval.

## Naming / affiliation rule

Public simulations use neutral names such as **Client XYZ**, **Firm Alpha**, **Investment Bank Beta**, or **Industry Partner**. References to Deloitte, EY, KPMG, PwC, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, or similar firms describe public industry context only and do not imply sponsorship, endorsement, certification, affiliation, or equivalence to proprietary systems.

## Research opportunities

The Academy can support controlled experiments comparing:

- no-AI vs single-agent vs sequential-agent vs governed multi-agent conditions;
- novice vs advanced students;
- independent judgment before vs after agent exposure;
- evidence-visible vs evidence-hidden agent recommendations;
- agent consensus vs structured critic–defender disagreement;
- different Human Gate designs;
- domain-specialist routing policies;
- effects on learning, calibration, skepticism, overreliance, documentation quality, and decision revision.

Any research using students requires applicable ethics, consent, privacy, and institutional approvals.

## Release gate

This design is a **target capability**, not a claim of completed institutional deployment or external certification. A pilot becomes validated only after executable adapters, frozen cases, privacy/consent controls, regression tests, reproducible scoring, failure analysis, and Human Gate review are demonstrated.
