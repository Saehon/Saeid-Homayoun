# Public Launch Checklist

- Confirm repository/code license and contributor terms.
- Confirm trademark/name review for NAAIL/OpenLab branding.
- Keep commercial-firm simulations fictional and non-affiliated.
- Audit all third-party assets, datasets, standards, articles, images, and course materials for redistribution rights.
- Add accessibility testing (WCAG) before institutional use.
- Complete GDPR/privacy review before any personal-data processing.
- Complete university research-ethics review where human-subject research is involved.
- Add terms of use and acceptable-use rules before public accounts are enabled.
- Add API-provider terms review before sending data to hosted AI services.
- Add security review before enabling uploads, tools, code execution, or external actions.
- Obtain qualified legal review before a public production launch; this checklist is risk-control guidance, not legal advice.
