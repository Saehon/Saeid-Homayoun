# CCCMP™ — Research, Education & Evidence Governance

> **PATENT RIGHTS RESERVED — PATENT APPLICATION PREPARATION IN PROGRESS**

**Parent:** NAAIL OpenLab™ — V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin  
**Status:** Public high-level governance; implementation-level mechanics withheld.

## 1. Architecture rule

CCCMP™ is a governed specialist programme within NAAIL OpenLab™. It does not create a third permanent core.

- **Knowledge Core™** governs standards, project/accounting knowledge, research evidence, constructs and evidence semantics.
- **Technology Core™** hosts replaceable analytical tools, simulation technology, connectors and execution infrastructure.

## 2. Research-quality rule

Every CCCMP study should distinguish theory, construct measurement, causal identification, prediction and professional judgment.

Where journal status is used, verify the **current** FT50 list and Chartered ABS Academic Journal Guide at the time of the study. Do not rely on stale hard-coded rankings.

High-ranked journals guide theory and method; rankings do not substitute for identification, robustness, replication or construct validity.

## 3. Preferred evidence hierarchy

CCCMP research may combine:

- public/rights-cleared project, procurement, infrastructure, macro, energy, commodity and legal data;
- governed accounting/reporting/sustainability knowledge;
- lawful scholarly metadata and open research;
- synthetic project-company evidence where appropriate;
- behavioral and human–AI experiments;
- reproducible Digital Twin simulations.

Every source remains subject to applicable license and access restrictions. Public access is not unrestricted redistribution permission.

## 4. Behavioral-science boundary

CCCMP may study observable project-decision constructs such as planning fallacy, optimism, anchoring, escalation of commitment, evidence search, AI reliance, AI override and trust calibration.

These are **empirical behavioral proxies**, not permanent psychological or personality classifications.

Human–AI experiments should use appropriate randomization, manipulation checks, preregistration where applicable, power analysis, treatment integrity, reproducibility, replication/falsification and human approval.

## 5. Digital Twin research boundary

CCCMP Digital Twin simulations may model project lifecycle decisions and economic/operational/contractual shocks at a high level. Simulation output is not automatically evidence of real-world causal effectiveness.

Claims about causal effects require suitable empirical identification and validation outside the simulation when appropriate.

## 6. Research / education / industry separation

### Research Mode

For causal inference, behavioral/human–AI experiments, archival work, replication, falsification and research publication.

### Education Mode

For MSc/MBA/PhD and professional-learning exercises in project controls, accounting, contracts, procurement, claims, project finance and professional judgment.

### Industry Mode

Not licensed by the public repository. Commercial use, consulting, enterprise deployment, production integration or monetization requires separate written authorization.

## 7. Evidence and reproducibility

Public-safe CCCMP studies should document:

- source identity and provenance;
- applicable rights/terms;
- retrieval/version information;
- variable/construct definitions;
- transformations;
- analysis code where redistributable;
- robustness and replication steps;
- limitations;
- human approval for consequential outputs.

Detailed Evidence Passport™ schemas, connector logic, contract/claims graph mechanics, entitlement logic and automation remain private under patent hold.

## 8. Independence

References to firms, standard setters, regulators, universities, datasets, professional bodies, journals and open-source projects do not imply endorsement, partnership, certification, accreditation or commercial authorization.
