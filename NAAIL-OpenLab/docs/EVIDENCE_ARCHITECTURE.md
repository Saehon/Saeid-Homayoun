# Evidence Architecture — FT50 + AJG/ABS

NAAIL OpenLab separates professional authority from academic prestige.

A journal ranking is not a professional auditing standard. For a professional audit task, applicable standards and regulation are the primary authority. FT50 and AJG/ABS literature are used for theory, empirical evidence, measurement, pedagogy, benchmark design, and research validation.

## Research evidence gate

Every research hypothesis must pass:

Literature search → relevance screen → FT50/AJG tier tagging → theory extraction → construct/method extraction → contradictory-evidence search → replication/data availability check → evidence synthesis → hypothesis decision.

## Course evidence gate

Every module should have:

- authoritative professional references;
- 2–5 foundational scholarly readings;
- 2–5 recent research readings where appropriate;
- at least one methods/data reading for master/research level;
- explicit learning outcomes linked to readings and assessment;
- an update date and evidence owner.

## Research-agent behavior

Research agents must search across competing explanations. They may not rank a paper solely by journal tier. Journal tier is one signal alongside topical relevance, design quality, identification, data quality, reproducibility, recency, and conflicting evidence.

## Current sources

- Financial Times, “The 50 journals used in FT rankings,” updated 29 April 2026.
- Chartered Association of Business Schools, Academic Journal Guide 2024 methodology. The methodology uses a 4-to-1 rating scale and identifies a small subset of 4-rated journals as Journals of Distinction.

The repository does not redistribute the full AJG guide. Institutions/users should import ranking metadata they are entitled to use.
