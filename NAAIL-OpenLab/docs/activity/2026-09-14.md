## Executive summary

14 September 2026 was a major architecture, engineering, governance, and research-design day for NAAIL OpenLab. The work moved the public project from a general AI-for-business-school concept toward a more explicit **evidence-governed multi-agent Digital Twin and scientific-discovery platform** for Accounting, Auditing, Finance, Economics, sustainability, and professional research.

The validated public software marker remains **v0.2.2 / Audit Digital Twin Prototype 002**. In parallel, the next implementation architecture was frozen as **V2026.3 — Multi-Agent Digital Twin Scientific Discovery Architecture**. The day also included work on repository professionalization, research reproducibility, citation infrastructure, non-commercial research licensing, public/private IP boundaries, future data adapters, educational delivery, and research-agent design.

## 1. NAAIL OpenLab architecture advanced

The project architecture was strengthened around a provider-neutral multi-agent design that can support comparative research rather than dependence on one model vendor.

Key architecture elements consolidated today include:

- governed specialist-agent roles;
- **Audit Digital Twin** experimentation;
- **GraphRAG** and evidence-linked retrieval;
- **A2A + MCP** interoperability concepts;
- explicit **Evidence Passport™** provenance;
- persistent **Professional Decision DAG™** governance;
- mandatory **Human Gate** approval before consequential professional or scientific claims;
- separation of stable knowledge/evidence from replaceable technology components;
- preservation of a public research layer and a private R&D/IP layer.

## 2. Scientific-discovery engine formalized

A research workflow was defined to make NAAIL useful not only for professional task automation but also for rigorous academic discovery.

The target workflow is:

**Research Question → Literature Validation → Competing Hypotheses → Multi-Agent Critique → Reproducible Empirical Design → Computational Discovery → Empirical Test → Adversarial Review → Falsification → Replication → Chain-of-Evidence → Human Gate**

The architecture incorporates research patterns inspired by publicly described scientific-AI approaches, including:

- AI Co-Scientist-style hypothesis generation, critique, ranking, and refinement;
- ERA-style conversion of hypotheses into explicit empirical tests;
- AlphaEvolve-style evaluator-guided comparison and improvement of models, measures, prompts, and specifications;
- AlphaFold/DeepMind-inspired latent-structure reasoning for hidden relationships and representations;
- computational-discovery loops for controlled model and specification search;
- Science One-style traceable **Chain-of-Evidence**;
- dedicated **Critic, Defender, Replicator, and Falsifier** roles.

These concepts are used as research inspirations only. NAAIL OpenLab remains independent and does not claim affiliation with or endorsement by the referenced organizations.

## 4. Evaluation and adversarial-review standard expanded

The project continues to use professional-quality and system-reliability metrics rather than accuracy alone.

Core audit evaluation dimensions include:

- **RPA** — Risk–Procedure Alignment;
- **AA** — Assertion Alignment;
- **EG** — Evidence Grounding;
- **PS** — Professional Skepticism;
- **DS** — Documentation Sufficiency;
- **DIST** — Decision/Inference Stability;
- citation validity and traceability;
- false-positive and false-negative analysis;
- reproducibility;
- adversarial robustness;
- latency and cost;
- human override frequency and rationale.

A key principle reinforced today is that model evolution must not optimize for favorable p-values, preferred conclusions, or benchmark leakage.

## 5. Research-data integration scope defined

Several public or licensed data families were identified as future adapters for NAAIL research studies. These are **integration targets**, not claims that all data have already been ingested into the public repository.

Priority research-data families include:

- SEC / EDGAR / XBRL filings and structured financial data;
- Audit Analytics CAM/KAM and ICFR-related data where licensed and permitted;
- Fama–French factor and asset-pricing datasets;
- Damodaran valuation and corporate-finance datasets;
- public macroeconomic and capital-market datasets;
- ERP/accounting-system data in synthetic, licensed, or institutionally approved research settings;
- FT50 / AJG evidence corpora subject to copyright, access, and licensing rules.

Every future adapter should preserve data provenance, licensing status, variable definitions, transformation logic, and reproducible run manifests.

## 9. IP, licensing, and responsible disclosure

The project’s research/education orientation and public/private boundary were reinforced today.

Current policy direction:

- public assets may include research-safe documentation, synthetic cases, selected code, educational materials, citation metadata, and reproducibility artifacts;
- private R&D should retain patent-sensitive mechanisms, unpublished orchestration logic, licensed/restricted data, private benchmarks, experimental findings, and pre-commercial implementation;
- third-party data, papers, standards, models, and software retain their original licenses and rights;
- commercial use is not licensed unless separately authorized;
- public disclosure should be reviewed carefully before patent-sensitive details are released.

## Research integrity statement

NAAIL OpenLab treats AI output as a research hypothesis or professional work product requiring evidence, testing, critique, reproducibility, and human approval. Statistical significance, model confidence, or agent consensus alone does not constitute scientific discovery or professional assurance.
