## Preliminary patent-search themes

The initial screen confirms that the following broad concepts already have substantial prior art and should not be treated as novel by themselves:

- AI agents and multi-agent systems;
- digital twins and state-transition simulation;
- human-in-the-loop approval;
- AI/model provenance and audit logging;
- agent evaluation and validation;
- AI/agent evolution or self-improvement;
- containerized / policy-gated agent execution;
- AI-supported audit/compliance workflows.
