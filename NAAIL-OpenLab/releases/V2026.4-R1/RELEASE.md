# NAAIL OpenLab™ V2026.4-R1
## Open Research Infrastructure Release

**Release date:** 2026-09-24  
**Release class:** `PUBLIC_RESEARCH_RELEASE`  
**Repository:** `Saehon/Saeid-Homayoun`  
**Scope:** NAAIL OpenLab public research infrastructure, open-agent tooling, reproducible cross-platform research packaging, and evidence-governed workflow documentation.

## Release purpose

V2026.4-R1 advances the public NAAIL research infrastructure from the 17 September 2026 integrated baseline toward a more explicit open, reproducible, cross-platform research environment.

This release is intentionally conservative about scientific claims. It records public research infrastructure, catalogues, reproducible demonstrations and publication-ready packages. It does **not** convert registered experiments, design specifications, manuscript packages or external-tool listings into empirical validation.

The previously documented validated executable checkpoint remains:

**NAAIL OpenLab v0.2.3 · Audit Workspace V0.4 · Prototype 003**

unless a later executable package completes its stated validation gates.

## 1. Open accounting, audit and assurance stack

The repository now maintains a provider-neutral public catalogue for reusable accounting, auditing, ICFR/internal-control, corporate-governance, CAM/KAM, IFRS and ESG research components.

Key public entry points include:

- `OPEN_SOURCE_ACCOUNTING_AUDIT_STACK.md`;
- `OPEN_AGENT_CATALOG_2026.md`;
- `Open-Agentic-Accounting-Audit-Lab/`.

The catalogue distinguishes verified open-source licensing from source-available or public-source material rather than treating all public repositories as equivalently open.

## 2. Open Agentic Accounting & Audit Lab v0.3

The integrated Open Agentic Accounting & Audit Lab records **Version 0.3 — 24 September 2026**.

The current public package adds:

- a root-level open-source accounting/audit stack catalogue;
- verified license metadata for core upstream components;
- explicit separation of open-source, source-available and public-source tools;
- links to relevant public research repositories under the Saehon account;
- a specialist-agent catalogue spanning accounting, audit, ICFR, governance, IFRS and ESG research use cases.

This is an open research and education layer, not a representation of equivalence to proprietary professional audit platforms.

## 3. Cross-platform research infrastructure

Public repository materials now define and demonstrate a research architecture connecting:

**ChatGPT × GitHub × Hugging Face × Kaggle × Google Drive**

for reproducible accounting and audit research workflows.

The repository includes publication-ready or public-safe package structures for Kaggle and Hugging Face, with GitHub retained as the canonical code/provenance layer and Google Drive used for controlled research records and working documents where appropriate.

Cross-platform packaging is a reproducibility and dissemination mechanism; it does not imply that every external platform artifact has passed independent scientific validation.

## 4. Public-data provenance demonstration

The repository contains a reproducible Phase-1 Hugging Face public-data demonstration with:

- a small public sample;
- retrieval metadata;
- a SHA-256 provenance record;
- source-verification metadata;
- a documented boundary between the small GitHub sample and the external full dataset.

The purpose is to demonstrate a traceable public-data bridge rather than to mirror unrestricted external datasets into GitHub.

## 5. CAM/KAM research packaging

The repository now contains public-safe CAM/KAM research package structures for GitHub, Kaggle and Hugging Face.

These materials are designed to support:

- reproducible audit-research demonstrations;
- provenance-aware dataset packaging;
- research notebooks and benchmark workflows;
- controlled reuse of public-safe examples;
- separation between public research artifacts and restricted/proprietary source data.

## 6. Research-paper and benchmark integration

The open accounting/audit stack is linked to a public conceptual-paper and benchmark package on agentic financial assurance and cybernetic control.

The package connects:

- conceptual research documentation;
- a reproducible assurance benchmark;
- an open-agent/tool catalogue;
- Kaggle-ready materials;
- Hugging Face-ready materials;
- evidence and validation files.

Publication-ready packaging is not represented as journal acceptance, causal validation, or independent replication.

## 7. Governance and human accountability

V2026.4-R1 retains the NAAIL evidence-governance principles already established in the public repository:

**Evidence / Data → Controlled Tools → Specialist Agents → Independent Review → Human Approval**

Human approval remains the terminal authority for consequential research/professional judgments in the public NAAIL design.

## 9. Public release boundary

Public materials may include:

- high-level architecture;
- rights-cleared or public-source examples;
- reproducible code and small samples;
- provenance manifests;
- benchmark protocols;
- catalogues of external tools and frameworks;
- public-safe educational/research packages.

Restricted, licensed, confidential, unpublished, patent-sensitive or personally identifiable material must remain outside the public release unless explicitly cleared.
