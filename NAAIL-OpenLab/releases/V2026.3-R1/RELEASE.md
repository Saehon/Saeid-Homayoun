## Release purpose

V2026.3-R1 freezes the first coherent public research release of the current NAAIL architecture and integrates the strongest already-executed public proof-of-concept evidence with the new author-finalized forensic benchmark programme.

This release does **not** relabel incomplete scientific experiments as completed. Instead, it distinguishes three evidence states inside one release:

1. executed bounded validation evidence;
2. author-finalized benchmark evidence;
3. registered next-stage empirical tests.

## 8. Public release boundary

R1 may be cited as a released research platform/package. It must not be cited as proof that every registered experiment has been scientifically validated.

Public materials may include high-level architecture, rights-cleared evidence, bounded reproducibility artifacts, protocols, executed test records and non-sensitive benchmark definitions.

Private/protected materials include instructor solutions, Gold Vault contents, hidden labels, private regulatory crosswalks, unpublished patent-sensitive algorithms, dependency/revocation mechanics and private provenance contracts.

## 9. Patent boundary
