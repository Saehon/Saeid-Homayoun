## 1. Permanent architecture

NAAIL has exactly two permanent cores:

1. **Stable Knowledge Core™** — governed scientific/professional meaning, theory, standards, evidence semantics, causal structures, construct definitions and human/professional judgment. Stable management-accounting knowledge includes BSC/Strategy Maps, ABC, TDABC, budgeting, variance analysis, responsibility accounting, cost-driver theory, profitability, capacity and performance measurement.
2. **Replaceable Technology Core™** — models, agents, RAG/GraphRAG/KAG implementations, databases, simulation engines, APIs, connectors, programming environments, MCP/A2A, benchmark/evaluation systems, AI FinOps, observability, telemetry and visualization tools.

**No third permanent core is permitted.** New capabilities extend NAAIL through modular layers, specialist agents/programmes or replaceable technology.

## 3. Management Accounting & AI Cost Intelligence

### [Management Accounting & AI Cost Intelligence Layer™](./MANAGEMENT_ACCOUNTING_AI_COST_INTELLIGENCE_LAYER.md)

The layer applies established management-accounting knowledge to traditional and AI-enabled operations. It covers Balanced Scorecard/Strategy Maps, ABC, TDABC, budgeting, variance analysis, responsibility accounting, cost pools/drivers, customer/product/service profitability, capacity/unused capacity, strategic management accounting, performance measurement and value creation.

NAAIL-developed experimental extensions include **AI Activity-Based Costing™**, **Token- and Time-Driven AI Costing™ (TTD-AIC)** and **Cost-to-Value Decision Engine™** research. These are not represented as original Kaplan/Kaplan–Norton/Kaplan–Anderson frameworks.

Public scientific objective:

**Resources → Activities → Time → Tokens → Agents → Decisions → Quality → Evidence → Cost → Value**

Operational objective:

**Minimum defensible AI cost subject to required quality, evidence, reliability, reproducibility and professional-judgment thresholds.**

### [Open Model Benchmark & Cost Intelligence Layer™](./OPEN_MODEL_BENCHMARK_COST_INTELLIGENCE_LAYER.md)

Supports evidence-governed model/workflow comparison using replaceable benchmark, price, latency, throughput, reliability, context and task-fit information. Candidate resources may include HELM, LiveBench, Hugging Face benchmark resources, public Arena-style preference data, LiteLLM pricing metadata, MLPerf, OpenCost, OpenTelemetry GenAI conventions and open FinOps/observability tooling, subject to current license/provenance/quality verification.

**Benchmark rank ≠ truth. Cheaper model ≠ automatically preferred. Higher benchmark score ≠ automatically preferred.**

### [Visualization & Decision Intelligence Layer™](./VISUALIZATION_DECISION_INTELLIGENCE_LAYER.md)

Supports BSC, ABC, TDABC, AI Cost/FinOps and scientific dashboards using replaceable visualization technology. Visualization supports decision-making but does not replace causal identification, Evidence Passport™ verification or Human Gate™ approval.

## 5. Stable meaning vs replaceable implementation

NAAIL separates scientific meaning from implementation choice.

Examples:

- BSC / ABC / TDABC / strategic management accounting → Stable Knowledge Core™;
- innovation theory, Kahneman-style behavioral theory, contract theory and causal inference → Stable Knowledge Core™;
- SEC/FRED/patent/startup/accounting transaction evidence → Data & Evidence Mesh™;
- HELM/LiveBench/LiteLLM/MLPerf/OpenCost/FinOps/observability/dashboard systems → Replaceable Technology Core™ or cross-cutting support;
- vector DBs, graph engines, embeddings, RAG/GraphRAG/KAG frameworks → Replaceable Technology Core™;
- Management Accounting & AI Cost Intelligence → cross-cutting layer;
- Open Model Benchmark & Cost Intelligence → cross-cutting layer;
- Visualization & Decision Intelligence → cross-cutting layer;
- Innovation & Entrepreneurship and Behavioral Science → cross-cutting layers;
- CCCMP™ → specialist programme;
- Business School Digital Twin → cross-cutting simulation layer.

## 6. Architecture principle

**Stable Theory / Knowledge → Evidence / Data → Replaceable Technology → Agents / Simulations → Decision → Verification → Evidence Passport™ → Human Approval Gate™**

## 7. Governance standard

Every layer/programme must use, where applicable:

- provenance;
- license controls;
- Evidence Passport™;
- causal/decision DAGs;
- versioning;
- reproducibility;
- replication;
- falsification;
- red-team/adversarial review;
- Human Approval Gate™.

For model-cost evidence, current price/benchmark observations require timestamped provenance. Public access does not imply unrestricted redistribution. Cost minimization may not override required evidence, quality or governance.

## 8. Scientific research standard

Publication-grade studies should, where applicable, document:

**Theory → Literature Evidence → Causal DAG → Hypotheses → Power Analysis → Pre-registration → Data → Variable DNA™ → Randomization/Identification → AI Treatment / Cost Measures → Main Test → Robustness → Falsification → Replication/OOS → FT50/AJG Benchmark → Evidence Passport™ → Human Gate™**

Current FT50/AJG status must be verified when used rather than hard-coded from historical lists.
