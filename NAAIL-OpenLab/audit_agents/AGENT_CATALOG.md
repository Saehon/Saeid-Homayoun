# Audit Digital Twin Agent Catalog

The V0.1 simulator represents 37 roles: 36 operational audit-agent roles plus one Audit Scientific Supervisor. These are NAAIL-defined functional roles for education and research. They must not be described as exact replicas of any real audit firm's proprietary agents.

Families: orchestration; engagement; risk; controls; data & analytics; substantive audit; financial statements; knowledge & judgment; documentation & quality; scientific supervision.


## FRANKENSTEIN Finance & Operations Audit System

**Status:** IMPLEMENTED research prototype; validation requires the programme's evaluation gates and NAAIL Human Approval Gate.

FRANKENSTEIN is a specialist programme within the Audit Agent Lab for finance controls, forensic analytics, treasury, revenue, procurement/AP, payroll, tax, FP&A, ICFR, AI/data governance and operations risk. It combines deterministic evidence tests, independent challenge, provenance and human-gated synthesis.
