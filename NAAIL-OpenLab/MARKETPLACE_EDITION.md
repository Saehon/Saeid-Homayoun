# NAAIL™ Marketplace Edition
## Evidence-Governed AI for Business

**Powered by NAAIL OpenLab™**

> **Turn professional questions into traceable evidence, reproducible analysis, and human-reviewed decisions.**

NAAIL Marketplace Edition is the public product-distribution layer for NAAIL OpenLab™, designed for future delivery through major AI and enterprise ecosystems while preserving the research core, governance requirements, and private IP boundary.

## Marketplace product families

| Product | Primary users | Core outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **NAAIL Research Co-Scientist™** | Researchers, universities, doctoral students | Research question → hypotheses → DAG → empirical design → code → robustness → replication → Human Gate |
| **KIWI™ Audit Intelligence** | Audit education, assurance research, professional training | Audit risk / CAM / KAM → evidence → critique → professional judgment → Evidence Passport |
| **ECONOVA-S™ Finance & Data-Economy Intelligence** | Finance, economics, valuation and capital-markets research | Financial/economic question → public data → models → scenarios → reproducible evidence |

## Why NAAIL

```text
Evidence first
     ↓
Specialist multi-agent analysis
     ↓
Independent critique
     ↓
Robustness / falsification / replication
     ↓
Chain-of-Evidence + Evidence Passport™
     ↓
Human Gate™
```

NAAIL is designed so that **model confidence, statistical significance, agent consensus, or predictive accuracy alone cannot establish a scientific or professional conclusion.**

## Planned distribution surfaces

- **OpenAI / ChatGPT** — planned Research Co-Scientist / professional workflow app;

- **Microsoft Marketplace / Copilot** — planned professional, university and enterprise business-intelligence agent;
