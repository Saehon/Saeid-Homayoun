# NAAIL Visualization & Decision Intelligence Layer™

> **PATENT RIGHTS RESERVED — PATENT APPLICATION PREPARATION IN PROGRESS**

**Status:** `PATENT_HOLD_NON_ENABLING`  
**Constitutional placement:** cross-cutting supporting layer under the frozen two-core NAAIL architecture.

This layer provides replaceable visualization and decision-support surfaces for BSC, ABC, TDABC, AI-cost/FinOps analysis, scientific experiments, Digital Twins and specialist agents. It is not a permanent core.

Candidate replaceable technologies may include Apache Superset, Plotly Dash, Apache ECharts, Grafana and suitable open graph/network visualization tooling, subject to current license, maintenance, security and quality review.

Public dashboard families include:

- **Balanced Scorecard dashboard** — four perspectives, strategy map, KPI status, targets/actuals/variance and causal links;
- **ABC dashboard** — resources, activities, cost drivers, cost objects and profitability;
- **TDABC dashboard** — practical capacity, unused capacity, time equations and bottlenecks;
- **AI Cost / FinOps dashboard** — model/provider/workflow/agent usage, cost, latency, quality, evidence and savings;
- **Scientific dashboard** — hypotheses, FT50/AJG evidence, experiments, replication, falsification and Evidence Passport™ status.

Visualization is explanatory and decision-supporting. It does not replace causal identification, professional judgment, Evidence Passport™ verification or Human Gate™ approval.

Detailed dashboard schemas, routing, aggregation logic and internal telemetry contracts remain private pending filing review.
