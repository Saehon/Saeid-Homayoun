## Purpose

NAAIL OpenLab™ is an evidence-governed, provider-neutral multi-agent research and professional-intelligence platform for accounting, auditing, finance, economics, sustainability, forensic analytics, internal controls, education, and reproducible scientific discovery.

The platform combines autonomous scientific reasoning, empirical research agents, professional digital twins, reproducible analytics, adversarial AI-to-AI review, causal DAG governance, Chain-of-Evidence, and mandatory human approval.

## Co-Scientist hypothesis layer

NAAIL uses a Google AI Co-Scientist-inspired scientific workflow to generate and improve multiple competing explanations before empirical testing.

Core functions include:

- hypothesis generation;
- theoretical critique;
- mechanism identification;
- novelty assessment;
- competing-explanation generation;
- ranking and refinement;
- red-team criticism;
- falsification design.

Weak hypotheses should be rejected or revised before expensive empirical execution.

## AlphaEvolve-style research evolution

NAAIL uses AlphaEvolve-inspired evolutionary search to compare and improve:

- algorithms;
- econometric models;
- measures and constructs;
- variable definitions;
- prompts and agent configurations;
- identification strategies;
- forecasting models;
- robustness procedures.

Candidate solutions compete against frozen scientific fitness criteria. NAAIL must never optimize exclusively for p-values or statistical significance.

Recommended fitness dimensions include predictive validity, out-of-sample performance, economic significance, construct validity, causal credibility, evidence quality, reproducibility, robustness, calibration, parsimony, interpretability, and professional relevance.

## AlphaFold / DeepMind-inspired latent-structure discovery

NAAIL uses AlphaFold-inspired latent-structure reasoning as a scientific design metaphor for discovering hidden structures in complex business data.

Target structures may include:

- economic factors;
- accounting structures;
- audit-risk relationships;
- reporting patterns;
- causal mechanisms;
- textual representations;
- control weaknesses;
- CAM/KAM structures;
- sustainability relationships;
- network dependencies.

Every latent structure must pass economic/professional interpretation, external validation, robustness analysis, replication, and human review.

## DAG Governance™

Causal DAGs discipline the transition from theory to empirical design. They identify treatment/exposure, outcome, confounders, mediators, colliders, moderators, temporal ordering, alternative mechanisms, and identification threats.

The empirical system must not silently change the causal story merely because another specification produces a stronger result.

## AI-to-AI scientific review

Before Human Gate approval, an independent AI configuration may act as:

- reviewer;
- critic;
- replication agent;
- methodology auditor;
- evidence auditor;
- statistical reviewer;
- domain specialist;
- falsifier.

The reviewing system should actively attempt to show that the original conclusion is wrong. Disagreement and null results are retained rather than hidden.

## Cross-provider scientific benchmark

The same professional problems should be evaluated across competing AI systems under frozen tasks and equivalent evidence access.

Initial benchmark target:

- 10 IFRS cases;
- 10 PCAOB / audit cases;
- 10 CAM/KAM cases;
- 10 ICFR cases;
- 10 ESG cases;
- 10 Finance / ECONOVA-S™ cases.

Core measures include evidence accuracy, citation accuracy, hallucination rate, tool-selection accuracy, professional-rule compliance, reproducibility, human-review acceptance, latency, compute/token usage where observable, monetary cost where applicable, and cross-agent agreement/disagreement.

KIWI™ cases additionally retain RPA, AA, EG, PS, DS, and DIST.

## Scientific philosophy

NAAIL OpenLab is not designed to replace scholars, auditors, accountants, analysts, instructors, or professional judgment.

Its scientific operating model is:

> **AI proposes.**  
> **Co-Scientists compete.**  
> **ERA operationalizes.**  
> **AlphaEvolve improves.**  
> **Computational Discovery searches.**  
> **Latent-Structure Agents reveal.**  
> **DAGs discipline causality.**  
> **Code tests.**  
> **AI reviewers challenge.**  
> **Replication verifies.**  
> **Evidence decides.**  
> **Humans approve.**

## Final vision

NAAIL OpenLab™ is a multi-agent scientific laboratory and professional digital-twin platform designed to move accounting, auditing, finance, economics, and sustainability research from AI-assisted writing toward evidence-governed computational discovery.

Its long-term objective is not merely to create more agents. It is to create an environment where humans and competing AI scientists can generate, challenge, empirically test, reproduce, falsify, and verify professional and scientific knowledge through a transparent Chain-of-Evidence before any discovery claim is accepted.
