**Suggested title:** AI Investment, Global Factor Data and Reproducible Factor Research — Proposed Extension

Dear JKP research team,

I am Dr. Saeid Homayoun, a researcher in accounting, auditing, finance and AI at the University of Gävle, Sweden.

I am interested in contributing reproducible Python-based research to the JKP ecosystem following Jensen, Kelly and Pedersen (2023), *Is There a Replication Crisis in Finance?*

I propose three connected but independently testable directions:

1. Examine whether disclosure-based, time-stamped corporate AI investment characteristics explain or predict returns beyond documented global factor benchmarks.
2. Evaluate machine-learning challengers with strictly chronological out-of-sample tests, prespecified baselines, transparent multiple-testing control, and full reporting of null results.
3. Contribute public code for dataset provenance, validation and reproducible comparison, without redistributing licensed WRDS/vendor data.

I have developed a public, source-aware validation scaffold and a scientific protocol in my [GitHub research project](https://github.com/Saehon/Saeid-Homayoun/tree/main/research-integrations/jkp-global-factors/). It has only synthetic tests so far and contains no claimed empirical results.

I would welcome your advice on which feature requests, methodological validations, or replication issues could be most useful to the upstream repository and whether a focused pull request would be appropriate.
