# NAAIL Audit & Accounting Digital Twin™ — Public Patent-Hold Overview

> **PATENT RIGHTS RESERVED — PATENT APPLICATION PREPARATION IN PROGRESS**

**Parent architecture:** NAAIL OpenLab™ — V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin  
**Status:** research/education prototype family; public description intentionally non-enabling

## Public objective

The NAAIL Audit & Accounting Digital Twin™ is a research and education prototype family for studying evidence-based accounting and audit judgment in human–AI workflows.

At a high level, prototype scenarios may combine:

- synthetic or rights-cleared company evidence;
- public SEC/XBRL and macro/market context where appropriate;
- IFRS-related accounting issues;
- internal-control and audit-risk scenarios;
- CAM/KAM judgment;
- public PCAOB-oriented audit/regulatory context;
- specialist NAAIL agents including KIWI™, POMELO™, VERA™, IFRS Agent™, PCAOB Agent™, ESG Agent, and ECONOVA-S™;
- professional judgment by students, researchers, or practitioners;
- simulation of consequences and verification; and
- accountable human review.

The public research objective can be summarized as:

**Evidence → Human/AI Analysis → Professional Simulation → Judgment → Verification → Human Approval.**

## Public-data principle

Where public evidence is used, NAAIL preserves source attribution, applicable rights, period/version context, and research limitations. Large third-party datasets and credentials are not committed to the public repository by default.

## Research integrity

The Digital Twin is not presented as a production audit system, a replacement for professional judgment, or a representation of any Big Four proprietary platform. Synthetic outcomes are not automatically treated as real-world causal effects.

The existing validated public executable checkpoint remains Prototype 003 for its stated frozen synthetic scope. This dynamic Audit & Accounting Digital Twin family should not be described as provider-backed or live-data validated unless separate execution evidence supports that claim.

## Patent hold

Detailed scenario state models, Evidence Passport structures, decision-dependency logic, agent-routing rules, evaluation conditions, state-transition rules, consequence-ledger mechanics, verification algorithms, professional-judgment scoring structures, technical flowcharts, and unpublished implementation details are withheld from the current public branch pending patent filing review.

Earlier implementation-level versions of this file and related artifacts were publicly committed before adoption of the patent-first policy. Current sanitization does not erase historical Git disclosures. See [`../../PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE_LOG.md`](../../PUBLIC_DISCLOSURE_LOG.md).
