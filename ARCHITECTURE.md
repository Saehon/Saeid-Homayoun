# ECONOVA-S™ Canonical Architecture

**Architecture specification:** V2.5  
**Permanent cores:** exactly two  
**Software status:** v0.2.0 citable release; v0.3 active development

## Purpose

ECONOVA-S™ is a governed scientific economic intelligence architecture for studying when data and AI become economically and socially valuable. It separates stable scientific meaning from replaceable AI and software infrastructure.

> **Stable Economic Meaning + Living Evidence + Replaceable Technology + Independent Scientific Verification**

## Exactly two permanent cores

### 1. Stable Economic Knowledge Core™

Preserves the scientific objects that must not drift when models or software change:

- economics, finance, data economy, behavioral economics and welfare;
- FT50 and AJG/ABS 4*/4 relevance-gated evidence;
- causal DAGs and theory mechanisms;
- Variable DNA™ and construct definitions;
- econometric identification rules;
- replication and falsification standards;
- behavioral and experimental protocols;
- finance and asset-pricing logic;
- sustainability and climate-finance mechanisms;
- private, sustainable and social value functions;
- discovery-acceptance criteria and human scientific responsibility.

Principle: **Stable Schema + Living Evidence**.

### 2. Replaceable Technology Core™

Contains technology that may be upgraded, retired or routed without silently changing scientific meaning:

- current and future LLMs;
- model and agent routers;
- Python, R, Stata and EViews adapters;
- retrieval systems and vector/graph databases;
- MCP/A2A and tool connectors;
- simulators and code execution;
- observability, permissions and error recovery;
- technology evaluation and retirement logic.

Principle: **Stable Economic Intelligence + Replaceable Technology**.

## Supporting fabric — not a third core

The **AI-to-AI Scientific Intelligence Fabric™** connects the two cores through a structured scientific contract. A typical message carries:

`TaskID | Claim/Hypothesis | Evidence | Method | Assumptions | Confidence | Contradictions | FailureStatus | Provenance | RequiredNextAction`

The fabric preserves disagreement rather than forcing consensus.

## Discovery acceptance rule

A result is not a scientific discovery merely because it is novel, statistically significant, predictively accurate or endorsed by an LLM.

A discovery candidate must survive, where relevant:

- literature validation;
- construct and measurement validation;
- data-quality and provenance checks;
- identification classification;
- replication;
- adversarial review;
- robustness and falsification;
- chronology-safe temporal/OOS validation;
- reproducibility;
- economic significance;
- welfare interpretation; and
- explicit human scientific approval.
