# ECONOVA-S™ Canonical Architecture

**Architecture specification:** V2.5  
**Permanent cores:** exactly two  
**Software status:** v0.2.0 citable release; v0.3 active development

## Purpose

ECONOVA-S™ is a governed scientific economic intelligence architecture for studying when data and AI become economically and socially valuable. It separates stable scientific meaning from replaceable AI and software infrastructure while making hypothesis generation, empirical execution, scientific search, verification, and human approval auditable.

> **Stable Economic Meaning + Living Evidence + Replaceable Technology + Independent Scientific Verification**

## Exactly two permanent cores

### 1. Stable Economic Knowledge Core™

Preserves scientific objects that must not drift when models or software change:

- economics, finance, data economy, behavioral economics and welfare;
- FT50 and AJG/ABS 4*/4 relevance-gated evidence;
- causal DAGs and theory mechanisms;
- Variable DNA™ and construct definitions;
- econometric identification rules;
- replication and falsification standards;
- behavioral and experimental protocols;
- finance and asset-pricing logic;
- sustainability and climate-finance mechanisms;
- private, sustainable and social value functions;
- Chain-of-Evidence requirements;
- discovery-acceptance criteria and human scientific responsibility.

Principle: **Stable Schema + Living Evidence**.

### 2. Replaceable Technology Core™

Contains technology that may be upgraded, retired or routed without silently changing scientific meaning:

- current and future LLMs;
- model and agent routers;
- Python, R, Stata and EViews adapters;
- retrieval systems and vector/graph databases;
- MCP/A2A and tool connectors;
- simulators and code execution;
- automated evaluators;
- observability, permissions and error recovery;
- technology evaluation and retirement logic.

Principle: **Stable Economic Intelligence + Replaceable Technology**.

## Supporting AI-to-AI fabric — not a third core

The **AI-to-AI Scientific Intelligence Fabric™** connects the two cores through a machine-readable scientific contract. A governed handoff carries:

`TaskID | RunID | Sender | Receiver | Claim | Evidence | Method | Assumptions | Confidence | Contradictions | FailureStatus | Provenance | ParentHash | IndependenceClass | RiskFlags | RequiredNextAction | ContentSHA256`

The fabric preserves disagreement and failed experiments rather than forcing consensus.

## Methodological mapping

### AI Co-Scientist-style hypothesis search

ECONOVA-S™ mirrors the logic of a multi-agent hypothesis tournament through explicit functions for **Generation, Reflection, Ranking, Evolution, Proximity, and Meta-review**. Hypothesis ranking is a search heuristic, not ground truth.

### ERA-style empirical execution

Every surviving hypothesis should be translated into executable empirical work: data, Variable DNA™, code, estimand, evaluation metrics, robustness tests, falsification tests, and replication/OOS logic.

### AlphaEvolve-inspired search

Algorithms, measures, estimators, models, prompts, and specifications may be evolved against a **frozen scientific fitness function**. The system must not optimize p-values alone. Discovery and validation systems should be separated where feasible.

### Computational Discovery

Candidate models and programs may be explored and evaluated in parallel using automated evaluators, immutable raw scores, explicit lineage, and exploration/exploitation controls.

### AlphaFold-inspired latent-structure reasoning

AlphaFold is a biological system and is **not** treated as an economics model. ECONOVA-S™ borrows the general scientific idea of inferring hidden structure and requiring rigorous benchmarking. Candidate latent factors, regimes, networks, risk dimensions, or hidden mechanisms require interpretation, simpler baselines, stability tests, and external/OOS validation.

### Science One Chain-of-Evidence

Every material claim should link to a recorded evidence chain. The two core tests are:

1. **Completeness** — every important claim has evidence.
2. **Correctness** — the evidence genuinely supports the claim.

### CoE Audit

Publication-grade work should audit:

- reference validity;
- score/result reproducibility;
- specification integrity;
- method-code alignment;
- claim-evidence alignment.

### Mirendil-inspired closed-loop R&D

Mirendil is **not a Google product**. ECONOVA-S™ uses Mirendil-inspired language only for the general closed-loop R&D principle:

`Propose → Build → Execute → Evaluate → Diagnose → Revise → Re-run → Compare → Preserve Improvement`

Self-improvement may improve execution but may not rewrite the scientific constitution: evidence classes, Human Gate, provenance, falsification, licensing, security, or discovery-acceptance rules.

## Discovery acceptance rule

A result is not a scientific discovery merely because it is novel, statistically significant, predictively accurate, highly ranked by agents, or endorsed by an LLM.

A discovery candidate must survive, where relevant:

- verified literature grounding;
- hypothesis-tournament documentation;
- DAG/system governance;
- construct and measurement validation;
- real-data provenance and chronology checks;
- identification classification;
- governed search integrity;
- latent-structure validation when used;
- replication/OOS validation;
- adversarial review;
- robustness and falsification;
- Chain-of-Evidence completeness/correctness;
- CoE Audit;
- reproducibility;
- economic significance;
- welfare interpretation; and
- explicit human scientific approval.

The repository machine-enforces this boundary with [`discovery/study_manifest.schema.json`](discovery/study_manifest.schema.json) and [`discovery/validate_study_manifest.py`](discovery/validate_study_manifest.py).
