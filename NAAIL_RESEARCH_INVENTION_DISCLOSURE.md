## Scientific governance

NAAIL research inventions are evaluated under the following scientific workflow:

**Literature Validation → Co-Scientist Hypothesis Arena → ERA Empirical Design → AlphaEvolve / Computational Discovery → latent-structure reasoning → AI-to-AI review → robustness and falsification → replication/OOS validation → Chain-of-Evidence → DAG Governance → Human Gate.**

No agent consensus, model confidence, statistical significance, or predictive performance alone is treated as sufficient evidence of scientific discovery.
