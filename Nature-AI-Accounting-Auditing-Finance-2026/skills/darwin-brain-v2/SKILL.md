# DARWIN BRAIN V2 — RESEARCH SKILL

## Trigger
Invoke for multi-project research design, Nature-to-FT50 transfer, reproducible empirical studies, project phase planning, or CEO-perspective strategic critique. Do not trigger for ordinary factual questions.

## Non-negotiable principles
1. Treat the "100 CEOs" as **100 explicitly simulated strategic perspectives**, not actual CEOs, their endorsements, interviews, or proprietary knowledge. Never fabricate identities, votes or consensus.
2. System thinking: map stocks, flows, feedback loops, delays, constraints, second-order effects, incentives and failure modes.
3. Separate observed evidence, verified citations, assumptions, synthetic data, forecasts, and conjecture. A simulation is not empirical validation.
4. Google Drive is the canonical archive, GitHub is versioned code; do not delete or overwrite original files. Log project ID, sources, data versions, run IDs and links.
5. Follow non-bypassable gates: provenance; rights/license; COSO/ICFR grounding when relevant; evidence sufficiency; independent review; falsification; reproducibility; human approval.
6. Never auto-approve high-stakes accounting or audit conclusions. Block when data rights or provenance are unknown.
7. No claim of FT50/TAR readiness without verified citations, real empirical results, defensible identification, sensitivity analyses and submission checklist.

## Pipeline (execute one gated phase at a time)
P0. Scope: define target journal, construct, sample, dependent variable, economic mechanism and exclusion criteria.
P1. Evidence inventory: inspect all 35 Nature-related candidate references; verify DOI, year, venue, actual method, code, data, licenses and replication constraints. Mark UNVERIFIED until checked. Map each to specific project; never invent bibliographic facts.
P2. Systems map: identify stakeholders (management, auditor, audit committee, investor, regulator), incentive loops, information asymmetry, delayed effects and alternative mechanisms.
P3. Hypothesis generator: create 3 rival explanations and 3 falsifiable hypotheses; ask independent critic to find prior art, endogeneity, leakage and non-identification.
P4. 100-perspective strategic ensemble: 10 roles x 10 scenarios, not 100 real CEOs. Roles = CFO, CEO, audit partner, audit committee chair, regulator, investor, controller, data scientist, cybersecurity officer, ESG assurance lead. Scenarios = expansion, recession, restatement, fraud alert, control weakness, model drift, litigation, privacy constraint, liquidity shock, AI capital spending. Score evidence quality, strategic value, feasibility, ethics and downside risk on a 0–5 anchored rubric. Report disagreement and minority objections; no invented interview data.
P5. Data acquisition: prioritize lawful public SEC EDGAR/XBRL, PCAOB public reports, Fama-French factors, open KAM/CAM, sustainability reports and verified repository data. Log unit of observation, time, missingness, sample attrition and license.
P6. Design: prespecify DAG, estimand, selection mechanism, controls, FE, clustering, sample splits and power. Avoid post-treatment controls, look-ahead bias and p-hacking.
P7. Implement: Python/R/Stata replication code with environment lockfile, data dictionary, immutable inputs, synthetic smoke tests and deterministic seeds. Synthetic smoke tests never stand in for real effects.
P8. Validate: independent replication, negative controls, placebo, robustness, multiple testing, calibration, sensitivity to model and prompt, subgroup fairness, out-of-sample and leakage audits.
P9. Publication: TAR/JAR/JAE/JFE/Management Science fit, contribution vs strongest comparators, journal-specific formatting, verified references, limitations, data/code availability and human author sign-off.
P10. Archive: write PROJECT_MASTER_INDEX with phase status, source citations, file hashes, GitHub PR, Drive links, Kaggle/HF links only when verified. Maintain open repair queue. After 2 failed retries on noncritical task, log blocker and advance to independent permissible task; never bypass critical science gates.

## Project routing
- ICFR/SEFD/TimesFM: target next-period material weaknesses; PR-AUC, Brier, calibration, Recall@K, time splits; never confuse predictive performance with causal inference.
- Risk–CAM alignment: ex ante account risk independent of CAM text; model CAM selection and next-period outcome, industry/account FE; guard against account/topic leakage.
- PCAOB AI-to-AI inspection: evidence lineage, adversarial inspection, human regulatory sign-off; public inspection findings only.
- AI CapEx/asset pricing: measure investment disclosures, future cash flows and risk-adjusted returns using public factors; carefully distinguish spending from valuation.
- ESG assurance: trace claims to source documents, evaluate contradictions and greenwashing with independently coded labels.
- Paper2Agent/FT50 replication: execute original licensed code/data in sandbox; compare table cells, preserve environment and differences.
- Digital Twin / education: simulation is pedagogical or hypothesis-generating until externally validated.
- IFRS startup / LEMON / POMELO: value creation versus value protection, licensing, governance and customer-zero evidence.

## GPT/Claude operating contract
Use the same skill content as context in either model. Never assume persistent execution, access to other model sessions or installed integration. Before each tool action inspect available permissions. The system cannot claim cross-model agreement without running independent model outputs and saving their transcripts.
