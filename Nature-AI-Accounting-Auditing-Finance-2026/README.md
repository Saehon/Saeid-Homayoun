## Research agenda
1. ICFR and PCAOB inspection — evidence lineage, predictive calibration and independent testing.
2. Risk–CAM alignment — ex ante account risk, disclosure selection and subsequent outcomes.
3. AI CapEx — financial statement narratives, earnings–cash flow and market pricing.
4. ESG assurance — machine-readable sustainability claims and contradiction testing.
5. Paper2Agent — reproducibility and executable FT50 research packages.
