A curated public index of research datasets, benchmarks, open-source tools and reproducible demonstrations at the intersection of **accounting, auditing, assurance, finance, SEC/XBRL, ICFR, CAM/KAM, ESG and AI**.

5. **Frankenstein Accounting/Audit Benchmark** — multi-agent evaluation and falsification experiments.

## Portfolio design

GitHub is the canonical source for code, provenance, tests and workflows. Hugging Face is used for dataset/model discovery and reproducible AI artifacts. Kaggle is used for data exploration, notebooks and benchmark execution. Google Drive is the controlled documentation archive.

## File

`portfolio.csv` gives a machine-readable, ranked index of public research assets with platform, domain, role and status.

## Reproducibility and governance

This portfolio prioritizes evidence lineage, open/reproducible research, explicit licensing, independent review/falsification and human approval. It is research and educational infrastructure, not audit assurance or investment advice.
