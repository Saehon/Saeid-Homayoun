## Evidence taxonomy and attribution

- A repository copied or forked from another owner (e.g. `financebench`, `inspect_ai`, `timesfm`) is a **third-party asset**, not proof of personally written source code.
- A public synthetic CI demonstration is **engineering scaffold evidence**. It does not prove actual evaluated model accuracy or production readiness.
- A private project may be used to discuss concepts in interviews only after reviewing IP rights; a recruiter without access cannot inspect it. Do not expose confidential POMELO internals or restricted data.
- Current external job text is **an employer requirement**, not a validated statement of the applicant's personal experience. Qualification gaps marked *unknown* must be documented by a CV, independent references, executed outputs or signed work records.
- Postings are **not official offers** and their presence in this file is not a claim of company association.

## Three practical development tracks

**Finance evaluation:** Gold-50/Gold-150 question bank with authorized SEC/company filings, realistic GAAP/IFRS, audit, tax, CapEx and ratio ambiguity. Human-verified reference answers, provenance hashes, leakage checks, and genuinely executed, costed comparison of multiple providers. Follow FinanceBench CC BY-NC 4.0 license when applicable.
