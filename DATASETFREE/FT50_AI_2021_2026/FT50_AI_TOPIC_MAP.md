## Theme A — Financial reporting, ICFR, disclosure and governance
JAR 2026: Blankespoor/deHaan/Li, Choi/Xie, intermediary disclosures, trading alignment, numerical/look-ahead bias. RAST 2025 Ashraf: automation and material weaknesses. CAR 2024 auditors' AI reliance. **Strong source:** Chicago Booth official [Volume 64](https://www.chicagobooth.edu/research/chookaszian/journal-of-accounting-research/online-supplements-and-datasheets/volume-64). Code available for academic use, not necessarily all original firm/user data.

## Theme B — LLM textual analysis and price formation
Management Science: [de Kok](https://github.com/TiesdeKok/chatgpt_paper) and Siano. JFE: [Lopez-Lira/Tang](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/f39x226htv/1). RFS language-embedding finance sentiment. Strong open entrypoint: de Kok's labelled earnings-call non-answer dataset and code. Separate pre-trained representation measurements from original adjusted stock-return dependence.

## Theme C — AI labour, professional careers and productivity
JFE Babina et al. (2024), JAR Choi/Xie (2026), Management Science Bick/Blandin/Deming 2026, Cui et al. 2026, Demirci et al. 2025, QJE Brynjolfsson et al. 2025. **Proprietary professional or worker microdata limits:** Babina pseudo-data, platform/company employment logs, confidential RCTs. JobHop is pseudonymous and not linkable to public-listed firm executives.

## Theme D — Innovation, strategy and product markets
[JFE Babina replication](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/s26kxvspn7/3) provides code and synthetic substitutes; RAST [Cao et al. LLM Peers](https://github.com/ycao25/LLM_Peers) shares derived peer scores; [SMJ Doshi et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/smj.3677) has downloadable supplement zip. Public USPTO patents/trademarks plus SEC can build an *independently specified* alternative sample.

## Theme E — Agentic AI, delegation and multiagent evaluation
ISR 2026 social AI reply-agent delegation, human-guided versus delegated search, prompt adaptation and agency configurations; MS optimal human/machine/GenAI task allocation; ISR 2025 research governance editorial. **MacAma** is a separately labelled external methodology from a non-FT50 medical journal; it is not counted in the FT50 sample. Evidence-governed AI research design should trace agent plans, data inputs, decisions, code, generated claims and human signoff.

## Theme F — Digital twins and accessible research experiments
[Marketing Science Twin-2K-500](https://github.com/tianyipeng-lab/Digital-Twin-Simulation) is the most direct publicly linked digital-twin code/data framework. [Marketing Science LOLA](https://github.com/DDDOH/LLM_News) includes code and open Upworthy OSF data, a strong practical benchmark for LLM evaluation. Human survey privacy and paid API inference need separate audit.

## Theme G — Transparent ML, testing and financial data quality
JFE 2026 Glass Box ML bond returns and Deep Surrogates for option pricing; JAR 2026 Caution Ahead on look-ahead leakage. Assess chronological model vintages, sample leakage, hyperparameter tuning, selection of test companies and source-rights before claiming performance.

## Candidate DATASETFREE research hypotheses
H1 Cross-source inconsistency between AI claims and independently observed implementation predicts future ICFR weakness or accounting-reporting risk beyond generic disclosure tone.
H2 AI capability with credible audit/operational governance moderates conversion from public AI announcements into observable patent/product outcomes.
H3 Price response to AI claims depends on independent evidence assurance and manager/auditor AI judgment safeguards. This requires lawful firm-price microdata; FF factors alone are insufficient.
**All untested; do not assert never studied or significant.**
