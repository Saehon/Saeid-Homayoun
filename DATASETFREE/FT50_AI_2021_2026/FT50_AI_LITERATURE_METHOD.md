# Five-year FT50 / AJG4 AI, LLM, Agentic AI evidence-map protocol

Date/time boundary: 2021-10-10 to 2026-10-10 inclusive, based on first online scholarly publication when identifiable. AJG ratings refer to AJG **2024**; FT50 inclusion refers to the **2026 updated list**; separate columns prevent false equivalence. Source rankings cross-checked with AJG 2024 tables (https://journalranking.org/) and 2026 FT list (https://www.ft.com/content/db863a0a-6524-45f9-bc52-fee997634bdc).

Search classes: AI, artificial intelligence, ML, machine learning, generative artificial intelligence, generative AI, ChatGPT, large language model, LLM, agentic AI, autonomous agent, multi-agent. Disciplines: accounting, auditing, reporting, finance, economics, management, information systems, marketing, strategy. Sources: publisher article indexes; official Chicago Booth JAR supplements; INFORMS publisher supplements; Mendeley; Harvard Dataverse; source-linked GitHub; high-quality official publisher page as confirmation. Filter by journal, publication date and peer-reviewed article. Exclude SSRN-only working papers from the FT50 evidence set unless an accepted published article is identified.

**This is a targeted, non-exhaustive mapping of verified prominent articles, not a completed PRISMA systematic review.** It does not establish zero AI articles in absent FT50 titles; a genuine census requires per-journal publication API coverage and a deduplicated DOI log.

ACCESS TAXONOMY
1. Published article link verified.
2. Free code: direct GitHub/official academic research archive, or publisher-described supplement (clearly differentiate code found and 'supplement uninspected').
3. Free *original* data: raw input available and licensable vs code-only vs derivative vs pseudo-data vs private/proprietary. Codes requiring CRSP/Compustat, Cognism, newswires, auditor personnel, field-study firm logs or payment are NOT full open replication.
4. Numerical replication: currently **not executed**. An accessible ZIP is not a reproduced result.
5. Personal-data rights and external licences are separately verified before storage.

FUTURE PRISMA WORK
Freeze exact list of FT50-2026 and AJG2024 4/4* journal ISSNs; query Crossref/OpenAlex and publisher article page for each DOI in window; mark keywords in title/abstract AND manually review domain-applications. Two independent screeners. Extract outcome, AI exposure definition, code licence, input accessibility and numerical reproduction. Register independent study metadata and study-specific provenance hashes. Do not pool financial panel estimates unless common estimands/SEs and effect dependence are clear; avoid collapsing text-classification F1 and regression coefficients into a single meta-effect.

IMPORTANT: Agentic AI empirical articles are much sparser in top accounting journals than generic AI/ML; never describe all LLM studies as agentic. The 2026 ISR influencer reply-agent paper explicitly studies AI agent delegation, while the 2025 ISR editorial explicitly discusses agentic research tools and human governance.
