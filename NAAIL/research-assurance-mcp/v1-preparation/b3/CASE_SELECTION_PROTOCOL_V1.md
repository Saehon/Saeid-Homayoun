## Mandatory eligibility

Every candidate must:

1. have a normalized DOI not already present in the canonical registry or candidate set;
2. be a peer-reviewed accounting, auditing, or finance article, with FT50 preferred but not treated as proof;
3. have a public replication package with provenance and license/reuse-status disclosure;
4. support a documented table-to-code map;
5. expose an executable Stata or Python path with entry point, order, environment, and expected output;
6. identify a bounded public-data independent-reproduction path without substituting for licensed-data results; and
7. disclose prior exposure by the operator, Claude, the human owner, and earlier runs.

Failure of any mandatory criterion makes the candidate ineligible or, for unclear reuse rights, places it in human review. Eligibility is not selection.
