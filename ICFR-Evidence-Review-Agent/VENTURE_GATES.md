# Venture Stage Gates

Advance because evidence improves, not because more features exist.

## Gate 0 — Problem proof
- 10+ interviews with ICFR/SOX/internal-audit/control professionals.
- one workflow repeatedly identified as costly;
- baseline time/cost documented;
- buyer and user clearly identified.

Kill signal: pain is low-value, occasional, or already solved adequately.

## Gate 1 — Customer Zero
- synthetic/public benchmark;
- deterministic core;
- Evidence Passport;
- Human Gate;
- seeded-exception tests;
- reproducible CI.

Target: 100% lineage completeness and zero path where an AI provider can approve a control.

## Gate 2 — Design partners
- three design partners;
- same core workflow;
- read-only integrations first;
- success criteria agreed before build.

Do not accept three unrelated custom projects.

## Gate 3 — Paid pilot
- customer pays;
- measurable review-time reduction;
- precision/recall and overrides documented;
- security/data-processing terms;
- support workload measured.

## Gate 4 — Repeatability
- second and third customers deploy mostly through configuration;
- implementation effort falls across pilots;
- recurring patterns exceed bespoke work;
- credible gross-margin path.

## Gate 5 — Enterprise readiness
- Entra SSO/RBAC;
- tenant isolation;
- encryption;
- retention/deletion controls;
- audit logs;
- vulnerability/dependency management;
- incident process;
- privacy/security documentation;
- model/evidence lineage.

## Gate 6 — Marketplace
- production SaaS;
- stable subscription lifecycle;
- support/SLA process;
- legal/privacy assets;
- Microsoft Marketplace technical requirements tested end to end.

## Gate 7 — Expansion
Only after the wedge works:
- more ICFR processes;
- continuous monitoring;
- SharePoint/OneDrive/Teams/Excel;
- SAP/Dynamics/Oracle;
- CAM/KAM/IFRS modules.

## Venture logic

Use capital to accelerate proven bottlenecks, not to discover whether the problem exists.

Sequence:
- founder/research-funded Customer Zero;
- startup cloud/AI credits for prototype;
- design-partner revenue;
- accelerator/venture studio when it materially improves distribution, enterprise access or capital efficiency;
- institutional capital after repeatability evidence.
