## Coding dimensions for the pilot

- AGENTIC: explicit use of AI agents / agentic AI in audit or assurance.
- POPULATION_ANALYTICS: analysis extending beyond conventional sampling/full-population capability.
- EVIDENCE_TRACE: explicit evidence lineage, reasoning trace, documentation or auditable trail.
- RISK_ANALYTICS: AI/analytics used for risk identification, anomaly detection or risk response.
- HUMAN_GATE: explicit human professional judgment, oversight, accountability or review.
- AI_ASSURANCE: assurance/governance over AI systems themselves.

## Important methodological note

A blank or weaker signal in this register means only that the selected official source did not provide equally explicit public evidence for that coding dimension. It must not be interpreted as proof that a firm lacks the capability.

## Reproducibility / rights

This repository stores metadata, short factual summaries and source URLs—not copies of proprietary firm platforms, methodologies or copyrighted reports. Researchers should re-check source dates and URLs before publication.
