# ICFR AI

Purpose: internal control over financial reporting research, control testing and evidence traceability.

IBM-enabled components:
- Docling for policies, narratives, reports and evidence extraction
- Granite for control/risk classification and structured reasoning
- Optional IBM OpenPages Financial Controls Management for SOX/financial-control workflows
- Optional watsonx.governance for governance of AI models/agents used in the ICFR process

Research outputs must retain evidence lineage and human approval.
