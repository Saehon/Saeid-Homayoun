# Third-Party Licensing and Reuse Notes

This project links to external projects; it does **not** automatically incorporate or relicense their source code.

Before installing, distributing, modifying, or bundling a dependency, verify its current upstream license.

Key examples:
- CPA Skills — verify upstream LICENSE: https://github.com/adoptai/cpa-skills
- FinanceSkills — verify upstream LICENSE: https://github.com/GAJETOso/financeskills
- closegate — verify upstream LICENSE: https://github.com/esploro-group/closegate
- Docling MCP — verify upstream LICENSE: https://github.com/docling-project/docling-mcp
- SEC EDGAR MCP — verify upstream LICENSE: https://github.com/stefanoamorelli/sec-edgar-mcp
- LiteLLM — verify upstream LICENSE: https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm
- Google ADK — verify upstream LICENSE: https://github.com/google/adk-docs

- AI4SustainableX — currently source-available under BUSL-1.1 according to its repository; review commercial/research terms before use: https://github.com/lokeshbohra/ai4sustainablex

## Standards/content licensing

IFRS Standards and some professional/regulatory content have separate copyright and licensing terms. Do not publish a public RAG corpus containing copyrighted standards text unless the applicable license permits it. Prefer authoritative links, permitted extracts, taxonomy/public data, and user-licensed content.

## Research reproducibility

Record the exact dependency release/commit used in each experiment. A GitHub link alone is not sufficient to reproduce results.
