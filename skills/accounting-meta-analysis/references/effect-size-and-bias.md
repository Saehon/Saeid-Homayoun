# Effect-size and selective-reporting checklist

Before pooling, confirm:

1. The target estimand and direction are the same or a principled conversion is documented.
2. The reported statistic, standard error, sample size, and source location are preserved.
3. Multiple outcomes/specifications from a study are handled as dependent or selected by protocol.
4. The weighting and random/multilevel/robust-variance choice are stated.
5. `k` studies and `m` effects are both reported.
6. Heterogeneity and a prediction interval are shown when meaningful.
7. Funnel or small-study diagnostics are treated as sensitivity evidence, not proof of bias.
8. Missing or inaccessible results remain missing; no significance is inferred from omission.
