# Scientific discovery and adversarial research design

1. Specify the literature boundary: accounting mechanism, economic theory, empirical gap, policy/institutional relevance, target venue.
2. Build a causal or predictive diagram: variables, chronology, measurement process, confounding, mediators, moderators, selection channels.
3. Propose at least one genuinely competing explanation and falsifiable predictions; distinguish association, prediction, mediation and causal identification.
4. Run a Darwin-inspired cycle: generate alternative designs → adversarial screening → evidence-informed selection → preregister/freeze primary specification when appropriate → empirical testing → revision, with an audit trail.
5. Use system-thinking maps for regulators, auditors, management, firms, investors, technology vendors, incentives and feedback loops.
6. Use structure-prediction and evolutionary-optimization metaphors cautiously: they do not validate accounting theories or substitute for statistics.
7. Challenge construct validity, external validity, publication bias, reverse causality, simultaneity, measurement error, post-treatment conditioning and multiple testing.
8. Provide a hypothesis matrix with H number, claim, mechanism, observable proxy, expected sign, alternative explanation, identification strategy and falsification test.
