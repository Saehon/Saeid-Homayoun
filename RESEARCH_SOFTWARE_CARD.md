## Intended use

ECONOVA-S is research software for governed AI-assisted work in economics, finance, the data economy and sustainable welfare analysis. It is intended to support research design, evidence retrieval, variable construction, econometric analysis, replication, robustness, scientific red-team review and Evidence Passport™ generation.

## Intended users

- academic researchers;
- doctoral and graduate researchers;
- research engineers;
- economists and finance researchers;
- reproducibility and replication teams.

## Current public capabilities

- dual-core scientific architecture;
- GPT-5.6 Sol as a replaceable model backend in supported deployments;
- Co-Scientist-style hypothesis generation and critique;
- ERA-style empirical conversion;
- evidence metadata RAG;
- Fama–French official data adapters;
- Damodaran / NYU Stern industry-data adapters;
- SEC EDGAR XBRL CompanyFacts adapter with filing-date chronology controls;
- generic six-table econometric workbench;
- temporal/OOS checks;
- Stata export;
- scientific red-team review;
- Evidence Passport™ and Human Gate™;
- active FIZ→CIZ real-data measurement-change study.

## Primary inputs

- research question and theory context;
- literature/evidence metadata or licensed/user-provided evidence corpora;
- official or properly licensed datasets;
- empirical-design constraints and pre-analysis decisions.

## Outputs

Depending on the workflow, ECONOVA-S may produce:

- systems maps and causal DAGs;
- ranked competing hypotheses;
- Variable DNA™;
- empirical specifications;
- Tables 1–6;
- robustness/falsification diagnostics;
- OOS/replication reports;
- red-team reports;
- Evidence Passport™;
- human decision record.

## Scientific status

The public system is a **research workbench**, not an autonomous scientific authority. Current public outputs must not be interpreted as scientific discoveries solely because they are novel, significant, predictive or generated by advanced AI.

Default status:

```text
discovery_claim_allowed = false
```

See `SCIENTIFIC_ASSURANCE.md` for the acceptance standard.

## Known limitations

- the bundled evidence registry is a seed metadata corpus, not a complete FT50/AJG/ABS corpus;
- live third-party data availability may change;
- generic fixed-effects regressions do not establish causal identification;
- model outputs may contain reasoning or citation errors unless independently verified;
- industry crosswalks, construct choices and filing chronology require domain review;
- current v0.3 real-data work is still under active validation.

## Reproducibility expectations

Serious research runs should preserve source versions/hashes, data provenance, code versions, environment information, model/tool versions, random seeds where relevant, pre-specified thresholds and human review decisions.

## Data governance

Authoritative sources are preferred over mirrors. GitHub or Kaggle mirrors may support reproducibility, but should not replace official Fama–French, SEC, Damodaran or other primary sources when those are available.
