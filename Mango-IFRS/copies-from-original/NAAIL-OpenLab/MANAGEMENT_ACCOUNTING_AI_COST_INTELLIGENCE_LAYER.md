# NAAIL Management Accounting & AI Cost Intelligence Layer™

> **PATENT RIGHTS RESERVED — PATENT APPLICATION PREPARATION IN PROGRESS**

**Architecture:** NAAIL OpenLab™ — V2026.3 Multi-Agent Digital Twin  
**Constitution:** exactly two permanent cores — **Stable Knowledge Core™ + Replaceable Technology Core™**  
**Status:** `PATENT_HOLD_NON_ENABLING` public research/education architecture

## Constitutional placement

This is a **cross-cutting governed supporting layer**. It does not create a Balanced Scorecard Core, ABC Core, TDABC Core, FinOps Core, model-benchmarking core, visualization core, or any other third permanent core.

### Stable Knowledge Core™

The stable management-accounting meaning includes:

- Balanced Scorecard (BSC) and Strategy Maps;
- Activity-Based Costing (ABC);
- Time-Driven Activity-Based Costing (TDABC);
- budgeting, responsibility accounting and variance analysis;
- cost pools and cost drivers;
- customer, product and service profitability;
- cost-to-serve;
- capacity management and unused capacity;
- strategic management accounting;
- performance measurement and value creation;
- causal constructs and reproducible research design.

BSC, ABC and TDABC are treated as established management-accounting knowledge. NAAIL-developed AI extensions are not represented as original Kaplan/Kaplan–Norton/Kaplan–Anderson frameworks.

### Replaceable Technology Core™

Replaceable implementation targets may include model benchmarking/evaluation, AI FinOps, telemetry/observability, model routers, gateways, ERP/synthetic-data tooling, optimization/simulation software and dashboard technology. Any external tool remains subject to current license, maintenance, security, provenance and execution verification.

## Public capability families

### Balanced Scorecard Engine™

Supports the four familiar perspectives—Financial, Customer, Internal Process, and Learning & Growth—plus strategy maps, objectives, KPIs, targets, actuals, variances, actions and outcomes. NAAIL may connect strategic objectives to governed decision/causal DAGs and Digital Twins.

### Activity-Based Costing Engine™

Supports governed analysis of resources, resource cost pools, activities, cost drivers and cost objects across products, customers, services, workflows and AI agents.

### Time-Driven ABC Engine™

Supports practical-capacity and time-based costing across accounting, audit, ERP, finance, customer service, research, education, professional services and AI-agent workflows.

### NAAIL AI Activity-Based Costing™

A **NAAIL-developed extension inspired by ABC/TDABC** for studying AI-related cost pools and cost drivers such as token usage, retrieval, tools/APIs, compute, storage, orchestration, retries/failures, evaluation/observability and human review. Detailed allocation mechanics remain private pending filing review.

### Token- and Time-Driven AI Costing™ (TTD-AIC)

A NAAIL experimental extension for studying AI cost using both consumption and time/capacity information. Public research outputs may include cost per task, verified result, agent run, simulation, research experiment, replication, student or professional judgment. Detailed costing equations and allocation logic beyond established management-accounting formulas remain private.

### AI Cost-Minimization / Cost-to-Value Decision Engine™

NAAIL studies model routing, caching, retrieval/tool optimization, batching, retry reduction, prompt/context efficiency and alternative model/workflow configurations under a strict rule:

> **Do not minimize cost alone. Choose the lowest defensible cost that satisfies required quality, evidence, reliability, reproducibility and professional-judgment thresholds.**

The public scientific objective is:

**Resources → Activities → Time → Tokens → Agents → Decisions → Quality → Evidence → Cost → Value**

## Research and evidence gate

Management-accounting studies should use dynamically verified current FT50/AJG context when journal status is invoked. Foundational work may be linked to lawful open versions through SSRN metadata, OpenAlex, CORE, institutional repositories or author pages where rights permit.

Publication-grade work should, where applicable, document:

**Theory → FT50/AJG Evidence → Causal DAG → Construct → Rights-cleared/Synthetic Data → AI Cost Data → Experiment → Econometric/Causal Test → Robustness → Replication → Falsification → Evidence Passport™ → Human Gate™**

Research questions may examine AI-assisted cost-driver selection, AI/ML-supported cost-pool design, TDABC time estimation, managerial time-estimation bias, token/compute/time cost drivers, orchestration-cost allocation, AI-cost transparency, unused AI-agent capacity and cost per verified professional judgment.
