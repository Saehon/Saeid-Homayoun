## 2. Recommended formal registrations

### A. Trademark
Consider registering **ECONOVA-S** as a word mark before major commercialization.

Potential routes:
- Swedish registration through PRV for Sweden-focused protection.
- European Union Trade Mark through EUIPO for protection across EU Member States.

Before filing, conduct a professional clearance search for identical and confusingly similar marks and choose the relevant Nice classes based on intended products and services.

### B. Patentability review
Do not assume that the high-level economic/scientific architecture itself is patentable. Software, mathematical methods, and business methods face restrictions in Europe unless the claimed invention has the required technical character and contribution.

Candidate patent review should focus on concrete technical inventions, for example novel technical mechanisms for secure multi-agent orchestration, provenance integrity, chronology enforcement, fault containment, computational efficiency, protected model/tool routing, or other technical effects.

For any candidate invention:
1. prepare a confidential invention disclosure;
2. identify inventors and ownership;
3. search prior art;
4. obtain patentability advice;
5. file before additional public disclosure where appropriate;
6. publish only what has been cleared for release.

### C. Trade secrets
Production algorithms, private benchmarks, commercial calibration logic, security mechanisms, customer configurations, and unpublished technical implementations should be treated as confidential information where secrecy creates value.

## 3. Commercial licensing model

Recommended structure:

- **Academic/non-commercial research:** permitted subject to the repository LICENSE and citation requirements.
- **Commercial evaluation:** only under written evaluation terms where appropriate.
- **Commercial deployment:** separate paid license.
- **Enterprise integration / SaaS / API / consulting:** negotiated commercial agreement.
- **Trademark/brand use:** separate express permission.
- **Patent rights:** separately negotiated where applicable.

## 4. Release governance

Before each public release, confirm:

- authorship and ownership;
- third-party license compatibility;
- no secrets or credentials;
- no confidential or licensed datasets;
- no unfiled patent-sensitive disclosure approved for secrecy;
- correct trademark notation;
- correct citation and ORCID;
- correct non-commercial license;
- reproducibility materials are safe to publish.
